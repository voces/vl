## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **Fixed: a map read of a list of hole functions, narrowed and called through, no longer loses its `| null`.** When a narrowed read's callback pinned the hole functions, the monomorphizer recorded the pinned list type on every binding the receiver reads through, including one whose initializer admits `null`. That binding's cell was then read non-null, and its `!= null` test trapped on a missing key. Each binding now keeps its own `| null`.
