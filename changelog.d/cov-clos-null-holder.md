## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- Nullable bundle holder (lane COV-CLOS): a binding holding a map read of a two-signature bundle (`const h = m["z"]`, `m.get(k)`) holds the bundle's record or `null`. Before, its cell kind was the bare record (`letCellKindGo`), so a missing key trapped in both emitters: a module holder at its null test, a local at its declaration. Now the cell kind is `nulstruct`. A holder read narrowed past `null` recovers the record (`readOwesNonNullRecover`), and the IR holds such a holder as `record | null` until it is narrowed. Claimed code had the trap: 28 functions, 16 of them module starts. The miss-key variant of each of those 16 programs now runs and prints the miss. 10 more functions are claimed and none is lost. Filed D17585, a captured local holder that is refused on master too.
