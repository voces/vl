## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **D15978, D15979 closed: a record, or a list of records, narrowed out of a union alias and handed to a generic pins at its own type.** `monoNarrowedPinName` skipped a narrowed record whose wide pin's spelling held no brace (D2780) and a narrowed list of records whose wide pin's arms held none (D9330). Both guards stood in for "a record's render can be an arm of `wide`", but the arm test has read the wide pin's type since #7606. So an alias-spelled wide pin (`type W = {r: i32} | string`) pinned the instance at the box, and the module did not validate. Both guards are deleted, with `monoWideArmsHoldBrace`, `monoTyIsListOfRecord` and emit_mono's last `unionArmsOf`. A loud floor at each guard, firing only where the guard changed the answer, fired in 1 of 24,070 programs, a fixture that prints `7` either way.
