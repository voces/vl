## Infrastructure (Track F)

- **Which depth caps fire, and whether their answers depend on the depth** (type hash-cons stage 6a,
  a census; docs only). 380 cap sites, of which 317 are exhaustion caps. 79 of those fire on the
  corpus and 238 never fire. With every exhaustion cap allowed to run deeper, 1,409 of the 1,418
  files where one fired build byte-identically. Six recursive-type programs stop terminating, and
  three show that `fnTagHasSplitFieldIn`'s depth is a scope by design, not a cap. Probing that
  scope found D16730 (clause 1).
