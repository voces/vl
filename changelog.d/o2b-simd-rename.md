## Testing

- **`std:simd` operations are methods keyed on the receiver's shape (receiver overloading, #9946):** `minU8x16`/`addSatI16x8`/`ltF32x4`/… become `min`/`addSat`/`lt`/… per shape, `select` is keyed on the value (`a.select(m, b)`, D3), the mask ops are `and`/`or`/`xor`/`andNot`/`not`/`anyTrue`/`allTrue`/`bitmask` on the mask, `widenLow`/`widenHigh`, and `narrowSatToU8x16`/`narrowSatToI16x8`/`narrowSatToU16x8` name only the target. `splat*`, `load*`/`store*` and `f32x4` keep their suffix. Breaking for callers of the old names; pass an operation as a value through a lambda. A refused member call on a receiver overload names the plain name, never `select@F32x4`.
- **Migrating from the suffixed `std:simd` names:** `<verb><Shape>(a, b)` → `a.<verb>(b)` for `add sub mul div min max abs sqrt reduceAdd lt le gt ge eq ne addSat subSat and or xor andNot` on `F32x4 U8x16 U16x8 I16x8 I32x4`; `select<Shape>(m, a, b)` → `a.select(m, b)`; `widenLow<Shape>`/`widenHigh<Shape>` → `widenLow`/`widenHigh`; `narrowSatI16x8ToU8x16`/`narrowSatU16x8ToU8x16` → `narrowSatToU8x16`, `narrowSatI32x4ToI16x8` → `narrowSatToI16x8`, `narrowSatI32x4ToU16x8` → `narrowSatToU16x8`; `andMask<N>`/`orMask<N>`/`xorMask<N>`/`andNotMask<N>`/`notMask<N>` → `and`/`or`/`xor`/`andNot`/`not`; `anyTrue<N>`/`allTrue<N>`/`bitmask<N>` → `anyTrue`/`allTrue`/`bitmask`; drop the renamed names from `import { … } from "std:simd"` (methods need no import); unchanged: `splat*`, `f32x4`, `load<Shape>`, `store<Shape>`, `lane`, `withLane`, `dot`, `cross`, `normalize`, the getters and the operators.
