## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **A union type has one registry row, found by its type key (D16025).** Every registration asks
  `unTyKeyHasRow(ty)` before minting a row. That covers a declared alias, and a spelling that places
  `null` elsewhere among the same members. The label index `unRowBySid` and its bind paths are gone:
  `unRowOfName`, `unNameBound`, `unNameBindRow`, `unNameShareTyKeyRow`, `shareObjLitFieldUnionRows`,
  `annAdoptRegisteredName`, and the adopt's null-position test.
