## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **IR declines a bundle result used at none of its signatures:** a direct call of a function returning its parameter called at two signatures, whose value is used at neither (a map's value, a holder a lambda captures), is refused by legacy at `emitBundleResultPick`. The IR built the call anyway and, armed, reported `internal: the IR's plain closure call disagrees with legacy's`; at I8 that would be its first error (D17096, D17106, D17585). The builder now declines the call (`bundlePickRefused`, "bundle result pick refused"). Today legacy's refusal answers, carrying the decline's note. At I8 these legal programs count as capability debt, not as a design refusal.
