## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **D16016 and D2632 closed:** a member written into a union-typed element or map entry, used as a value, yields its own value (`print(us[i] = 5)` over `(i32 | string)[]`), and a record written into an `R` or `R | null` place yields the record. A record whose own row is not the place's is filed as D16019.
