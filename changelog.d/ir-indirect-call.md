## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **IR: calls of closures by any expression, and of capturing nested functions by name:** a call through a list element, a call's result or a parenthesized closure (`fs[0](x)`, `mk(2)(x)`) builds in the IR where legacy writes it plainly. So does a direct call of a nested function that captures through its environment, written as legacy's closure-and-`call_indirect`, and a lifted function passing its own capture on to a lifted callee. Byte-identical; `indirect call` falls from 659 functions to 268 in `tests/cases`. A closure call's argument widened into a wider number, or passed as a non-null closure, list or map, builds as legacy writes it.
