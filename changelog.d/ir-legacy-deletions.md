## Infrastructure (Track F)

- **`scripts/ir-driver-census.sh` ranks coverage steps by the legacy lines they would delete:** each driver row gains its `lines`, the `doors` still reaching it under the shipped claim (the blocker classes of the unclaimed functions that hit it, or `@ir`/`@start`/`@module`) and a `role` (`ast`/`value`/`decide`); `<out>.doors` ranks each class by the lines whose last remaining door it is, split by role.
