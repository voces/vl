## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **A map of two-signature closures walked by its keys (D17095):** `for k in m.keys()` over a `Map()` holding a closure called at two types, at top level, in a function or a closure, bound first or handed on, was refused at emit (and built an invalid module with IR claims). The map now keeps each value as its record of closures; a `values()` walk is still refused.
