## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **`print(m = null)` runs:** `print` of an assignment of `null` to a `K | null` binding (`string`, a literal union, `i32`, `boolean`, a list) took the `i32` arm and failed to validate, or refused a literal-union atom; it now takes the `nulNone` arm and prints `null` (D17118, D17119).
