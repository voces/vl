## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **D16971 closed:** a list literal declared at `i64[] | K[]` or `f64[] | K[]` (`K` a numeric literal union) is boxed under the tag of the arm it is built at. Before, its `.length` read `0`, `is i64[]` was false and `is K[]` trapped. D16970 is closed as ruled: an `i64[]` or `u8[]` arm answers `is K[]` by its tag, per the 2026-11-15 refinement of D16047.
