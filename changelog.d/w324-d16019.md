## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **D16019 closed:** a named record written into a field or element of a narrower record, used as a value, yields the record at its own type (`yOf(h.p = b)` with `b: B` into an `A | null` field). The write moves ahead of its statement, so the receiver is still read before the right-hand side. D16102 is filed for a narrowing that outlives a right-hand side that rebinds the receiver.
