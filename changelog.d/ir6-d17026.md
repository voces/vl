## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **Fixed: an `if` initializing an `A | null` binding, `A` a union member, wrote invalid wasm.**
  The binding's cell is `A`'s nullable variant, but its `if` joined at `A`'s plain record row and
  wrote a narrowed `A | B` box raw into it; the `if` now joins at the cell's variant, a narrowed box
  arm is unboxed to the variant's row, and the binding owes no further unbox. The un-annotated
  spelling, a loud emit reject before, runs too (unfiled: D17026).
