## Type system (Track A)

- **D16959 closed:** a union handed to a generic call at a list, record or map parameter, which binds the type parameter to no one type, is refused at check, naming the call and the union. Before, `vl check` accepted the result at any destination: the call failed to build, or built at `i32` and trapped. A union stored as one map or list still binds as that one.
