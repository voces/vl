## Codegen, memory & runtime (Track B)

- **Step 7 L2, mono: a narrowed argument's pin is tested against the wide pin's type, not its spelling.** `monoNameNarrowsFrom`, which split the ladder's spelled pin with `nullablePartOf` and `unionArmsOf`, becomes `monoTyNarrowsFrom`: the narrowed type is a top-level arm of the wide pin's type, by shape and declared record name. `monoNulRecNamesNarrow` asks the type too. Over 24,070 programs the typed test agreed with the spelling at every one of 16,710 decisions that had both types; where the wide pin had no type (158 decisions), the spelling had answered "no" every time.
