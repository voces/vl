## Infrastructure (Track F)

- **The I8 census counts the gate tools:** `scripts/ir-i8-census/census.sh` adds a sixth population, every `scripts/` program outside the probes and the distilled cells (103 tools), since I8 must build them too; `summary.sh` and `class-programs.sh` report it. The census probe also counts a function whose claim falls back on the verifier, a minted synthetic global or a legacy helper's refusal as unclaimed (`probe-patch.sh`); before, those counted only a module-level row and the function was missing from the census.
