## Type system (Track A)

- **D16994 closed:** a read-only empty `Map()` or `Set()` whose keys or values are read into an annotated `let` takes that type: through `for k in m.keys()`, `for v in m.values()`, `for k, v in m`, or `m[k] ?? d`. Previously it took the `i32` default and the read was refused. Two reads that disagree are refused at check.
