## Infrastructure (Track F)

- **`$VL_IR_REQUIRE_CLAIM=1`:** a function or start body the IR does not claim fails the build. It reports the claim's own refusal verbatim, or else the miss it declined on. `VL_IR_STRICT=1` cannot see such a unit, because a clean decline still builds through legacy, so a coverage claim is now proven under the new switch (lane brief, step 6). A seed without the export refuses the switch.
