## Type system (Track A)

- **D16966 closed:** `as`, `as!` and `as?` over a union of a list and its literal twin (`i32[] | K[]`), which is stored as one list, test its elements as `is` does and run, where they were refused at emit. The whole list always fits, and `K[]` fits when every element is a `K`. The refusal they reached now names a `| null` niche only when the operand has one.
