# D16399: a union field compares with a member field, in either order, 2026-11-15

Lane B. Fixpoint seeds on both sides: before is master `287507121`,
after is this branch.

## Change

`eqRefusals` (`check_ty_relate.vl`) refused `==` when the LEFT operand's record held a field
that is not value-comparable as a whole, such as a general union. So `{f: A | B} == {f: A}` was
refused, while `{f: A} == {f: A | B}` ran through D16394's field-wise compare and tested the arm.
The coordinator settled from the owner's union ruling (2026-11-15) that a union compared with one
of its members is legal inside a record as it is bare.

A pair that compares field by field (two records of one field set stored apart,
`recEqFieldWise`) is now judged per field pair. Each pair meets its own `==`: a union against a
member tests the arm, and a map or function field is still refused, now by that field's own
sentence. `recEqSide` moves from `check_operators.vl` to `check_ty_relate.vl` beside the new
predicate, and the rewrite imports it from there.

Net compiler lines: +28 (+42 / −14, `check_ty_relate.vl` and `check_operators.vl`). Pin-helper delta: 0.
