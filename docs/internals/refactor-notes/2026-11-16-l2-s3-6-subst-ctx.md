# L2 S3.6b: the substitution modes are one scoped context, 2026-11-16

The second slice of S3.6 (`2026-11-16-l2-s3-plan.md`: retire the replay). Coordinator ruling
2026-11-16: option B.

## What changed

Five ambient modes steer substitution and constraint ownership:
`substNestMode`, `substNestDecl`, `cstrOwnedStrict`, `cstrSetOwners` and `substSelfOwner`. They
were set by 50 setter calls across 11 functions, each with a hand restore. One of those
(`fsbRecheckOne`) forced `cstrOwnedStrict` to `false` instead of restoring it.

They are now one record, `SubstCtx` (`check_subst.vl`):
- `substCtxEnter(c)` installs `c` and answers the modes it replaced.
- `substCtxLeave(was, entered)` traps if the modes are no longer `entered`, which means an inner
  scope left one set, then restores `was`.
- `substCtxNest`/`Strict`/`Owners`/`Self` build a variant.

`callFtyPinCstrs` enters three nested scopes (set owners ⊃ nest mode ⊃ strict), and every other
site enters one. The five setters and `SolveAt.ownerWas` are gone. The readers are unchanged.

## A, true arguments, is deferred

The plan says "the owner modes become its arguments". That is deferred to a reassessment after
S3.4d (lane STORE replaces `SS_ARG`'s validator).

The evidence:
- **Not solver-scoped.** The modes are read deep in substitution and relate: `check_subst.vl`
  (`substNestMode` 8, `substNestDecl` 8, `substSelfOwner` 7), `check_relate_core.vl`,
  `check_demands.vl` (`substNestMode` 10) and `check_assignable.vl`. `callFtyPinCstrs` holds the
  nest mode over about ten non-solver steps.
- **Wide threading.** Arguments would mean threading a context through `substTyDeep` and its
  callers.
- **Shrinking need.** `cstrOwnedBy`'s ambient modes mostly serve the argument validator S3.4d
  removes.
