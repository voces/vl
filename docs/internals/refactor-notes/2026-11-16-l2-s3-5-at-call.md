# L2 S3.5: report a failed bound at the call, 2026-11-16

S3.5 of `2026-11-16-l2-s3-plan.md` (design §4.2, choice 1). A failed bound already reported at
the call node. What changes is the wording, by the coordinator's ruling B (2026-11-16): each bound
keeps its own sentence and fix hint, and the parenthetical that said "the body of `f` at the
call's argument types" names the parameter and the body site instead.

## The shape

`compiler/check_at_call.vl` renders it. `atCallWhere(fn, who, on, site, call)` gives
"parameter `x` of `f`, used at L:C":
- **the parameter** is the one parameter of `fn` that the bound's type `on` reads, through derived
  holes (`hdBase`). A hole names its parameter by position (`?f.k`, `?f@o.k`, `?#L<node>.k` for
  a lambda); a `<T>` names it when exactly one parameter is annotated `T`. Zero or several
  readers leave the name out, and the subject is "the body of `f`".
- **the site** is the bound row's `site`. It is left out when it has no position or sits in
  another module than the call (D16862).

Per form:

| form | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| member, UFCS, overload, LET, BM, ARRDEF | `(the body of `f` at the call's argument types)` | `(parameter `x` of `f`, used at L:C)` |
| return | `(the return of `f` at the call's argument types)` | `(parameter `x` of `f`, returned at L:C)` or `(the return of `f`, returned at L:C)` |
| operator | `(the body of `f` applies '+' to this argument)` | `(`f` applies '+' to parameter `x`, at L:C)` |
| hole callee | `(the call of `h` at the call's argument types)` | `(the call of `h` in `apply`, at L:C)` |
| `if` join | `at the call's argument types, the branches … in the body of `f` join A and B: …` | `the branches … join A and B (parameter `x` of `f`, used at L:C): …` |
| argument flow, `ACF_*` | `(the call's argument types)` | `(parameter `x` of `f`…)` |

Unary `-`/`~`, `match` and `min`/`max` now record their node as the BIN row's `site`
(`noteBinCstrAt`). The two readers of a BIN row's site act only on a `BinExpr` or `AsExpr`, so
nothing else reads it. `cexNoteHoleWidth` and the `ACF_EXACT*` rows still record none.
