# IR B0: the `is` test's rungs as one decision legacy's ladder consumes, 2026-11-17

Lane IR2. The self-compile's door to the IR is a boxed-union local and its `is` test
(`const n = nodeAt(ix); if n is LetDecl { … }`): on S1's tree, 2,242 self-compile functions are
held by `expression is` and `local rep box` alone, 2,883 with `member record` beside them. A
throwaway that tagged each self-compile `is` miss with the rung legacy took (legacy runs first in
the shadow) found 579 of 585 at the last rung, `emitIsTestVariant`'s tag test. The IR may take
that rung only where no earlier rung answers, so the ladder becomes one decision first. `CLAIMED`
is unchanged, and no emitted byte moves.

## What landed

- **`isArmOf(isIx, fnIx, from, staged)` names the rung.** `IsArm` is the ladder's 19 rungs in
  their load-bearing order, from `optNull` to `variant` (and `noUnion`, `notIs` for a node that
  is no `is`). It answers the first rung at rank `from` or later that may answer.
  - A rung whose writer always answers is decided exactly, by the predicate the ladder tested.
  - A try rung (`recordFit`, `listFit`, `listAcross`, `litArms`, `armTag`, `numLit`) is decided
    by a superset of its writer's answering paths. `emitListEmptyFitIs` and
    `mapSoftTwinTagFail` touch legacy state or refuse while they decide, so a try rung keeps
    its writer's own conditions and may still decline.
  - `staged` is the receiver staged by `emitIsTest` (`isRecvStagedIx`), passed explicitly.
- **Legacy's ladder is its consumer.** `emitIsTestRungs` writes `isArmOf`'s rung through
  `emitIsArm` and, where a try rung declines, asks again from the next rank. The order and every
  byte are legacy's.
- **What the decision deletes from the writers:** each try rung's opening checks that the
  decision now makes (the null and box tests of `emitRecordFitIs` and `emitListFitIs`, and
  `emitListIsAcrossReps`'s list-union and null tests), `emitIsTestNumLit`'s box guard, the arm
  type `emitIsTestArmTag` computed inline (now `isArmPatTy`, shared with the decision), and the
  ladder's repeated "evaluate a receiver that is no place, drop it, answer a constant" tail
  (`isConstAfterRecv`).
- `IsArm` is registered as a closed set in `compiler/lint.vl`.

For the IR, `variant` from rank 0 means no earlier rung can answer. A "may answer" is a miss,
never a wrong byte.

## The lines

- **Deleted, 216:** the old ladder (169, with its twelve `is` guards and three copies of the
  receiver-then-constant tail), the try rungs' opening checks, `emitIsTestArmTag`'s inline arm
  type, and `emitIsTestNumLit`'s box guard.
- **Moved, about 165:** the exact rungs' conditions into `isArmOf` and two predicates, their
  writers into `emitIsArm`, the try rungs' former heads (`recordFitIsShape`, `listFitIsLeaf`,
  `listAcrossIsPlanned`), `isArmPatTy` and `isConstAfterRecv`.
- **New structure, about 75:** `IsArm`, `isArmRank`, `emitIsArm`'s frame and the consumer loop.
- **Summary, 33:** `litArmsMayAnswer`, `armTagMayAnswer` and `numLitMayAnswer`, supersets of
  the answering paths still inside the three writers that decide while they write. Making those
  writers a plan and a writer (B0b) turns each summary into the exact plan and deletes the
  in-writer decision.
