# 2026-11-13 — lane H: an `==` test's else over a hole defers its literal remainder (D16005)

The `is` complement over an un-annotated parameter defers its remainder to the pin as an
`HD_GNOT` derived hole (D1243). The `==` complement had no such remainder. In the `else` arm of
`if p == "red"`, `p` stayed the parameter's own hole, so a literal demand there (`onlyGreen(p)`)
pinned the parameter to `"green"` and the `then` arm's comparison was refused.

`pushLitEqFactAt` (check_narrow_flow) now pushes `mkDerivedHole(HD_GNOT, "", -1, lit, cur)` on the
false side when the place is a hole, under the same `holeElseRemainderOk` gate as the `is`
complement. The relate step subtracts the literal at the pin. Since D1486 the `!=` test's then arm
takes this false-side fact too, so `if p != "red" { onlyGreen(p) }` runs as well.

Graded on the witness and seven neighbours (D16005's Close section). The numeric literal union,
the generic `T` parameter and the `!=` spelling go from refused to running. The plain `string`
argument, the `then` arm, the three-member union and the annotated twin print the same.
