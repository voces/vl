# L2 S2 group 4 closed: no literal judge needs a stored row, 2026-11-16

Group 4 (literals) of the L2 S2 prerequisites (`2026-11-15-g77-l2-s0-judge-coverage.md`): the
`holeDigits`, `holeLit`, `holeNeg` and `holeNum` families. It asks group 2's question
(`2026-11-15-relate-g2-close.md`): does the body part of any judge need a `(decl, param)` row for
S2's scheme? Docs only.

## Method

This is group 3's probe and population (`2026-11-16-l2-s2-g3-close.md` §Method), from the same run
at master `26f7a8fb`. There are 19 functions,
`grep -E "^(export )?function (holeDigits|holeLit|holeNeg|holeNum)" compiler/check_*.vl`, and 17 were
probed. The probe puts its note before the first line that ends in `{`, so on the two one-line
bodies it landed in the next function:
- `holeLitPinOf`'s note sat in `holeLitMovedSet`, so the table below lists it under
  `holeLitMovedSet`;
- `holeLitNotCopied`'s note sat in `objWidthFitsExact`, so that row is dropped.

`holeLitPinOf` (a map read) and `holeLitNotCopied` (a constant `-1`) are therefore not measured.
Both answer from their arguments alone. No other note is misplaced: each `l2jNote` was checked
against its enclosing function.

## The split

| kind | judges | body part | call part |
|---|---|---|---|
| syntax | `holeNegNumLit`, `holeLitDeepCopy`, `holeLitNotCopied` | the node's kind; a copy of a literal tree | none |
| hole-name decoding | `holeDigitsOf` | the declaration index a lambda's or nested function's hole name spells (`holeParamDecl`) | none |
| literal adoption at a call | `holeNumLitAdopts`, `holeLitAdopts`, `holeLitAdopt`, `holeLitCopyAt`, `holeLitCopyOf` | the `argCstr` row the body wrote: the demanded type `exp`, the delivered `at` and the argument `node` | the literal retyped at the demand substituted under the call's bindings (`adoptHoleLitArg`, from `adoptHoleLitArgs` in `callFtyPinCstrs` and `fsbRecheckOne`) |
| per-argument records | `holeLitPinSet`/`PinOf`, `holeLitMovedSet`/`MovedHas`, `holeLitClaimSet`/`ClaimedHas`, `holeLitCopyAdd`/`CopiesOf` | none | rows keyed by the caller's argument node, written at a call and only when loud, read back by the next call and by the monomorphizer (`emit_mono.vl`) |
| forwarded result at a call | `holeLitFwdRet`, `holeLitFwdRetHeld` | the callee's `HD_RET` row and the `holeBind` walk, stable once the body closes (group 2) | the result read with each generic argument at the one instance its calls take |

- **The prefix is not the mechanism.** `holeDigitsOf` is not about literals: it parses the digits of
  a hole name. It is grouped here only by its prefix.
- **Every body part is syntax or the `argCstr` row.** Literal adoption happens at the call, against
  a demand the body already recorded. In the design that demand is a `BK_LOWER` bound, and the
  adoption is the literal rule of §3.3 (settled choice 2), which arrives at S4.
- **The per-argument records are instance products, not body facts.** They are keyed by the
  argument node, written only outside `inferQuiet`, and read by mono. They are the per-instance
  records M3's caveat (design §10) says S3 must keep producing. S2 leaves them in place.
- **The fit entries with an open owner** are reads: `holeLitMovedHas` (80 programs) and the copies
  (15) read call-side rows from inside a fit question, and the syntax judges read the arena.
  `holeLitAdopt` returns at once under `inferQuiet`, so its 7 fit programs are that early return.

## What it decides for S2

No group-4 judge needs a stored row. The body's share is the `argCstr` row, which S2 moves into the
scheme like the other tables. Adoption and the per-argument records stay with the call: S3's solver,
then S4's literal bounds.
