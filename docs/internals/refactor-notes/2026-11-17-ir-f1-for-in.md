## What landed

- **`forInPlainWhy` is the walk's decision.** It answers "" for the plain walk, else the reason
  in legacy's own predicates:
  - the element is no i32 or string;
  - a string or byte source, a second name, a map or a shadowed `is K[]` list;
  - a literal iterable, which legacy may hoist into a module list;
  - a slot pinned by node;
  - a hoist-safe body, which reads its header once and may unroll.
- **Legacy's walk is cut at its body.**
  - `forInLoopAt` builds the loop record (`ForInLoop`, which now carries `fiHOk`), and
    `emitForInStmt` reads it.
  - `forInListStore` and `forInListSync` follow the list's value.
  - `forInLoopOpen` is everything up to the body: the break block, any unrolled copies, the
    loop, the exit test, the tombstone skip, the bind and the continue block.
  - `forInLoopClose` is everything after it. `emitForInLoop` is the two around `emitStmts`.
- **`SForIn { xfList, xfVar, xfBody, xfAt }`** is a statement in the `control` family. Its
  lowering replays `emitForInStmt` for the plain walk in legacy's order:
  - the loop record at the element variable's declared slot;
  - the list read in stack order, then synced and stored;
  - the cursor;
  - the body's index hoists, which `loopHoistOpen` opens none of for a body that is not
    hoist-safe;
  - the scope bind;
  - `forInLoopOpen`, the IR's body, `forInLoopClose`, and the hoists and scope closed.
- **The loop variable is a local at the list's element rep** (`irBLoopVar`), and a read of a
  `LoopVar` resolves to it.
- **Legacy's loop owns its branches.** The builder holds the walk on its loop stack under a
  label no branch may take, so a `break` or `continue` that targets the walk or crosses it is
  a named miss (`for in break`, `for in continue`). A loop inside the body keeps its own.
- `SForIn` joins `IrNode` in `compiler/lint.vl`, and `xfList` its field tables.

## A fallback this reaches

The self-compile's mode 3 falls back in one more function, `armAdopt`, with the known reason
`non-stack temp: out of order`. That is not the walk. It is a call whose arguments are six global
reads and then an `&&` value. The same three-line call outside any loop falls back the same way on
the A1 seed. F1 reaches it because the walk earlier in the function no longer stops the build.

## Pins

Each is a shared witness, one covered function matching legacy and printing the right value:
- a walk over an i32 list;
- a walk over a string list;
- a walk whose body holds a `while` with its own `break`.

A walk's own `break` and `continue` and a hoist-safe walk are a test of their own: each a named
miss.
