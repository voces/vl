# IR coverage: a v128 list literal on the short list arm (2026-11-21, lane IR-N7)

With v128 a value rep (#9332), a list literal of vectors still missed as `array v128`. In
`tests/cases/simd` that was 19 door rows (`node-in array: miss array v128`), 4 constant cells
(`const cell plain array: miss array v128`, IR-D1's I8a; D16482), 5 start rows and 1 function.
Legacy's v128 arm (`emitArrV128`) writes the elements as they stand, then the backing and the
wrapper at `fa128`/`fl128`: the shape of the scalar short arms.

## Change

- `arrLitShortTail` gains the `v128` arm, `arrLitBackWrap(fa128Ty(), fl128Ty(), n)`. Legacy's
  `emitArrV128` takes it for a short literal and keeps its chunked build past `NEW_FIXED_MAX`.
- The builder's `arrayLit` admits the `v128` arm. `arrLitShortWhy` still names a long literal, and
  the element is delivered at the list's element rep, as for every scalar arm.
