kind onto the seq-ordered walk, but `callFtyPinCstrs` (`check_calls.vl`) still ran it in three

2. `withUnboundAsNever`, then `ifaceSolveAt(at, SS_RET, SS_BM)`, then `refuseOpenNullHoleArg`

- `forElemArgCstrs`, then the open-null refusal.

Every rule kind of the callee is answered in body order (`Bound.seq`), and `SS_ARG`'s validator
runs once, after the walk.
