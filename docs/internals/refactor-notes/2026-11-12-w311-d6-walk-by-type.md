# Step 7 L2 D6: the shape walk takes a type, not a spelling, 2026-11-12

Lane F, D6 interner re-key, the walk cut. Measured at master `b323f223`. The design is in
`2026-11-12-w311-d6-interner-rekey-design.md` (step 2 of its PR sequence).

## What changed

- **`internShapeDeepTy(ty)` and `internFuncTypeShapesTy(ty)` lose their text parameter.**
  - The walk already keyed every step by `shapeWalkKeyOf(kn, ty)`. Its `kn` was the type's
    nominal name, or else the caller's spelling.
  - `shapeWalkKeyOf` read that spelling only to compare it with the nominal name, so a spelling
    other than the nominal led to the same render as "".
  - `shapeWalkName(ty)` now answers the nominal name, or "" on a cycle.
  - Callers stop passing a field text, an arm atom or a declared name. These are
    `internShapeArms` (2), `internNonLowerableFieldShapesTy` (2), `internInlineShapeTyGo`,
    `markRefArrayArmsOf`, `internFuncTypeShapesTyGo`, `collectAnnShapes` and
    `collectNestedFieldShapes`, plus the walk's own recursion.
- **`internShapeDeepOfTy` is deleted, and its render with it.**
  - The 13 callers that dropped its result call `internShapeDeepTy(ty)`.
  - `forceListLeafRepsOfTy` used the render as the ref-list element name. It now mints under
    `rlMintNameOfTy(ty)`, the name every other type-keyed ref-list mint uses.
- **`internOwnShapeOfTy` returns the label of the row its walk interned.** It no longer returns
  the type's render.
  - `internShapeDeepTyGo` now returns the row its record leaf interned at, through `| null`,
    list and map wrappers, and -1 for any other leaf.
  - Mono's call-return pin is the one reader of that string. Its sibling branch already
    returns `sNames[si]`, so both branches now name a row by its label.

  - reached in 806 programs;

  - every one of those is a caller that drops the result (`emit_collect`'s own-shape mints).
- **Mono's call-return pin**, the only reader of the result: reached in 0 of 24,202 programs.
  - The control is D8682's witness, `tests/cases/soundness/record-literal-built-in-hole-beside-boxed-row.vl`. It fires the
    own-shape probe and not the mono probe.

    now names the row the literal was interned at, as its sibling branch does.
