# Step 7 L2 D6: the shape walk takes a type, not a spelling, 2026-11-12

Lane F, D6 interner re-key, the walk cut (step 2 of the PR sequence in
`2026-11-12-w311-d6-interner-rekey-design.md`).

## What changed

- **`internShapeDeepTy(ty)` and `internFuncTypeShapesTy(ty)` lose their text parameter.**
  - The walk key is `shapeWalkKeyOf(kn, ty)`. A hint `kn` equal to the nominal name keys the
    type by that name, and any other hint keys it by its render.
  - A fresh walk takes its hint from the type: `shapeWalkName(ty)` is the nominal name, or "" when
    the type is already on the walk stack.
  - A frame that hands its own type to the other walker passes its own key on, through the
    private `internShapeDeepTyAs` / `internFuncTypeShapesTyAs`. There are two such hand-overs:
    `internShapeDeepTyGo` → the functype walk, and the functype walk's union arm →
    `internShapeDeepTy`.
  - Callers stop passing a field text, an arm atom or a declared name. These are
    `internShapeArms` (2), `internNonLowerableFieldShapesTy` (2), `internInlineShapeTyGo`,
    `markRefArrayArmsOf`, `collectAnnShapes` and `collectNestedFieldShapes`.
- **`internShapeDeepOfTy` is deleted, and its render with it.**
  - The 13 callers that dropped its result call `internShapeDeepTy(ty)`.
  - `forceListLeafRepsOfTy` used the render as the ref-list element name. It now mints under
    `rlMintNameOfTy(ty)`, the name every other type-keyed ref-list mint uses.
- **`internOwnShapeOfTy` returns the label of the row its walk interned**, not the type's
  render.
  - `internShapeDeepTy` returns the row its record leaf interned at, through `| null`, list and
    map wrappers, and -1 for any other leaf.
  - Mono's call-return pin is the one reader of that string. Its sibling branch already returns
    `sNames[si]`.

## What the caller's spelling decided

The old walk took the hint from the caller's spelling on a re-entry. Ignoring that spelling
everywhere (`shapeWalkName("", ty)`) moved no byte: all 24,202 programs are the same. It moved
cost:
- the `vl_scaling_shape_test.ts` pair "diamond record depth" went from under 4.45 to 6.38;
- "diamond record depth under a function type alias" went to 5.81 (bar 2.5).

A probe in the old `shapeWalkName` (first marker per program) found the cheap re-entries: a
frame handing its own type to the other walker, hint equal to the nominal name. Those keep their
key in this PR. Two other re-entry rules were tried and refused:

| re-entry of a type already on the stack | runs → not-runs | new traps | scaling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| keyed by its nominal name | 299 | 302 (unbounded recursion) | passes |
| skipped (a plain cycle guard) | 49 | 6 | passes |
| keyed by its render, except a frame's own hand-over (this PR) | 0 | 0 | passes |

So a type that reaches itself is walked a second time under its structural key, and that second
walk interns rows the first does not.
