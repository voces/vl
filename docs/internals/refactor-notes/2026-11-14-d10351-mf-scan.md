# D10351: the scratch-frame scan reaches a test, a conversion and a match

**Change.** `mfScan` (`emit_collect.vl`) reserves a `.map`/`.filter` scratch frame for every call
it reaches. It listed `IsExpr`, `AsExpr` and `MatchExpr` as leaves, so a call under one of them
had no frame at emit. The three now have arms: the operand of a test and of a conversion, and a
match's scrutinee and arm bodies.

**Measured.** `collection-call-under-test.matrix.vl` (new, 76 cells, 56 graded), master fixpoint
against this branch's: 54 cells move from a loud emit reject to runs in both faces, 2 ran before
(the call as a match scrutinee); 0 runs → not-runs, 0 silent.

**Size.** Net +13 compiler lines (three arms and a two-line comment; the leaf list loses three names); pin-helper
delta 0. Seed +172 bytes against the master fixpoint.
