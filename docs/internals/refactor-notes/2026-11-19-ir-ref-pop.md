# IR coverage step: a ref or string list `.pop()`, 2026-11-19

Lane F2. A coverage step under `ir-design-2026-11.md` §10, stacked on the scalar pop step (#9168);
measured on fixpoint seeds of this branch and of #9168 merged onto master `f2281525b`.

## Why this class

After the scalar pop, the self-compile's pops left were over ref and string lists: `pop ref element`
(statements) 64 first-misses and `method pop` (values) 9; split by list kind, the string-list
statement pop alone kept 36 functions uncovered and the ref-list pops 11 and 6.

## What landed

- **One layout.** `popRefPlanOf` lays out a ref or string list's pop (wrapper, backing, default
  scratch base, ref row) and `popAnyPlanOf` is the scalar plan or that one. `emitPopGo` now takes its
  layout from `popAnyPlanOf` instead of choosing it inline; the no-plan refusals keep their messages.
  A ref row's staging frame stays lazy, resolved by `listOpStageBase` only where a receiver stages.
- **One ref pop tail.** `popRefTail` is what `emitPopGo` did for a ref element once its receiver was
  held: the unchecked read of a pop the checker typed as the element (with its stale-box retag), else
  the checked nullable read. Legacy's `.pop` and `.get` end through it.
- **The IR.** Value and statement pops take `popAnyPlanOf`, hold or stage the receiver, then the
  tail the plan names; a statement drops what the tail left, as legacy's statement caller does.
