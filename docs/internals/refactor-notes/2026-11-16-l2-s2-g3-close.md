# L2 S2 group 3 closed: no container judge needs a stored row, 2026-11-16

Group 3 (containers) of the L2 S2 prerequisites (`2026-11-15-g77-l2-s0-judge-coverage.md`): the
`holeArr`, `holeOf`, `holeMap`, `holePlace`, `holeRec`, `holeField`, `holeElem` and `holeOpen`
families. The question is group 2's (`2026-11-15-relate-g2-close.md`): for each judge, which part is
the body's and which is the call's, and whether the body part needs a `(decl, param)` row that S2's
scheme would read. Docs only.

## Method

A scratch copy of `compiler/` at master `26f7a8fb` (never pushed) calls a note at the entry of every
judge whose name starts with a group-3 prefix: 30 functions in 8 files,
`grep -E "^(export )?function (holeArr|holeOf|holeMap|holePlace|holeRec|holeField|holeElem|holeOpen)" compiler/check_*.vl`.
Each entry records:
- the **mode**: `fit` (`inferQuiet` with `quietDepth > 0`), `flat` (quiet at depth 0) or `loud`;
- the **owner**: the top of `cstrOwnerStack`, else `curFnDeclIx`. Its state is `open` (its loud body
  check has not ended), `closed` (`bodyChecked`), `nested` (not a registered declaration) or `none`
  (module scope and emit);
- the **frame**: `inst` under a pinned re-check (`monoRecheckDepth > 0`), else `gen`.

The population is g77's, rebuilt from the tree: the 1,481 `tests/cases` programs marked `// @run`
that have a named function with an un-annotated parameter. All 1,481 compile, and all 30 judges are
entered. No entry was `flat`, and none had a `nested` owner.

## The split

The prefix names a container, not a mechanism. Read by what each judge takes as input, the 30
fall into four kinds.

| kind | judges | body part | call part |
|---|---|---|---|
| syntax predicates | `holeArrMethod`, `holeMapMethod`, `holeFieldCopyOf`, `holeFieldKey`, `holeOfDecl` | a method name, a `const` copy chain through `identDeclOf`, a key string, a hole name decoded against its declaration | none |
| writers of an existing body row | `holeMapDemandTy`, `holeElemRecFirst`, `holeElemRecLink`, `holePlaceElemTy`, `holePlaceFieldTy` | the map-demand columns `holeMapNames`/`holeMapTys`, which already roll back with the map rows; a `tyLinkRecLists` link between two records written to one `HD_ELEM`; a minted `HD_ELEMV` or `HD_FLD` derived hole | none |
| readers of a body row | `holeMapOf`, `holeMapDemandOpen`, `holeFieldWriteTy` | the map-demand columns; the demand type's open halves; a derived hole's `hdReadNullAsked` row and the written type | none |
| call-side | `holeMapNullValAt`, `holeMapArgNullPin`, `holeMapNodeTy`, `holeOpenFnRetAt`, `holeOpenCallsWait`, `holeFieldCallAdopt`, `holeFieldInstAt`, `holeFieldCallWidened`, `holeFieldRecOf`, `holeFieldCloNote`, `holeFieldSetNote`, `holeFieldBuildsFlush`, `holeRec*` (5) | the callee's map demand or derived holes (rows above); the caller's argument sources, a pure decode of the caller's syntax and the write-scan memo `cw*` | the demand met at the argument's type, the callee's body read under the call's bindings, the records an argument holds built at the instance the field call asks |

- **Every body part is syntax or a row the body already writes.** The map demand is a row keyed by
  hole name, written by the body's map operations and read at the call (`holeMapOf` at
  `check_demands.vl`). The element links and the derived holes are body records of the `HD_*`
  alphabet, which the design maps to `BK_CONT` and `BK_ROW`. S2 moves those rows into the scheme;
  it needs no new one.
- **The fit-question entries with an open owner** (386 programs for `holeMapOf`, 340 for
  `holeFieldCopyOf`, 226 for `holeElemRecLink`) are syntax predicates or reads and writes of those
  rows. A row written inside a fit question rolls back with it (`holeElemRecFirst` keys its record
  under `!r!`, so it rides the map rows), so the open entries record nothing the loud check does not
  record again.
- **The call-side judges are loud only, or ask at a call's bindings.** The `holeRec*`/`holeField*`
  family runs from `judgeFnArgHoleCalls` and from the field-call arm of the call check. Both return
  at once under `inferQuiet` (`holeFieldCallAdopt`'s 62 fit programs are that early return, and
  `holeFieldInstAt` is a pure type comparison). Its queue (`hfb*`) is flushed at module scope
  (`holeFieldBuildsFlush`, 1,481 programs, no owner). The
  `holeOpen*`, `holeMapNodeTy` and `holeMap*Null*` judges read the callee body under the call's
  bindings. Each is the solver's at S3: a `BK_CONT` bound met at the argument, or an instance read.
- **`holeField*` is function-valued.** It builds the records an argument holds with a field at the
  instance a call through that field asks (D13969). That decision is lane G's group 1 (function
  sets) together with S3's per-shape arrow bounds, as #8468 places the bundle decision. It is listed
  here because its prefix is a container's.

## What it decides for S2

No group-3 judge needs a stored row. S2 carries the map-demand columns, the element links and the
`HD_ELEM`/`HD_ELEMV`/`HD_FLD` derived holes into the scheme with the rest of the body's rows. The
call-side judges stay where they are until S3's solver takes them.
