# L5 step 2b: the emitter's `body: u8[]` parameter is gone, 2026-11-15 (lane Q)

Step 2 of `2026-11-14-phase-products-design.md` §3 (an explicit `FnCtx` in the emitter), second
slice. This is also the engineering review's small item 8.

The emitter writes through one writer, which `wTarget` binds to a buffer (`curBuf`). Even so,
**551 `wasmEmit.vl` functions took a `body: u8[]` (or `_body`) first parameter that was the same
buffer**, and handed it to the next call. Only three read it, and only for its length (the
statement spans of `emitFuncBody`, `idxSpanOn` and `emitStmts`).

## Change

- Every leading `body`/`_body` parameter in `wasmEmit.vl` is removed, and with it the matching
  argument at **2,049** call sites in `wasmEmit.vl` and **17** in `emit_sections.vl`.
- The six `body.length` reads become `curBuf.length`.
- Six calls that passed a literal `[]` for an unused parameter (`emitStr`, `pushList*`) lose it.
- In `emit_sections.vl`, `emitFallThroughPad`'s `body` and `emitStartGlobalInitValue`'s
  `bodyExpr` were used only to pass on, so they go too, with their callers.
- The 14 exported functions change signature with the rest. Their only callers outside
  `wasmEmit.vl` were those 17 `emit_sections.vl` sites.
- Each call site's first argument was `body`, or the buffer the caller had just bound with
  `wTarget` (`one`, `bodyExpr`, `buf`). So `curBuf` is what it carried, and the identity diff below
  is the proof.
