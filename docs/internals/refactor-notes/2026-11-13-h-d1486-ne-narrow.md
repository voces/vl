# 2026-11-13 — lane H: a `!=` on a literal discriminant narrows both branches (D1486)

`check_narrow_flow.vl` gave the `==` test on a literal discriminant two facts. The then arm
intersects the parent with the tested literal's arms, and the else arm excludes them
(`pushDiscrimParentFact`). The `!=` test had neither, so both of its branches kept the whole
union and a field read refused with `not on every member`.

- its then arm excludes the tested literal's arms, as the `==` test's else arm does;
- its else arm intersects with them, as the `==` test's then arm does.
