# D15746 closes: a copy of a copy of a bundle holder holds its record, 2026-11-12

D15741 let a `const` copy of a bundle holder be handed on as the holder is. A copy of that
copy was refused: `elabResMayHold` (emit_elab.vl) admitted a copy only of a parameter or of a
call-initialised holder, so `const u = v` was no copy, and `v`'s read by it was one the holder
group could not account for.

- `elabResMayHoldAt`: a `const` copy of another such copy may hold a record, up to 8 deep.
- `elabCopiesDeep`: `elabHolderRecordWith` checks and records a holder's copies through copies
  of copies; `elabHolderCopiesHold` counts a copy's own copies among its reads. The holder's own
  read count keeps its direct copies.

**Grid** (hand-written, eight programs, master against this branch): the witness; a copy of a
copy called directly; three deep handed whole; handed to a one-signature parameter; inside a
function; the last copy inside a lambda; two copies of one holder handed beside a direct call;
a module holder copied inside a function. Master refuses all eight; this branch runs all eight
with the expected output.
