### D15880 — a record that `collectS` will mint a declared row for answered as a union arm while the arms were tagged, so its rep changed during emit

**runs today and must keep running · a refutation pin: before the fix it was reachable only under the step-7 codec candidate that moved the five tag-assignment arm compares onto the storage rep, which turned 2 runs into the loud emit reject `emitProgram: a record type's rep changed during emit, from a union arm to a plain struct: H` · the fix, `repDeclRowsPending`, makes those compares safe, and they now read the rep · found in w314's small-files batch (#7469) · filed 2026-11-12 against master `8f3814d1`**

#### Mechanism

Traced, and the first filing had the direction backwards. `assignTags` runs at the end of
`collectU`, before `collectS` mints the declared struct rows. `repTyIsVariant` and
`repVariantShapeRowOf` (`emit_rep.vl`) exclude a record from the arms only when
`repSlotOfTyDecl` finds its declared row. So during tagging, `H` has no row yet, matches the
shape of an arm of the instance-only union of the nested field's records, and is answered a
union arm. `rvsAnswerNote` records that answer. Once `collectS` mints `H`'s declared row, the
same question answers a plain struct, and the note refuses the change. The refused candidate
made `variantRowsTagEqual` ask that question during tagging, and nothing before it had.

The fix is `repDeclRowsPending` (`emit_rep.vl`). At the end of `collectU` the variant table is
complete, so the declared rows `collectS` will mint are known (`declRowTysOf`, the same
`typeDeclMintsRow` predicate `collectS` mints by). The rep layer counts them as rows from then
on, and `collectS` clears the set once they exist. A record's rep is then the same at its first
question and its last. With the fix, all five tag-phase compares read the storage rep.
