### D15880 — asking a field's storage rep while union arms are being tagged fixes a record's rep before its arm status is decided, so `repOfTy` is order-dependent there

**runs today and must keep running · a refutation pin: reachable only under a refused change, the step-7 codec candidate that moved the tag-assignment arm compares onto the storage rep · that candidate turned 2 runs into the loud emit reject `emitProgram: a record type's rep changed during emit, from a union arm to a plain struct: H` · found in w314's small-files batch (#7469) · filed 2026-11-12 against master `8f3814d1`**

#### Mechanism

Inferred, not traced. The refused candidate made `variantRowsTagEqual` (`emit_variant_sig.vl`)
compare two arm rows by `repOfTy` of their storage types (`armRowsStoreAlike`), not by their
field codes. A probe on that compare, validated by a control that fires on every call, found no
row pair where the rep answer differs from the code answer, so this is not a wrong answer. The
inference: the compare runs while the arms of `K | J` are being tagged, which is before `H`'s
arm status is decided. `repOfTy` records the rep it hands out, so asking it there fixes `H` as
a plain struct. Later `H` becomes an arm, and the emit-time check that a record type's rep never
changes fires. The precondition for moving these compares onto the rep (cut 5 of
`2026-11-11-step7-codec-field-rep-column-design.md`) is one of two things: the tag decision
comes before the first `repOf` of an arm's record, or `repOf` does not memoize a rep whose arm
status is undecided.
