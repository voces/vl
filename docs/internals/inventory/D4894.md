#### Mechanism

The three first-element classifiers in `emit_classify.vl` (`arrLitElemKind`, `arrLitElemName`
and the `arrLitIsRef` they feed) had an `e0 is ArrayLit` rung that keyed the outer list by
the lead inner literal's leaf (`string[]` here), ignoring the union element the checker
recorded, and `arrLitInferUnionElemName` declined to register the union's box row because the
literal is a ref list. The outer backing was built for the lead arm, and the second inner list
(`f64[]`) was stored into it. Both rungs now take the recorded union first, and registration
mints the box row when the lead element is a list literal.
