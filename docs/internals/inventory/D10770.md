#### Mechanism

Not traced. The closure's declared result is a record whose field is a nested list
of a nullable literal union, and the object literal the lambda returns is matched
against a union registry that holds no variant for that record shape. Found while
probing slice C3 (#4509); the outcome is the same on the seed before and after it.

Traced at the close. The closure-result walker mints a second row for the record under the
spelling `{f:("a" | "b"|null)[][],n:i32}` beside the declared `{f:("a"|"b"|null)[][],n:i32}`.
`repRowOfTyStruct` finds both under the literal's adopted type's canonical id, and
`structFieldCodesEq` called them two layouts: the `f` element names differ, so it asked the
inner lists' LEAF rows, and a literal-union leaf has none, so it refused. With no row for the
adopted type, the field-set scan refused every row on the literal's own `string[]` element,
and the literal fell to the union-box path. A list-of-lists field whose two leaves are both
non-record twinned when the inner lists' ref-list element keys (`repElemId`) were equal, until
D10771 stopped the walker minting the second row and the arm was removed.
