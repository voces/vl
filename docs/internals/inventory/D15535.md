Not yet ablated past its ingredients. `u` joins two map bindings of different one-member sets,
and the read `u["k"]` called at `3` reaches emit with an empty parameter type: the call's
signature is never interned for the joined set. The list spelling (`let u = [inc0]` rebound to
`[dbl]`) runs, so the gap is the map holder's.
