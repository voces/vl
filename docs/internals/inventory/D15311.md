#### Mechanism

D15240 builds a call binding's function at the read's record through `recAdoptNote`, which hands
`mk4`'s build on to `mk2` (D8143), and the held arm also adopted the binding there
(`recAdPushDecl`). The adoption declines the hand-on, so `mk4` returned J while `mk2` returned its own
record. Two narrower attempts each regressed and were measured before this one: delivering every held
call unadopted broke a maker also called at its own type (`d15240_maker_also_own`) and lost
`d15240_own_use`'s refusal; scoping only by "hands on a call" lost the refusal of a two-level default
also handed on at its own type. An inline `?? mk4()` beside a held one-level call default still
fails: D15317.
