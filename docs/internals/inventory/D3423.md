#### Mechanism

D3240's `HD_TVW` read declined a declaration that holds a literal (`tvPlaceWrite`), so this place
kept the template's non-null narrowing and the shared body folded its null tests. The literal
union now takes that read (`tvWriteLandTy` lands a non-null pin on the whole union, as no one
member names it). A `??` over that read keeps the union when its default is a member (`HD_COAL`
holds the unwidened default; `monoPinCoalJoin` pins the union), and otherwise lowers as the
operand's atom widened to `string` (`coalesceRetypedAtomToStr`). `.length` of a string-literal
union is its member's length. A number union ([D4164](D4164.md)) and a mixed one
([D4163](D4163.md)) keep the template's read.
