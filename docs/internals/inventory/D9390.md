#### Mechanism

D9331's plain record parameter now takes its signature token from the declared record's row.
A union parameter still has no such rung: `sigParamTokOfTy` renders the whole union with
`tyToEmitName`, `7·2^depth` characters, and `annParamKindTy` classifies that spelling
(`annUnionSigKind`, `tyTopLevelSplit`, `splitUnionAtoms`, `cukTextOfName`), each scanning it.
The render is memoised within one walk, so the remaining cost is the length of the spelling and
the scans over it. Under the null collector the spelling outgrows the 64 MiB object cap at
depth 22, which `tests/vl_compile_gc_test.ts` uses as its trap witness.

| face | depth 8 | depth 12 | depth 16 | depth 20 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `g(u: A \| string)` (this witness), master `efdcd15d0` | 17.3M | 89.8M | 753.7M | — |
| the same, after D9331 | 16.7M | 76.8M | 509.7M | 6.59e9 |
| `g(u: A)` (D9331's witness), after D9331 | 13.5M | 43.8M | 100.5M | 173.2M |

Fuel is `VL_FUEL=1` guest fuel of one `vl build`, which is deterministic.
