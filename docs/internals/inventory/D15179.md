#### Mechanism

The record solve notes a delivery of a generic identity's argument in `recAdoptNote`'s identity arm
(`recPassThroughArg`), and of a hole identity's in `recHoleIdentityAt`. Each judged a fresh call on
the raw peeled argument (`recInferredFnOf(pp)`), so a binding holding the call (`k`) matched no
branch, no adoption was noted, and `mk()`'s literal stayed at its own record. The freshness judge
(`recFreshOriginDecl`) follows `idn(k)` to `k` to `mk()` and accepted the delivery, so the
acceptance followed the chain and the build did not. The direct spelling `const m: J = k` is
delivered through the Ident arm's held-call origin (D8140), and a literal-holding `k` through the
identity arm's literal branch, which already reads `recOriginExpr`.

#### Two origin walks, kept apart on purpose

The freshness judge (`recFreshOriginDecl`) and the record solve's origin walk (`recOriginExpr`,
check_rec_fresh.vl) agree on this row's path and differ in four places by design; merging them
changes every freshness reader.

1. **What they return.** A declaration, the binding or function whose storage is shared, against
   the origin expression, the node a build is stamped on.
2. **Identities.** `recOriginExpr` peels a generic identity itself; `recFreshOriginDecl` does not,
   and its callers peel one with `recPassThroughArg` first.
3. **Element reads.** `recFreshOriginDecl` follows an index to its list binding (`recIndexBase`);
   `recOriginExpr` stops at the `Index`, which the solve delivers through its own arm
   (`recContainerReadDeliver`).
4. **A call writing type arguments** is an origin for `recOriginExpr` (D8022), the value built at
   those arguments; `recFreshOriginDecl` has no such stop.
