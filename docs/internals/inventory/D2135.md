#### Mechanism

`gwCall` in `compiler/typecheck.vl` refused every callee `fnDeclIx` named, because the checker
had no fact about a function beyond its type. The getter step budget (D2061) walked getter
bodies only, `fnWriteEffects` answers per parameter and folds an escape into "writes
everything" (right for narrowing invalidation, wrong for a write fact), and the three syntactic
"no effect" predicates stop at a call (`function-effects-design.md` §A1). The ruling (owner,
2026-09-23, scope "(b+)") needed one analysis with four facts and a reader for each.

#### Grading list

The generic at two pins (`same<T>` over `==`: admitted at `i32`, refused at `string`); a UFCS
call (`self.twice()`); a std function (`hypotF64`); a user operator; a two-deep helper chain;
an un-annotated helper; the body-shape test's reach through the helper
(`tests/vl_getter_body_shape_test.ts`); the refusals in D2136.

#### Close

A per-function summary in the checker (`esRowFor`, keyed by declaration plus the type bindings
a call pins, computed callees first with Tarjan's strongly connected components, once): what it
writes, whether it allocates in the source, its cost in the getter's steps (the D2061 walk, now
shared), and its host calls. `gwCall`, `gwBinExpr` and the `"[]"` arm admit a callee whose
summary at the call's types has none of the four; the call costs 1 plus the callee. Hover and
the `effectsDump` export are the summary's other readers. Fixtures
`getters/getter-calls-functions.vl` and `tests/vl_effects_summary_test.ts`.
