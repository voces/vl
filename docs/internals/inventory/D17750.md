#### Mechanism

A function's result and the literal it returns share one layout. With one call site the
destination chooses it (D15900); with two, the result is the join of its calls' destinations.
Here the annotated binding's destination holds closures while the other call's holds the set's
ids, and the two meet at `deliver`'s result: no single layout serves both, so the tag plan
refuses the set (`ftHoldCtxOk`) and the variable-index call `xs[k].f` at two signatures has no
dispatch. The join needs a rule for where a result whose calls disagree is built.
