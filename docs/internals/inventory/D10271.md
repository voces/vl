#### Mechanism

Not traced. The `null` face of [D10270](D10270.md): at the pin `T = null` the callee's result
`T | null` is the degenerate `null | null`. The un-annotated lambda is refused by the pinned
re-check; annotated as `(): T | null => x` it passes `vl check` and the emitter refuses
`a function-value call has no interned signature — its result type null|null has no
representation in the function-value ABI`, so the two faces land in different clauses.
