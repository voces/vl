#### Mechanism

The `null` face of [D10270](D10270.md): at the pin `T = null` the callee's result
`T | null` is the degenerate `null | null`. The un-annotated lambda is refused by the pinned
re-check; annotated as `(): T | null => x` it passes `vl check` and the emitter refuses
`a function-value call has no interned signature — its result type null|null has no
representation in the function-value ABI`, so the two faces land in different clauses. The
re-check failed because `argPinRecheck` compared the argument substituted at the pin
(`() => null`) against the parked want unsubstituted (the enclosing adapter's `() => U`); the
emit face because `substNullableTy` rebuilt `T | null` at `T = null` as a nullable of `null`.
