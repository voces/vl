#### Mechanism

The general rule: every call through a `const` bound to a lambda that captures nothing, in any
frame, is a direct call of the lambda's function at its signature's rep. Built naively in
`callRhs`, it moved 18 running distilled classes to loud emit rejects, by two mechanisms (the
filing named three; `argument count`, `print operand rep` and `convert novalue -> i32` are one):

1. **A template's binding is not the instance's.** In a generic instance, `identDeclOf` names the
   template's `LetDecl`, and `fnStmtsPosOf` on its lambda falls back to `monoOrigNode`: the first
   instance's clone. A later instance called that clone, at another instance's signature. The
   callee is now the binding the instance reads (`identLetOfRead`), and `constLambdaPos` declines
   a slot that does not hold the lambda itself (`fnStmts[pos] != init`), which covers a lambda
   captured from an enclosing generic frame, where `identLetOfRead` names no binding.
2. **A bundle record is picked only off a closure call.** A top-level `const` lambda returning a
   bundle record (`topLambdaRecordFe`) had its member picked only where the call was not an
   `RCall`; the direct call is one, so its value stayed the record and met a closure-rep global
   (`global initializer coercion`). The pick now applies to both.
