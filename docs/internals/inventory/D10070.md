#### Mechanism

A union source delivered into a map type is not walked field by field, so its widened slots are
one `*` entry: everything under the handle. Stored into `hm`, that entry becomes `[]*`, and
`rcwWriteHits` (check_program.vl) counted a store at `[]` itself — another `.set` or index store
into `hm`, which replaces the handle in the slot — as a write under it. The delivery's own store
was exempt since D10042; a second store of the source, of an alias, or of a fresh map was not.
A store at the base of a `*` entry is judged by the typed entry beside it, when the slot is
the source's own, and is the container's business otherwise.
