#### Mechanism

The annotation is a union whose first member is a nullable, not the nullable wrapper
`A | B | null` is, so neither the substitution's union arm nor D3499's fold in its nullable arm
flattened it, and the instance's `boolean | null | i32` disagreed with what the caller narrowed.
`substTyDeep`'s union arm now folds a null the substitution leaves inside the union (a nullable
member, or a parameter bound to `null`) out to one nullable wrapper over the rest, in member
order (`substUnionNullFolded`), so `(A | null) | B` and `A | (B | null)` are `A | B | null` at
every pin. A union that declares a bare `null` member keeps its shape, and so does one with a
literal-union or literal member, whose folded and declared spellings do not yet share a rep
([D3140](D3140.md)'s price). Kept-apart and deferred
joins are untouched: spreading a binding there moved the member order of joins that already
built (D3499's first attempt).

The closure-body defect first filed here is not this mechanism and is now its own row,
[D3506](D3506.md).
