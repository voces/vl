### D3751 — a `const` holding a map read or a scalar list's `.get`/`.pop` was a `S | null` box, where `?? d` over the same read made none

**closed — the witness prints `12`, and its unoptimised `a` makes no allocation where it made one per call and two per call that found `k` · a cost row, no wrong value · sunpa SP-046 · lane SP · filed 2026-10-06 against master `082c36f12`**

#### Mechanism

`??` over a map read, a scalar list's `.get(i)` or a `.pop()` is lowered fused
(`emitMapGetOr`, `emitListGetOr`, `emitPopOr`): the probe's slot or the bounds test selects the
element or the default, and no `S | null` value is made. A binding of the same read took the
binding's declared rep, the boxed nullable, and binaryen's Heap2Local cannot take apart a box
held in a local that is null on one path. `nulScalarSplit` (emit_rewrite.vl, a pass after
`loopValueRewrite`) rewrites a function-local `const g = e`, typed `S | null` for `S` one of
`i32`, `i64`, `f64`, `f32` and `e` one of those fused reads, into `let $np = true` and
`const $nv: S = e ?? (if true { $np = false  0 } else { 0 })`. It does so only when every use
of `g` in its scope is a null test (`== null`, `!= null`, `is null`, `is S`, and so `match`,
which the checker desugars to them), a read the checker narrowed to `S`, or `g ?? d`. A
capture, any other use, a shadowing name or a generic body leaves the binding boxed, so a
value handed on as `S | null` keeps the box it needs. Two fused paths were widened with it:
`xs.get(i) ?? d` over an `i64[]` or `f32[]`, and `xs.pop() ?? d` over an `f64[]`, `i64[]` or
`f32[]`, each where the `??` keeps the element type.

`boolean | null` needs nothing: it is the 0/1/2 niche, and made no allocation before. A
producer that is not fused (a call returning `S | null`) is left alone: it hands over a box
already, and splitting it after the fact hid the box from binaryen's own folding (one
`struct.new` survived at -O3 where none had).

#### Ablation

Graded by 5 scalars × 5 producers (`m.get(k)`, `m[k]`, `xs.get(k)`, `xs.pop()`, a call) × 13
uses (9 null-test forms, 4 escapes) × 2 faces (`const g` and `const g: S | null`): 650 cells,
every one printing its hit and miss value. All 650 print the same on master and after. In the
288 non-escape cells of the four fused producers over the four wide scalars, `f` keeps no
`struct.new` after, unoptimised or at -O3; on master it kept two or three unoptimised and one
or three at -O3. The 72 `boolean` cells had none before either. The call producer and every
escape cell are unchanged.

| ingredient removed | outcome |
| --- | --- |
| the fused producer (a call instead) | boxed, as before (the callee made the box) |
| the null test (a use passing `g` as `S \| null`) | boxed at the binding, the value right |
| `const` (a `let`) | boxed: a later write could store `null` |
