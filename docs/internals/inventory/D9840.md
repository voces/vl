#### Mechanism

A record whose rep key is longer than 262,144 characters is interned under a runaway cap,
`"@big:<n>"`. Depth 15 is the first depth past it. `<n>` was the ARENA index of the type, so
each copy of `A15` the un-annotated locals produced had its own key, and the builder took
another row's struct for one of them. Depth 16 and 17 happened to resolve every copy to one
index; annotating the locals does the same. The cap now carries the structural id it caps,
which two copies of one record share. Closed 2026-10-22 on master `a901a928e`.
