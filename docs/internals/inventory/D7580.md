#### Mechanism

`cwIxBuild` (compiler/check_write_scan.vl) walks every node with `cwIxNote` and banks the
write-scan rows, and `cwFrameReset` throws them away whenever the arena's length changes. The
first build comes from `checkNode` through `calleeTyIsScoped` → `cwSoleBinderOf`. The second
comes from `rcwRunPending` → `rcwArenaReady`, after the checker's rewrites have grown the arena.
In a `--names` profile of `src/game.vl` (827 samples), epoch 1 holds 11 samples of frames and 25
of index, and epoch 2 holds 8 and 20. Inside `cwIxBuild`, `cwIxNote` is 48 of 59 samples
inclusive: `sidOf` 16, `cwIxNoteParent` 14 (its `nodeSpineChildren` list), `cwFrameOfUse` 9.

Every checker arena write now goes through `arenaSetNode` / `arenaNoteEdit` (the
`arena-slot-write` lint holds it), so the log names every in-place edit. It still cannot drive
an incremental rebuild. The index answers from a snapshot taken at its first question: an
in-place edit made after that question is invisible to every reader until the length changes.
Deferring or narrowing the epoch-1 build therefore changes what the unconverted readers see.
A build that answered `cwSoleBinderOf` from a lazily built table, with the full index beside it
as a verify twin, matched the index on every question asked (`difftree --identity` 0 differ).
Without the twin, 67 modules differed, all of them generic function values delivered through
bindings. Moving one reader to its own table moves the build to the next reader in line:
`cwBoundInitOf` / `cwLambdaDeclOf` from `constLambdaOf`, then `recFnDeclByName`, then
`recBindDeclOf` in epoch 2.

An identity-preserving incremental rebuild needs the closure of every node whose row depends on
an edited slot. Frame resolution, `cwPeel`, `rcwBlockTail`, `cwFnTailStmt` and the liveness
rows all read non-locally, and about 52% of diderven's nodes sit under a slot the checker edits.
It also needs rows spliced into node order and sids minted in the same order. The ceiling is the
epoch-2 index and frames (28 of 827 samples). The constant factor inside `cwIxNote` (`sidOf`, the
child list in `cwIxNoteParent`) is the lever that leaves the answers alone.
