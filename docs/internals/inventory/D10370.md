#### Mechanism

Measured 2026-11-11 on master `5613f704`. `fsPlanMember` and fn_split.vl are no longer in the
tree. The check's tag decision tried the binding's join `{id, third, same}` (its reads are typed
at it, `elabFnLetTy`) and refused the `let` itself: `ftNodeOk`'s `LetDecl` arm joins the
initialiser `pick(5)`, whose set `{id, same}` is a subset, and `ftValuesOk` admitted a subset only
through a read (`ftSubsetRead` took an `Ident`). The join stayed untagged, the re-check vetoed the
wide `let` (`fnTagEtaAgain`), and `{id, same}` then refused the store of `pick3(1)` (`ftJoinAt`).
