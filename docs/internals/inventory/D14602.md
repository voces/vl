#### Mechanism

E1 moves the emitter's capture lists onto one per-function list of captured declarations,
read at the template from `identDeclOf` with sibling folds closed over callee rows. Graded
over every case, that list answers all but 6 functions in 4 files, and those 6 are this
shape: a sibling or lambda calls a capturing nested generic through an alias `k = inner`.
The emitter decides per INSTANCE of the enclosing template whether the call goes through the
binding (capture `k`) or is re-pointed to an instance of `inner` (fold its captures, `x`);
two instances of `sib` above take different arms. Treating a single `cwFnValueTargets`
target as a fold fixes these 6 and breaks 10 others, so the checker's devirtualisation fact
is not the decision. The other witnesses are
`generics/alias-of-capturing-nested-generic-delivered-as-value`,
`generics/hole-lambda-calls-per-instance` and
`generics/unwritten-hole-lambda-let-captured-in-generic-at-three-instances`.

The deletion target is the monomorphiser filing each call under the instance it re-points
the call to (D14601's target); the capture list then reads that filing for instances. Until
it exists the emitter's by-name capture banks stay, and E1 does not land.
