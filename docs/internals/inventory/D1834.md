#### Mechanism

`fnRetF32ArraySid` and `fnRetAnnF32ArraySid` are the two members of the sixteen-reader
return-kind family that still took a bare `(sid)` and resolved through `fnIndexOfSid` — the
flat, first-occurrence-wins name→slot map. `#2815` converted twelve and `#2781` two; these
two were left because f32 has neither channel its i64/f64 siblings use, and the omission is
invisible until a per-pin clone exists. With the STRUCT pin instantiated first the flat map
answers the template's slot in the f32 instance too, so `exprF32Array`'s callee arm never
recognises the `f32[]` result and the index read reaches no collected array type. The f64
twin is the oracle and has always run, because `fnRetF64ArraySid` was converted.
