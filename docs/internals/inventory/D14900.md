#### Mechanism

A record result's row follows the rep stamp of every value the function returns
(`criRowByStamp`). Where a value is built by a call, directly or through a `const`, its row is the
one the checker stamps that root call at (`retValueRootCall`); where the stamp is open, as an
identity instance's is at its template, the row is the function's recorded result. That result is
the record the calls reaching the instance agree on (`elabDeliveredRowOf`), or for a plain
function its own calls' agreed stamp, so no value keeps the body walk by a predicate.

What stays open is the delivery row for an instance whose calls do not agree on one, or agree on
one the lowering does not build: the `J | string` box a delivery widens to (`elabWidenedRet` has
no feed at a call) and the instances whose calls disagree. The row-stamp shadow counts 20 such
record functions on master `5da508641` with stage 2 applied: 6 boxes, 3 with no token at the
stamp, and 11 whose calls disagree or name another row.
