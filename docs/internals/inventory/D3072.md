**closed · the same wrong pin as [D3071](D3071.md), at a generic's hole: the walk variable now binds `T` at its union · fixture `tests/cases/closures/unannotated-lambda-union-walk-variable.vl` · was loud emit reject · found by the #3266 review · the same on master `b09566e16` · filed 2026-09-28 against master `b09566e16`, closed 2026-09-28 against master `67ca68cf3`**

#### Mechanism

`app`'s `T` binds from `monoArgPinName(e)`, the ladder [D3071](D3071.md) describes. The
narrowed walk variable `e` fell to its `i32` catch-all, so the instance was keyed at
`(i32)=>boolean`, a signature the lambda (built at the checker's `string | i64`) never interns.
Annotating the lambda moved the failure to invalid wasm, from the same `i32` instance. The
D3071 rung binds `T` at the narrowed union, and both faces run.
