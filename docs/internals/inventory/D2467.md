### D2467 — `m[k] as? V` over a scalar-valued map refuses at emit: the read has no box and no nullable ref to test

**loud emit reject · found grading D2466's consumers · filed 2026-09-25 against master `086466d1a`**

Repro:

    const m: {[string]: i32} = Map()
    print(m["zz"] as? i32)
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl build: `as i32` needs a BOXED union operand or a nullable-REF one

#### Mechanism

`emitAsCast`'s `as?` arm lowers only a boxed union or a nullable ref. A bare scalar map read
is neither: its `null` is the probe's miss (D2466). The `as!` arm already fuses the probe; `as?`
has no such arm. The cast is also a no-op at the type level (`i32 | null as? i32` is the read
itself), so the fix is the probe fused the same way `as!` fuses it.

#### Ablation

| ingredient removed | outcome | needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the read bound first (`const v = m[k]; v as? i32`) | not graded | unknown |
| `as!` instead of `as?` | runs | **yes** |
