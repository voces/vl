### D14562 — checking top-level functions under module-binding narrowings costs more per narrowed binding than reading one

**runs today and must keep running · a COST row, not a wrong answer: the scaling-shape axis `narrowed module bindings` (`tests/vl_scaling_shape_test.ts`) reads a guest-fuel ratio of 2.479 on master `a015e4e3c`, 2.524 after the width-family PR and 2.606 after D14434's global-storage slice (2.565 on master `69966e9c8`), each time by a fixed cost leaving both arms; the bar went from 2.5 to 2.6 and then 2.7 · filed 2026-10-27 against master `a015e4e3c`**

#### Mechanism

Not yet profiled. The axis's two arms declare the same 600 nullable module `let`s, assignments
and one-binding readers, and differ only in whether the functions follow the assignments and so
are checked under their narrowings. The many-entity arm costs about 2.5 times the other, so
something a top-level function does still scales with the narrowed bindings in scope rather
than with the ones it reads. [D2529](D2529.md) is the rule that made the narrowing reach those
bodies at all.

The width-family PR moved the ratio from 2.479 to 2.524 by cutting a fixed cost from both arms,
not by adding a per-entity one (guest fuel, `a015e4e3c` seed vs candidate):

| arm | master | candidate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| many-entity | 1.146e9 | 1.134e9 |
| few-entity | 4.624e8 | 4.493e8 |
| ratio | 2.479 | 2.524 |

D14434's global-storage slice did the same (master `69966e9c8` seed vs candidate): the many arm fell 1.131e9 → 1.120e9 and the few arm 4.411e8 → 4.296e8, so the ratio rose 2.565 → 2.606.
