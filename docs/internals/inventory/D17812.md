#### Mechanism

D17800's close refuses a delivered fact for any binding whose own type is nullable
(`eplDeliveredAgrees`, emit_elab.vl). The coordinator's rule is general: a delivered fact is taken
only where it is the binding's own type, closing its holes, never a narrowing of it. A destination
reached through a narrowing (`is`, `!= null`) types the narrowed read, not the binding. The check
records the delivery (`ctnDeliveryNote`, check_assignable.vl) without saying whether the read it
delivers was narrowed, so the elaboration cannot ask that; the nullable test stands in for it. The
close moves the test to the note: record a delivery only where the delivered read is the binding
unnarrowed, then delete the nullable rung.
