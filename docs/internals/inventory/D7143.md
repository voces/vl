#### Mechanism

Probed at `vrProp`, with the no-`push` spelling as the control. The likely reading held: the
value-flow question met `fs.push` as a member read that is not a call and answered lost, so
the literal's slot kept its open type and was named after the pruned `other` stub. The
question now treats the receiver of a list `push` as no read, and treats the pushed argument
as stored at an element of the receiver's declaration.
