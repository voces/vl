### D3357 — a join of two list parameters `T[]`/`U[]` read through an element builds invalid wasm at a nullable pin

**check-clean invalid wasm · clause 1 · open · the same on master `78abe1318` · found by the #3310 round-2 re-review (`rv3310r2-Q7ZK/x/t6.vl`, `t7.vl`, `pf6.vl`, `pf9.vl`) · filed 2026-09-30**

#### Mechanism

Not localised past the ablation. The two lists join apart as `T[] | U[]` (D2760), and the element
read `r[0]` rewrites to a narrowing chain whose arms join `T` with `U`. Lane JW's kept-apart join
of bare parameters (D3268) typed that inner join `T | U` and broke two shapes: a field read
through the element (`e.x`, the `pf*` witnesses) refused as a read on `never`, and the
`string | null` element above trapped on a null reference. So the kept-apart join is **gated
off** in any body taking a list of a type parameter (`tyParamOfInnermostBody`). That restores
master's typing, and master's defect: the witness above is invalid wasm, as on master.
