#### Mechanism

With equal identifiers sharing one string, `__str_eq__` answers by identity, but
`__map_probe__` still runs `__str_hash__` over the key, which is O(length), on every probe. On a
local-name program at length 256 the leaf owners are `scopeLivePos` (emit_state.vl:628, 38 of
775 samples; its callers are `paramTypeNode` and `declaredSlotOf`), `lookupDepth`
(check_scopes.vl:124, 11) and `identResolve` (check_side_rows.vl:368, 5); `cpAccFlush` (31) is
the lexer's own scan and is inherent. A fix keys the emit scope stack and the checker's scope
lookup by symbol id (`symbols.vl` `sidOf`), resolved once per identifier node, rather than by text.
