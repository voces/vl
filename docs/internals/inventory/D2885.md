#### Mechanism

As filed, not localised; closed with D3231's fix (`printNarrowLiveAtoms`). The `else` arm narrows `v` to `string | boolean`, a remainder of the box whose
print plan the tag dispatch declines once the declared union carries a list arm. The two-member
remainder of a scalar union prints, and so does the list union with one other member. A sibling
of [D2779](D2779.md) and [D2808](D2808.md), which reach the same refusal through a pin.
