#### Mechanism

`a1` is called directly at two types and runs: mono's per-type split gives each call shape its
own parameter. Through `b`, a binding of `F` seeded from `a1`, the two calls reach `a1` by its
value, which the split does not follow, so mono refuses. With `a("x")` beside the calls, `F` stays
generic and the check passes (D17323's closing does not apply). The refusal is emit's.

| --- | --- | --- |

| the annotation: `let b = a1` | runs | **yes** |
| `a("x")` | refused at check instead (D17323) | **yes** |
