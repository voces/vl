#### Mechanism

`monoNarrowedPinName` answered a narrowing to one arm, or to a niche base, but not to a smaller
union of the wider one's own arms, so the narrowed `string[] | i32[]` had no name and `W` was not
bound. Closed in `emit_mono.vl`: when every arm of the narrowed type is an arm of the wide one
(`monoArmsNarrowFrom`), the narrowed union is registered and answered (`monoInstUnionAnnPin`,
D4931's).
