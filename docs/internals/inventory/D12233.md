#### Mechanism

`fnFlowAliasLam` walks a chain of un-annotated aliases back to the hole lambda's `const` so the
call can take that lambda's instance, and stops after `MONO_ALIAS_CHAIN_MAX` (64) links with
`FF_CHAIN_LONG`. `monoChainLamFe` turns that answer into a refusal. The bound guards the walk,
not the language: every link has the lambda's one signature.

#### Ablation

| ingredient removed | outcome | needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| one link (`l64(1)`) | runs, prints `1` | **yes** |
| the top level (the chain inside `function main`) | still refuses | no |

#### Grading list

The chain inside a function; a chain through a `let`, which `viaLet` counts.
