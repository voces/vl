#### Mechanism

The refusal is at `midTwo(same, same)`. Under `vl run` that half runs alone, and so does the
`const` half. The `midTwo` half's refusal under the wasm cases oracle on its own was another
defect, D15602: the check read the previous case's annotation bank. This row still refused
under `vl run` on one fresh compile, before and after D15602's fix.

The refusal is mono's, on a SECOND walk of `outerTwo$1`. `monoCloneKeepsHoles` pads `monoGen`
with 0 up to the slot of a clone that keeps holes, here `outerFn`'s capturing lambda. That pad
covers every instance the walk appended before it, `same$1` and `outerTwo$1` among them. The
loop over non-generic declarations read `monoGen.length` after the walk, so it walked those
instances again. The first walk had already rewritten `midTwo(same, same)`'s arguments in place.
The second walk re-pinned `b` from a rewritten read that no arm closes. A lambda cloned before
`outerTwo$1` exists pads nothing past it, which is why the order matters.
