#### Mechanism

The refusal is at `midTwo(same, same)`. Under `vl run` that half runs alone, and so does the
`const` half. The `midTwo` half's refusal under the wasm cases oracle on its own was another
defect, D15602: the check read the previous case's annotation bank. This row still refuses
under `vl run` on one fresh compile, before and after D15602's fix. Not yet traced past the message.
