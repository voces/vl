#### Mechanism

Not localised. The read is typed at the hole's variable, and the `== null` arm lowers `null`
by that type's rep, which has none. At a nullable pin, the list literal is now annotated at its
element list ([D2899](D2899.md)) and the compare runs. At an `i32` pin, nothing annotates it.
