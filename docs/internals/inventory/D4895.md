#### Mechanism

The record list's own ref-list slot is the anonymous row (`#anon0`), while the union's
`{x: i32}[]` arm resolves to the slot keyed by the shape's spelling, so
`unionHasRefArrayArmSlot` finds no arm with the value's slot and `emitUnionCoerceUnder` fails.
One of the anonymous-row twin-slot family.
