#### Mechanism

The IR claims `go` (`VL_IR_SHADOW=2`: covered, bytes differ in locals) and writes the last
`is` over the cell's member read where legacy reads the cell's box (`__map_entry__`, then the
box's tag). The branch write makes the cell narrowable; the later whole write re-narrows it to
`string`, and the test's receiver is then built at that member's rep under a box-typed slot.
