#### Mechanism

A profile of the N = 4,800 build on a `--names` seed puts the growth in three per-union
lookups that each walk every union. `unRowOfCanon` (compiler/emit_rep.vl) scans `unTyIx`
comparing canon ids, 29.5% inclusive. `declaredStructUnionMembers`
(compiler/check_nj_join.vl) scans `gRootStmts` and string-compares each `UnionDecl`'s name,
19.4%. `buildVariantTwins` (compiler/emit_union_atoms.vl) has an inner loop over all
variants per variant, 10.3%. `__str_eq__` is 9.4% of the total.
