#### Mechanism

From the ablation only. At the call `mid(same, same)` the checker reads `b` as `F`'s instance,
the one `a("x")` takes at `string`, so the delivery to `twice` is refused; a direct call `b(3)`
is not, and after [D6703](D6703.md) runs at `a1`'s instance. The two answers disagree about one
binding, and which one the design owes is not settled.
