#### Mechanism

No call fixes `y`, and the validator's sentence shows the lambda lowered with `y` at `i32`
while its body concatenates it with a `string`. Unlisted and uncalled, it runs (D5934 lowers
an uncalled hole lambda at its default); the list read is what reaches a lowering the default
does not fit. The emitter's refusal for the cell-write spelling (`stored in a list, or in a
variable a nested function writes`) does not fire here. Not traced further.
