Was: loud emit reject · clause 2 (`emitProgram: a function returning a parameter called at two signatures is called where its result is not used at one of them`) · found grading [D15321](D15321.md) · filed 2026-11-04 against master `bf39d33f0`**

Repro:

    const u = lap((a) => a + 1)
    const w = { f: u }

Want:

    5 2.5

#### Mechanism

A holder's fact (D15144) admits a read only as a call through it, a hand, a
copy into a `const`, a `return` or a link (B2d). A read as a field or element of a literal is none
of them, so `u` gets no fact. The slot facts (B2a, B2b) take only a call stored in the literal
directly. The slot fact now takes a read of a `const` call holder, written only by its
initialiser, as that initialiser's call (`elabSlotValueCall`). It records the holder in the same
step, when every read of the holder is a store into the slot or a call through it at the record's
signatures, in its own function (`elabSlotHolderHolds`). A copy of the field called at one
signature beside a place call at the other is [D15324](D15324.md).
