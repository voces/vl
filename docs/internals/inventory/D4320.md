#### Mechanism

`valueAtomKind` reads a union's render, and the renderer keeps one part when two members spell
alike. `i32[]` and `K[]` both ride the i32 list (kind 7), and two function types both ride the
closure (kind 11), but their spellings differ, so the type alone does not say what the render
reads as: `valueAtomKindOfTy` (`compiler/emit_classify.vl`) still renders this residue — a
union of two or more members sharing kind 7, 9, 11 or 18 — and every other shape is read off
the type (L2 stage 2). The refusal above is the same ambiguity one step on: the box has no tag
that separates two members of one rep.
