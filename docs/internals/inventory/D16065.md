### D16065 — a getter with a constant-range loop is refused, where the owner's ruling admits it within the step budget

**closed 2026-11-14 · runs, prints `8` · `gwForRange` admits a constant-range loop in a getter body and `gcForRange` charges it its trips times its body, each trip at least one step (`constRangeTrips`, `compiler/check_range_var.vl`); `while`, `for … in` and any other range keep the refusal, now worded `a getter may loop only over a constant range`; fixtures `tests/cases/getters/getter-constant-range-loop.vl` and `error-getter-loop-refusals.vl` · was loud check reject · clause 2: `the getter `total` on R contains a loop, and a getter must not loop — make it a method` · the getter half of the owner's ruling (2026-09-22, D-Q2): a `for i in <a> until|to <b> [step <s>]` over literal or `const` ends, its variable never written, is bounded, its body's cost multiplied by its trip count, within the 16-step budget · split from [D2065](D2065.md), whose loop-variable half runs · filed 2026-11-14 against master `cdc902ef0`**

#### Mechanism

The getter cost walk in `compiler/check_program.vl` answers every `ForRange` with
`esUnbound(r, "it loops (`for` …)")`, as it does `while` and `for … in`. The ruling
(design-review-getters-effects-2026-09.md §D-Q2, property-access-design.md §D3a-contract,
function-effects-design.md §C1) keeps `while`, `for … in` and a data-bounded range unbounded,
and makes a constant range cost its trip count times its body. The steps metric it multiplies
into is built (D2061 to D2063).

Each trip costs at least one step, so a loop whose body calls nothing still counts against the budget: `0 until 17` costs 17 and is refused. The ruling says only that a loop multiplies. The floor keeps a long arithmetic loop from passing at zero cost. The cost trail repeats the body's calls for up to 64 trips, so the over-budget sentence counts them per trip.
