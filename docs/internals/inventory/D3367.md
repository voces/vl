### D3367 — a join of two un-annotated parameters pinned at lists the direct spelling refuses to join builds invalid wasm

Want:

#### Mechanism

The direct spelling (`a0: i32[], a1: (i32 | null)[]`) refuses the join by design: the two lists
store their elements differently, so the join would be a copy. A hole is gradual (D3303), so
the refusal is owed at the call, where the pins make the join; the call's re-check of the body
does not ask the join, and the instance builds it as one list. Before lane UG the two holes
collapsed to the first (D3333) and the instance refused loudly; parting them reached the
`<T>` spelling's own invalid wasm.
