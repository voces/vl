#### Mechanism

The global section declares `c` `(mut i32)`, since its initializer yields the atom
id, and the claimed `ret` returns that cell as the `string` its type names with no decode. The armed
shadow reports `fail a claimable function did not match: bytes: prefix or locals` in `ret`. D17260
is the same cell read by the module's `start`.
