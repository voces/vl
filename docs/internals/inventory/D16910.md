#### Mechanism

The arena sig walk (`collectTyReachCloSigsArm`) skips a closure type whose result
`funcRetUnrepresentable` names unless `cloResultMaybeMixed` says the result may be a mixed
scalar-plus-composite union. `unionArmsMixed`, behind it, left a nested union member out of both
sides, so `K0 | {w: i32}` read as not mixed while `funcRetUnrepresentable` (which counts a literal
union as scalar) read it as unrepresentable: the walk interned nothing and the call kept its floor.
