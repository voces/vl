#### Mechanism

The literal's recorded type is the join of its elements, `{z: {f: {a: i64} | {a: {a: boolean}}}}[]`,
not the annotation's: its union of records is spelled nowhere, so `collectU` registered no row for it
and the nested record holding it had no element row. Mechanism and fix are [D16948](D16948.md)'s.
