### D16672 — a radix `^` whose 32-bit result is zero reads as its exact value, `(-1) ^ 0xFFFFFFFF` printing `-4294967296`

**check-clean silently wrong · clause 1 · open · `vl check` rc 0 · ruling A (a radix literal is a bit pattern at its use's width, under every operator): at `i32` the pattern `0xFFFFFFFF` is `-1`, and `-1 ^ -1` is `0` · every `^` whose 32-bit result is not zero reads its pattern, and `&`, `|` and `+` over the same operands do too · the `i32` destination face is a loud check reject: `constant -4294967296 overflows i32` · found by the independent oracle's arithmetic generator (lane T's cells `ar_s13_0110`, `ar_s13_0136`) · filed 2026-11-15 against master `f195dacbc`**

Repro:

    print((-1) ^ 0xFFFFFFFF)
    print(0xFFFFFFFF ^ (-1))
    // master: vl check rc 0, prints -4294967296 twice

Want:

    0
    0

#### Mechanism

Not ablated to a compiler function. The constant fold computes `^` exactly
(`-1 ^ 4294967295 = -4294967296`), and the exact result is used when its low 32 bits are zero.
Otherwise it is read at 32 bits. The exact value is outside `i32`, so the tree is typed `i64`
and widens its typed peers: `v2 * ((-1) ^ 0xFFFFFFFF)` with `v2: i32 = 7` prints
`-30064771072`, not `0`.
