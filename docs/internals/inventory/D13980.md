#### Mechanism

The union-arm layer gave a `null[]` member no atom kind: `unMemAtomKind` fell through on a
`null` element, `unMemIsRefElemArray` called it a ref-element list with no reflist slot, so
`isArmTagOfTy` had no tag. Most positions refused loudly (`` `is` names a declared union member
with no interned arm representation ``); a delivery into the union boxed the `null[]` value as
the other member's list. The `null[]` member is the ingredient: the same union with `string[]`
in its place runs, and the other member's element does not matter.
