### D14820 — a field or element assignment used as a value is refused when its place is wider than its right side

**closed 2026-11-01 · runs, prints `-2` · was loud emit reject · clause 2 · `asgReadUnwidenOp` (`emit_classify.vl`) takes a field's or element's own checked type as the place's storage where the checker records none, and `assignTargetReReads` re-reads such a place and narrows it back exactly as a name's · pin: `tests/cases/expressions/field-element-assignment-value.vl` · found grading the lifting census's tail-assignment case · filed 2026-11-01 against master `0a983e646`**

#### Mechanism

An assignment yields its right side, at the right side's type (DECISIONS §"Assignment is an
expression yielding its right-hand side"; D2398, D3733). The emitter lowers a value assignment by
storing and re-reading the place. A name wider than its right side is re-read and narrowed back
(`asgReadUnwidenOp`, off the storage type the checker records for a name). A field or element
place was re-read only where its type equalled the value's, since the checker records no storage
type for it, so a wider one fell through to the refusal. Its lambda-tail spelling already ran,
through the tail spill, and printed `-2`.

whose re-read would evaluate `i` twice, and a nested receiver (`w.p.v = v`).
