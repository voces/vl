#### Mechanism

Two refusals, two mechanisms, one union. The operator half: `softenLitTy` declined a set over
two bases, so `x + 1` met the operand as `D`. Arithmetic, ordering and unary `-` now soften
through `softenLitOpTy`, which takes an integer-literal union over two bases to `i64` (the
ruling) and sets `numUnionMinted`; the pin mirror `binOpDefinedFor` asks the same. The
emitter's numeric-union dispatch already reached the box's members through `njLitBasesOf`; a
literal side now computes every pair at `i64` (`njPairSideTy`), so the narrow member widens
before the operator, and `-x` converts out at `i64` and subtracts. The inferred-return half:
`valueUnionRetName` had no rung for a union of literals over two bases, so nothing banked the
box every annotated position lowers `D` as. `litUnionArmValueUnionRetName` now banks the
rendered box name when every leaf is a literal or a plain scalar over two bases or more
(`litScalarBoxLeaves`), which also takes `1 | "s"` and the `??` join `9000000000 | 1 | i32`.
`x += 1` over `D` stays refused: an `i64` sum is not a `D`.
