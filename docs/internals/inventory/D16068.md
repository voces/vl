#### Mechanism

`(i32 | null) | null` is `i32 | null`: `mkNullableTy` folds a nullable inner, and `tySame`,
assignability, printing, `==` and `is` all read the two as one type. Re-grading those surfaces
found no other place that treats them apart. Two separately written `W<i32 | null>` annotations
build two instances that interconvert. Only the alias memo compared indices, and only a recursive
alias re-applies inside its own expansion. There, the second instance became another level, until
D12131's 32-deep backstop refused it.
