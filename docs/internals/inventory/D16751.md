#### Mechanism

The template (D3003/D3004) was written when `same.v` read back at the literal's own `i32`: the
map store re-seated the literal at `D`, and the read narrowed from the wider slot. The checker now
types `same.v` as `f64`, so a delivery at `i32` is refused at every position and the template
measures nothing. It is not the tail-expression rot #8509 repairs. If the alias reading `f64` is
the design (the literal adopted `D`), the template's `TYPE` and `WANT` should move to `f64`; if
not, the read is a regression. The owner decides with D3003's question.
