#### Mechanism

Not ablated to a line. The call `f(z)` passes `z`'s open hole, and the arm-against-arm judge
(`holeLetWritesDisagreeAt`, check_relate_core.vl) defers only the list-element and field reads
at an open type. Nothing judges the arms again at `bad`'s pin, `string`.
