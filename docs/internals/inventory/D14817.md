Want:

#### Mechanism

One function body builds one record row (D8301). `mkcall` is delivered at `Circle` by `sink` and
at its own `K` by `both@K`, so its row cannot serve both, and the delivery to `sink` is refused
([D14814](D14814.md)). The design permits the program. The planned capability is a converting
delivery: build at the wider row, and copy into the narrower record where a fresh call result is
delivered directly at its own type. No alias can exist between the call and the copy. A value
stored first and delivered again is not fresh, so it stays refused.
