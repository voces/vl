### D1722 — a NESTED index place keys nothing in the emitter while the checker narrows it, so `ys[0][0].r` is check-clean invalid wasm

**closed 2026-09-06 · the keyer alone is NOT the fix, and the row's predicted trap is
measured: with it and no prefix retire, a write to `ys[0]` leaves the inner narrowing live
and the program traps `cast failure` · 0 corpus cells**

Repro:

#### Mechanism

The checker's `placeKeyOf` recurses through its own result, so `ys[0][0]` narrows; the
emitter's `indexPathKeyOf` took an `Ident` or a `Member` receiver only, so one subscript
level keyed and two keyed "". It recurses through its own answer now, exactly as its twin does.

**The write side is the other half and it is load-bearing, not defensive.** `writeRetiresNarrowing`
retires by PREFIX in both separators and does not consult the written type; the emitter
retired the exact key only, on the invariant that no nested key exists. `keyIsBelow` is the
emitter's copy of that rule, and the Ident, Member and Index write arms all apply it.
