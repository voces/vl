### D16892 — an un-annotated `Map()` read in place, not bound, keeps its open key and value, and emit has no map row for it

**loud emit reject · clause 2 · `print(Map().get("a") ?? 7)`: `` emitProgram: `??` over this nullable value is not supported yet ``; `print(Map().get("a"))`: `emitProgram: index receiver is not an array or string`; `print(Map().has("a"))`: `emitProgram: callee is not a function name` · the residue of [D16725](D16725.md) (`take([Map()])[0].get("a") ?? 7`) · filed 2026-11-16 against master `bc6546163` by lane AE4, from lane HASH's probe**

Repro:

    print(Map().get("a") ?? 7)

Want:

    7

#### Mechanism

`checkBuiltinIdentCall` (`compiler/check_calls.vl`) types an un-annotated `Map()` as
`mkMapTy(TY_OPEN, TY_OPEN, 0)`: the shared open placeholder, not holes of its own. A binding
(`const m = Map()`) gets holes the program-end flush can default, and a list literal holding one
is defaulted by [D16879](D16879.md)'s pending list, but a `Map()` read in place, or read back
out of such a list, is left open, so emit finds no map row for its receiver. Not traced further.
