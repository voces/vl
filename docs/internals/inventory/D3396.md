### D3396 — an `else if` link whose arms all exit is typed `void`, so a binding of the chain cannot be filled

**closed 2026-11-15 · runs, prints `7` · was a loud check reject, clause 2, `cannot assign {x: i32} to void | null` (later `this arm yields no value`) · closed by leaving an `else if` link whose every arm exits out of the chain's join, as an exiting `else` block is, with each of its arms given the `never` tail (`ifTailElseJoin`, `noteExitLinkArms`) · fixture `tests/cases/soundness/else-if-link-exits-leaves-the-join-runs.vl` · most of the #3315 review's `agrid.py` refusals · filed 2026-09-30 against master `8df60e472`**

#### Mechanism

Localised at the close: `ifTailElseJoin` dropped an exiting `else` arm from the join only when
it was a block (`elseBlk`), so a diverging link joined its `void`. The join leaves out an arm that
always exits (D3222), but an `else if` link both of
whose arms exit is itself typed `void`, and the outer join then takes `void` as an arm's value
rather than leaving the link out. The binding is `void | null`, and the fill is refused. The
same chain written as a nested `else { if … }` is left out and runs.
