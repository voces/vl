#### Mechanism

`recBuiltAtDeclared` skipped every argument of a call whose callee is a member, treating it as
a pinned generic argument. The argument of a list's `push` or a map's `set` takes its type from
the receiver, not from itself, so the refusal D8020 gives at a binding was skipped there, and
the call was accepted with the instance taken at `Col`. Those two arguments are now exempt from
the skip (`recAtStoreArg`).
