#### Mechanism

Not traced. `A<n>`'s structural spelling doubles in length per level, so a pass that renders the
alias's function type, rather than reading its arena row, pays for the whole spelling. Nothing
needs to be built or called: the declaration alone costs it. The inline spelling at a field is
cheap, and past the spelling cap of [D9784](D9784.md) the inline list `((x: i32) => A16)[]` is
cheaper at depth 16 (1.03G) than at 12 (2.35G), which suggests the capped arena route is linear.
