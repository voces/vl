### D17122 — a string chain's assignment part was stored ahead of the parts to its left under an IR claim

**closed 2026-11-24, a duplicate of [D17109](D17109.md), closed by #9866 · runs, prints `cdd` · was check-clean silently wrong, clause 1, shipped by an IR claim (claimless printed `cdd`) · pins: `tests/cases/assignment/value-after-a-lead-string.vl` · found by IR-N7 writing a `narrowed read` witness · filed 2026-11-24 against master `1784a86d`**

Repro:

    function loc() {
      let t = "c"
      print(t + (t = "dd"))
    }

    cdd

#### Mechanism

`buildStrBin` built each part in turn, and an assignment part's `assignTo` placed its store in
the block ahead of the concat's operands, so the parts to its left read the stored value.
`irBStageLeads` guarded a valued `if`, a `??` box and a literal stash, not a store. #9866's
`storeFrom` declines it, as for [D17109](D17109.md)'s call arguments.
