**closed 2026-10-16 — now prints `1 3` · was loud emit reject, clause 2 · a list union over a literal's `#anon` row names that member by its shape (`monoListUnionRowName`), and the `is T[]` the checker lowers a `.length` into banks the list of the shape as its check type (`monoAnonRowListTy`) · the complement `else` read of a generic `T[] | i32[]` is D5111, the un-annotated `Map()` value position D5113 · filed 2026-10-16 against master `502f8daa8` · pin: `tests/cases/unions/inferred-record-literal-list-union-arm-generic.vl` · matrix: `scripts/capability-probes/matrix/inferred-generic-record-literal-list-union.matrix.vl`**

#### Mechanism

The monomorphizer names the pinned instance's return union from the instance's row names
(`monoRetListRowName` → `monoRetRowName`, `compiler/emit_mono.vl`), so it registers
`#anon0[] | i32[]`. D5049's close makes the non-generic union keep a literal's record arm by
its shape; this registration bypasses that rendering, and the returned list finds no arm.
`emit_mono.vl` belongs to lane L1, so the fix is left to it.
