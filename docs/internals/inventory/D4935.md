#### Mechanism

The arm's empty `K[]` was a shadow local that `emitIdentNode` redirects to in the narrowing
function only, and a closure reads its capture from its own environment. `cloLiftDecide`
(emit_lift.vl) now keeps such a closure unlifted, and `listFitCaptureStandIn` (wasmEmit.vl)
builds an empty `K[]` for its read. A read typed at the type parameter's list takes `K[]` from
the enclosing `if <name> is K[]` when the read is delivered whole, and a widening copy after it
converts from `K` rather than from the capture's own list.

#### Grading list

A closure in the arm that iterates the capture or is stored in a list of closures: both in
`tests/cases/generics/is-list-arm-read-by-closure.vl`. One that passes the capture to an `f64[]`
parameter is refused by the checker ([D4963](D4963.md)). On the D4911
branch, an arm holding both a whole-list delivery and a list of closures was check-clean
invalid wasm inside the lambda.
