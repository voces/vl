#### Mechanism

A narrowing to one atom unboxes at the read; a narrowing to two atoms keeps the box, which is
right for every pair but the two whose `A | null` has a rep of its own: `boolean | null` (an
`i32`, 2 for null) and `string | null` (a nullable string ref). The checker types the read at
that pair, so a binding, a parameter, a return, a field or a list typed at it wants the niche
and got the box. The close converts at the read, as the one-atom unbox does, wherever the
emitter's narrowing is that pair and the checker typed the read node at it (`nicheReadKindAt`):
a bare name, a field or a cell is read with the narrowing lifted, which delivers the box, parked
in the literal-`is` stash, and its null tag becomes the niche's null and any other tag the
unboxed payload (`emitNarrowedNicheRead`). `exprUnion`, `unionNameOfExpr` and the map-read
binding rung stop calling such a read a box, while a test on the name still narrows its
storage (`identHoldsUnionBox`). A hole parameter pinned by such an argument pins at the niche
(`monoNicheNarrowsFrom`), since that is what the call site delivers.
