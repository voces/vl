#### Mechanism

`u == null` over a hole records a deferred binary constraint. A call to a generic copies its
callee's constraints into the caller under the call's substitution (`validateBinCstrs`), and
`substTyDeep` mints a fresh arena type for each copy. `noteBinCstr` deduplicated by arena index,
so every copy was new: with every generic calling every other, each body's list held copies of
copies, and a 16-generic check spent 97.7% of its samples in `noteBinCstr`'s own scan. It now
compares by structure with each inference hole equal only to itself (`tyEqSameHoles`), which is
exactly when two constraints of one body substitute alike.
