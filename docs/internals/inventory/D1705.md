#### Mechanism

`elemRowsCaptureCompatAt` walks one nesting level or one container per step and decrements a
counter, because a self-recursive element type would otherwise walk its own rows forever.
Running out is LENIENT — the same answer an unresolvable row gets — so past the bound the
comparator stops refuting and the declared row captures the literal again, reading its box at
the other row's positional tags.

**The counter is proven to be the mechanism by an A/B, twice.** At 8 the wall stood at 9; at
24 it stood at 24; it ships at 64 and stands at 64. Each raise moves exactly the cells past the
old wall and costs nothing measurable — 0 of 3,057 `tests/cases` modules and one byte of seed
between 24 and 64.

**What removes it is a visited-pair guard, and the naive form of that was tried and refused
first.** A `(row, row)` memo whose "computing" marker is the cycle guard made the ladder clean
to 40 — and then the compiler could not compile ITSELF: `vl build compiler/entry.vl` under that
candidate trapped with `allocation size too large`, because the memo was a LINEAR-SCANNED table
of row pairs and on a program with thousands of rows it is quadratic in entries.

The close is the same guard as an INDEX: an open-addressed table keyed on the pair, sized once
per emit pass from `sScanLim()`, and generation-stamped so a new walk costs no clearing — a slot
is live only while its stamp equals the walk id. A pair the table cannot hold answers leniently,
which is a named default and not a silent one. `elemRowsCaptureWalk` is a `pass-stamped` row of
the memo-generation census, so the key is `emitPassGen` and no answer outlives its pass.

**The L2 self-compile is the control that separated the two**, and it is the only instrument
that did: the scanned version passed every one of the 3,059 `tests/cases` modules and failed
only there.
