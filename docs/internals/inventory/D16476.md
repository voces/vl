#### Mechanism

The first pass narrows `g` in the right operand of `g is string[] && …`, as it does for any `is`.
The deep `is` rewrite ([D1190](D1190.md), `jwRewriteSite`) routes the test through the generated
predicate and binds the converted copy only in the arm block the test guards (`jwArmBlockOf`). The
right operand of the `&&` is no arm, so its `g` is left as the raw place, and the re-check reads it
at the declared `Json`.
