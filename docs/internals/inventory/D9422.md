#### Mechanism

element through `arrLitUnionElemName`, reached from `collectAArrayLit` by `arrLitIsRef`,
`arrLitElemName`, `arrLitElemHintTy` and `arrLitElemKind`, over the checker's
`tyToStrObj` render. The parameter's signature token renders `(A | string)[]` and
`annParamKindTy` classifies it (`scalarListRepOfName` → `arrElemIs` → `nameIsArray` →
`unionMemberCount`, and `annRefListSlot` → `nameIsRefArray` → `refArrShapeKind`). D9390's rung
does not see a list. Profile of depth 12 under a `--names` seed.
