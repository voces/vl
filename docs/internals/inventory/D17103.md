#### Mechanism

Legacy's own lowering, under claims and claimless alike (`ir-identity --claimless`: fail under
both). `__mv` is a top-level `{f: null, g: i32} | null` global read narrowed under
`if __mv != null`. With `dA: J` and `J` inside a union parameter (`su`), the narrowed read is
written as the nullable ref while the binding `v` is declared at the non-null struct ref, and no
`ref.as_non_null` is written between them. The same program reading `__mv.g` straight into a
string concat or interpolation instead fails the emit ("field access receiver is not a struct"),
the second face of one mechanism by the ablation below. Found by IR-M1's Q58 position sweep for
heap identity batch 2b part 2; its IR change does not move either face (same bytes, same error).
