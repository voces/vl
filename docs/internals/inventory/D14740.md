#### Mechanism

The direct call `s(4)` tags `s`'s set, so `lapBack`'s split copies (D6210) are handed its id,
while the set's leaves sit in `go`'s body, which is instanced per binding of the hole `c`, and a
set with a leaf in a generic or hole function's body is not tagged per instance (step 4's
blocker 3, DECISIONS.md "A choice of names at a split parameter is copied, not tagged"). One of
the copies is emitted for a closure and receives the id. Noting the copies' result-position facts
for such a binding (as D14737 does for a module-scope one) makes the loud spellings below run, and
moves `generics/hole-fn-choice-nested-positions.vl` from runs to invalid wasm, where one `both`
body is reached by the same set tagged through a binding and untagged through a choice.
