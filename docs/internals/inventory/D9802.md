### D9802 — `*` mixing the members of a `??` joined `i64 | f64` in an un-annotated function is refused by the emitter, not the checker

**loud emit reject · clause 2 · DESIGN refusal at the wrong stage: the same program at top level is the checker's "mixes the members i64 and f64" · found closing [D9701](D9701.md) · present on master `d459cac18` · filed 2026-10-22 against master `d459cac18`**

Repro:

    const jq: i64 | null = null
    function take(a) {
      const r2 = a ?? 0.5
      print(r2 * 2.0)
    }
    take(jq)
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: emitProgram: a numeric union member pair has no operator type

#### Mechanism

The hole-parameter face of [D3613](D3613.md). At top level `r2` is `i64 | f64` and the checker
refuses `*` against an `f64` under the numeric-join ruling's pair rule. In the hole body `r2`
is the `??` hole, the template admits the operator, and no instance-time check re-asks the pair
rule, so `emitNumUnionBin` is the first to see the `i64`-`f64` pair. The refusal is the
design's; the checker owes it at the instance, with the `as` fix it gives at top level.
