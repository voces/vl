### D3091 — three hole functions, one with a nested named function, one capturing a local and one handing a lambda to a `<T>` generic, refuse together though each runs alone

**loud emit reject — `emitProgram: a function-value call has no interned signature`, naming an empty result type · clause 2 · open · found writing the lane HL fixtures · the same on master `d408f2da4` · filed 2026-09-28 against master `d408f2da4`**

#### Mechanism

Not localised past the ablation. Each pair of the three functions runs, so the refusal is a
per-program table that one function's lambda fills and another's reads (a "runs alone, fails in
company" shape): the lambda handed to `appG` finds no interned signature for its result.
