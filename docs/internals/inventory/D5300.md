#### Mechanism

Not ablated past the table below. D5243's close desugars `r?.f ?? d` over a union-box
receiver into a null test and a narrowed read, when `f` is never null; a bare `r?.f`, or
one whose field can be null, is not desugared, and the `?.` lowering accepts only a struct
chain as its receiver.
