**closed · `identBoundAt` asks the binding in scope at the read (and the function's parameters) where `identBoundInFrame` asked for any `let` of the name, and the global lookups carry the read's node · fixture `tests/cases/inference/global-shadowed-by-dead-local.vl` · was check-clean silently wrong (and check-clean invalid wasm at a ref-typed global) · clause 1 · from the #3292 review · filed and closed 2026-09-29 against master `e70102886`**

#### Mechanism

`globalLetOfSidIn` refuses a global the frame binds, and `identBoundInFrame` decided that by
name: in a scope-less pass its arena rung was `frameLetOfLive`, i.e. any `let` of the name in
the body. So the dead-scoped `const s` hid the global `s`, and the classifiers then found that
`let` (`boundBoolByName`, `frameLetOf`) and typed the read `boolean`. `identBoundAt` now takes
the read's node: a `let` binds the name there only when it covers the read. The node is
threaded through `globalLetOfSidIn`, `globalLetOfIn` and the twenty `global*Sid` classifiers;
a parameter also binds it, which the scope-less rung had not asked.
