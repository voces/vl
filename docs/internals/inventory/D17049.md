#### Mechanism

`compiler/ir_verify.vl` checks a lowered function against facts it asks legacy for, instead of
facts the builder recorded on the IR. At I8, with legacy codegen deleted, those queries have
nothing to answer them. The pattern to retire each: the builder records the fact on the IR node,
variable or global as it builds, as `irGlobalNote`/`irGlobalRepOf` does for a global's rep
(#9550). The verifier then checks against that record, and the import goes. The population, from
ir_verify's imports at `5229077b`. The IR rep vocabulary (`emit_rep`'s `R*` nodes,
`repNodeOfTy`), the AST, `symbols` and `check_types` are shared, not legacy queries, and are not
listed.
