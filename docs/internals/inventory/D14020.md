### D14020 — an un-annotated list literal of two record shapes is built at rows its type does not name

**closed 2026-10-26 — runs, prints `1` · was check-clean invalid wasm · closed with [D14220](D14220.md), the same defect: a literal of a union of records registers its union and builds at its box · its grading list's `lv1[0].r is Cir2` at module scope is [D14221](D14221.md), a separate loud emit reject · found by step 5b slice 1 (#4853) · filed against master `935da52aa` · pin: `tests/cases/unions/inferred-record-union-list-distinct-fields.vl`**

Repro:

    const l = [{ c2: 1 }, { s2: 1 }]
    print(1)

Want:

    1

#### Mechanism

The checker types the literal as a list of the union of two anonymous records, and the emitter
does not build at that type: the element rows come from the emitter's own row resolver, which
works from the elements' shapes. That resolver also adopts a declared row whose shape matches,
which is the six-cell family that slice 1 of step 5b leaves on the threaded pair
(`arrKeepsThread` family 4, the `d361e0031xx` cells). The ruling covers both: the literal takes
its anonymous row type from the checker, the emitter builds at that type, and no declared row is
adopted by shape. Not localised past that.
