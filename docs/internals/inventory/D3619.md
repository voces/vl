**check-clean silently wrong · open · clause 1 · the same on master `0c087fb3c` · found by the #3368 review · filed 2026-10-04 against master `0c087fb3c`**

#### Mechanism

Not localised. `v` is `null`, so `v ?? 9` is the `i32` `9`. The `is` reads the `??` result at
the template's join (`A | i32`) and answers from the pinned `string` member rather than from the
arm the value took. `print(v ?? 9)` prints `9`. Binding the result first,
`const w = v ?? 9`, is invalid wasm (`expected (ref $type), found i32`).
