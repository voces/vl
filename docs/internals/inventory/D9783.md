#### Mechanism

Not traced past the floor. The call `v()` reaches `cloCallUnionResultUnrepresentable`
(wasmEmit.vl), which refuses a function-value call whose result is a value union with a
composite arm when the value's signature key comes from somewhere other than a direct binding
or annotation. A list of functions or a list of maps is such an arm; a list of `i32` is not.
The fixtures `closures/error-nullable-closure-{array-scalar,litunion-2d-array,map-array}-union-result.vl`
reach the same floor through a nullable function-typed parameter.
