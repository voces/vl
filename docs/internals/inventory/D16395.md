### D16395 — `==` over two records of different field sets passes check and is refused at emit, or builds an invalid module when a side is nullable

**closed 2026-11-15 · now a loud check reject (`` `{f: i32, g: i32}` and `{f: i32}` have different fields (`g`); compare the fields you mean ``), as the owner ruled 2026-11-15 · was loud emit reject (check-clean invalid wasm for nullable records), clause 1 (`vl check` rc 0) · bucket (e) · fixed in `recEqFieldsAt` (`check_operators.vl`), beside D16394's field-wise compare · fixture `tests/cases/structs/error-eq-different-field-sets.vl` · found grading D16394's close · filed 2026-11-15 against master `33ef972d7`**

Repro:

    const a: {f: i32, g: i32} = {f: 1, g: 2}
    const b: {f: i32} = a
    print(a == b)
    // vl check: `{f: i32, g: i32}` and `{f: i32}` have different fields (`g`); compare the fields you mean

#### Mechanism

A record with more fields is assignable to one with fewer, so the checker's `==` accepted the
pair, and the emitter's struct-equality lowering, which compares one row, refused two; a nullable
side skipped the refusal and built a module that failed validation. The owner ruled (2026-11-15)
that `==` between records of different field sets is a check error naming the fields one side
lacks. A union compared with one of its members is unaffected: it tests the arm, and another arm
is unequal, whatever fields the arms share.
