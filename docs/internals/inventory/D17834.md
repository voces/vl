#### Mechanism

`tvElseBase[copy] = cur` and `tvElseTested[copy]` on the copy's index; the copy's identity is the pair
(`cur`, tested type). A mint keyed on structure returns `cur` itself, so `tvElseBase[cur] = cur`, and
`substTyDeep`'s else-base arm (`check_relate_core.vl`) redirects to itself without end, under
`monoSubstLetType`. Ablation on S2a: interning off for lists, nullables or maps still hangs; unions
off, or unions of one member only, runs; the copy minted fresh (`addTy`) runs. The same shape marks
other unions by row identity right after their mint: `tpJoinUnions`, `tpParamLitJoins`,
`joinHoleUnions`, `apartHoleJoins` and `noteHoleTpJoin`. The close keys each on what it means (the
pair, the join mark) rather than on a row that structure may share.
