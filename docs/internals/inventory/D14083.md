#### Mechanism

Every join site folds its values pairwise through `keepListStorage`, which keeps two lists
apart when the join would store a side's list differently (D4862). Its veto asked
`listJoinStorageDiffers(list, join)`, and that answers false for any union join. So
`f64[] ⊔ i32[]` stayed apart, but `(f64[] | string[]) ⊔ i32[]` was folded by `joinTys`'s
`assignable` into the `f64[]` member. The arm was then delivered into that member as an empty
list. The fold depended on order and arm count. The `if` chain joins right to left and dropped
the list at other orders: `i32[]`, `f64[]`, `K[]` dropped the `i32[]`.

The veto now flattens a union join and keeps a list apart unless some member it fits stores it
the same way. `ljwCopiedLevelCheck` shares it, so a spread into a union element refuses as
it does into a single list element.

Grid: permutations of `f64[]`, `i32[]`, `string[]` and `K[]` at 2, 3 and 4 arms, in `match`
and `if` chains, un-annotated. On master 48 of 120 join types dropped a member (24 `match`,
24 `if`). Now 0 of 120 do.
