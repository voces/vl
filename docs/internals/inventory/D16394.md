#### Mechanism

The emitter's struct-equality lowering compares two values of ONE row. Two records with one field
set whose fields are stored apart (an atom beside a string, two number widths, a boxed nullable
beside a plain field, lists of another element, a nested record of another row) are accepted by
the checker, since each field pair is comparable, and reach that lowering with two rows: it
refuses, and a nullable record side skips the refusal and builds a module that fails validation.
The close keeps the one lowering: `checkEqualityBinNode` (`check_operators.vl`) rewrites the
comparison into the field pairs' own `==`, so a nested pair is rewritten in turn.
