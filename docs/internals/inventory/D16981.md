### D16981 — a union of nested lists that spell one list, written literal-set member first, is stored at that member while its reads expect the other

**loud emit reject · clause 2 · found closing [D16969](D16969.md) · filed 2026-11-18 against master `f87150f71` by lane F1**

Repro:

    type K = 1 | 2

    print(q.length)
    // vl run: emitProgram: `is` over a list tests its elements, and this list's rep is not the one its type names

#### Mechanism

The same union written `i32[][] | K[][]` runs (D16969). Canon records the annotation at its first
member, here `K[][]`, so the binding is built at the `K[]` row while a read of it is fitted to the
`i32[][]` the union renders as; the list-fit check (`LIST_FIT_REP_MSG`) refuses the mismatch. On
master the declaration itself was refused (`only i32[] arrays and struct/union element arrays`).
