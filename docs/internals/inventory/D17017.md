#### Mechanism

The tag decision stores the list as ids and mono pins `xs` at the id list, but the elaboration
never keys `pick` at the set: its `V | null` result stays at the hole function, which has no box
(`atomBoxTail`). [D17012](D17012.md) is the same helper unkeyed at the set, met earlier: returning
`V` over members of different results, the check judges the call as a choice. One family; re-graded
on master `afb9ab3eb`, both rows and every ablation below hold, and the helper returning `V` over
one member runs.
