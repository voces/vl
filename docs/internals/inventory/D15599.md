#### Mechanism

The two backends did not disagree; the instances did. `vl run` compiles once per process, and
the cases oracle compiles every case on one instance. `canonEmitTypeNames` fills
`collectGenAliasFieldAnns`' bank (each generic's parameter annotations as declared, by node)
after the check. The check reads the bank at its end, through `tyParamOnlyBareParams`. So on a
fresh instance the check finds the bank empty. On a shared one it finds the previous program's
entries. Here node 39, `G`'s annotation, still held `aliased<T>`'s banked `T` tree, which spells
`F`. `F` then read as named by another annotation. `tyParamBareOnly` dropped it, the call
missed the own-parameter pin, and mono's floor refused `same`. A preceding case with no generic
annotation at that node changes nothing, which is why the case passed alone and under `--filter`.
