#### Mechanism

The `??` arm adopted the `const` default by pinning it at the read's record J, so the re-check
typed the default J and `m.get("z") ?? d` joined as the union `{h: {f: i32}} | J`. The flat
spelling survives that union because emit reads a field both arms share; at depth two the
receiver `b.h` has no struct row. A default that also reads its own nested field at its own type
was built rather than pinned, joined as one record, and ran. The call spelling (`const d = mk2()`)
behaves the same and stays adopted, since its function's other calls share its build: D15314.
