### D3729 — a captured `let` keeps its heap cell when a call to one of its capturers sits in the arguments of another

**runs today and must keep running · open · a cost row, no wrong value: the witness prints its Want, and the cell is the cost · residue of [D3664](D3664.md) · filed by lane L2 against master `687b42dbe`**

Repro:

      let k = 0

        k += d
        k * 2

#### Mechanism

A lifted call pushes its captures before its arguments, so a capture passed by value would be
read before an argument that changes it runs, where a cell is read after. `cuArgsScan` therefore
keeps the cell when an argument of a call to a capturer calls a capturer, assigns the binding,
or spreads. Pushing a lifted function's captures after its declared parameters (or spilling the
arguments first) would admit these.
