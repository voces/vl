#### Mechanism

[D2387](D2387.md)'s, with a member whose rep is not i32. The boxed binding's read is a
synthetic `cell[0]` with no recorded type; the read unboxes the f64 payload correctly, but
`exprIsF64` had no answer for a narrowed union-element `Index` and fell through to i32, so the
interpolation re-boxed the f64 into the i32 payload struct (`struct.new $vbI32` over an `f64`).
An i64 member failed the same way, and a list member (`f64[] | string`) was a loud emit reject
at `.length` or `v[i]`. The classifiers now ask `indexUnionReadKind`.
