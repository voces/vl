#### Mechanism

**Measured at `captureValKind`, the function that types the env field, with the annotated
capture beside it.** The two readings:

    un-annotated  binding=14 letType=-1 frame=32 letInitCellKind=[i32]   letIsUnion=0
    annotated     binding=15 letType=10 frame=33 letInitCellKind=[union] letIsUnion=1 annKind=[union]

so the env field is an `i32` holding a box ref, `capturedIsUnionBox` is false, and `emitIs`'s
value-atom arm — gated on `identSlotIsUnionBox` — declines and falls to the refusal.

**The obvious rung does not fire, and the reason is the frame.** `letPopUnionBoxElem` resolves
the receiver's ref-list slot through the AMBIENT locals, and this ladder types a capture while
the CAPTURING frame's locals are loaded. Recorded per call, the same question flips inside one
pass:

    pass=35 letInitCellKind=[i32]   letPopUnionBoxElem=0 exprRefArrayFrame=0 slotFrame=-1
    pass=35 letInitCellKind=[union] letPopUnionBoxElem=1 exprRefArrayFrame=1 slotFrame=0 elemKind=2
    pass=35 letInitCellKind=[i32]   letPopUnionBoxElem=0 exprRefArrayFrame=0 slotFrame=-1

The arm has to be FRAME-BLIND, like `letInitIsLitAtom` and `initIsNestedStructFieldRead` beside
it: the binding's own recorded type is the same answer and needs no frame.

**And the predicate that reads it must be the ROW twin.** `isValueUnionName` answers on a
SPELLING, so it declines the alias — `unRow=0 unName=[V] isValueUnionName=0` even with the
arena row in hand, which is [D1882](D1882.md)'s lesson one layer on. `isValueUnionTyOr` is its
documented twin over a row and answers 1. The inner is required to be a real `TyUnion`, which
keeps `i32 | null` — whose inner is a `TyPrim` — out of the arm entirely.
