#### Mechanism

#3579 (`4389270c7`, Q4 (c), D4056) let record arms that share a field name form a union, and
answers `(A | B).f` with `sharedUnionFieldPlan` (emit_classify.vl). Eight sites ask it, six of
them classifiers in emit_classify.vl (struct index, array-ness, union-ness, the field code),
so one member read rebuilds the plan several times. Each build starts with `exprUnion` and the receiver's
narrowed member set (`currentStructNarrowSetAt`, or `unionNameOfExpr`), splits the set into
atoms and looks every atom's variant and field up by name. Nothing memoises it per node. The
self-compile went from 68.62G to 71.26G fuel (+3.86%) for 887 source lines; in a guest
profile the function went from 1.92% to 3.69% inclusive, and `letUnionNameOf`,
`unionNameOfExpr` and `refListSlotOfExpr` rose under it. On `7ce930c32` it is 4.79% inclusive.
