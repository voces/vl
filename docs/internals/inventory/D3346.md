#### Mechanism

The guess filed here, that `boolean[]` and `i32[]` share one list rep, was wrong. `joinTys`
parts two type parameters' lists (`T0[]` beside `T1[]`, D2760), but beside the union that join
made, `T2[]` was assignable to it inside the body and was dropped: `const z: i32 = r` named the
`match` as `T0[] | T1[]`. The rewrite's `$lu` receiver carried that union, so at the third call
no arm test matched and the chain reached its trap. `tyParamArmAbsorbs` parted a union's member
from a parameter's list only in a return set; it now does everywhere, and the join is
`T0[] | T1[] | T2[]`. Reading the rewrite's minted annotation at the instance's bindings
(D14080) turned this trap into a check reject (`cannot assign string[] | boolean[] | i32[] to
'$lu19' of type string[] | boolean[]`), and that refusal also caught `.length` at the first
call, which ran on master.
