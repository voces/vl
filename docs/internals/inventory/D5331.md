#### Mechanism

D5301's close binds a computed `?.` receiver to a `const` ahead of the statement. It
declines under `&&` and `||` (the right operand may not run), in a `while` condition (it
re-evaluates every iteration) and as the value of a place assignment (`w.f = …`,
`xs[0] = …`, `m["k"] = …`, whose target is evaluated first). Each needs the binding placed
inside the evaluation order rather than before the statement.

| `w.f = mk(false)?.tag` into a `{ f: "a" \| "b" \| null }` field | refuses, `` `?.` receiver is not a struct chain `` | no |
| `const q = mk()` then `w.f = q?.tag` | runs | **yes** |

#### Grading list

The `&&` above; `||`; a `while` condition; a struct-field, list-element and map-index
assignment of the `?.` read.
