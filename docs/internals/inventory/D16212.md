#### Mechanism

`slice` builds its result at the receiver's row, `J`. Its readers took the call's type,
`{f: null}[]`, whose row exists once `ws` interns one. Without `ws` the type had no row of its
own and the readers fell back to the receiver's, so the program ran. `filter` had the same
reader (`mfResultSlotOf`), hidden behind D16134's refusal.
