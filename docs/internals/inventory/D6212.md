### D6212 — a hole parameter of a lambda, called at two types, is not split the way a declared function's is

**closed 2026-10-18 by lane L3 · runs, prints `1 s` · was loud emit reject · clause 2 · the split plan takes a lambda a never-reassigned binding holds as that binding's function, so its hole parameter gets one parameter per type as a declared function's does (`fsFnOwner`) · pins: `tests/cases/functions/hole-lambda-param-called-at-two-types.vl` (`const` and written `let`, an explicit generic, a `const` lambda `id`, a const argument, a lambda local to a function, a `const`-held result, a `const` alias of the lambda as [D6261](D6261.md) takes one) · a reassigned `let` stays declined; the string-concat spellings are ill-typed at the `i32` instance and filed as [D6300](D6300.md) · found closing [D6190](D6190.md) · filed 2026-10-17 against master `3805fcf57`**

#### Mechanism

From the ablation only. The split plan of [D5611](D5611.md) gives a hole parameter called at
two argument types one parameter per type, but only for a declared function. The same body as a
`const` or written `let` lambda keeps one parameter, so `id` has two pins and no single
signature.
