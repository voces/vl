### D4152 — a `string[][]` delivered to `readonly (string | string[])[]` is check-clean invalid wasm

**check-clean invalid wasm · clause 1 · filed 2026-10-11 against master `66f24dbe6` · found by #3563's review (probe `d06`)**

Repro:

    const xs: string[][] = [["a"]]
    const y: readonly (string | string[])[] = xs
    print(y[0] is string[])
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: the emitted module failed to validate: type mismatch: expected (ref null $7), found (ref $5)

#### Mechanism

A readonly destination admits covariance in the checker, but the two lists are different
reps (`string[][]` against `(string | string[])[]`) and no copy or view is built at the delivery.
The fresh-versus-named distinction of the record covariance ruling is the open design point:
either the delivery builds the converting copy or the checker owes the container-widening refusal.
