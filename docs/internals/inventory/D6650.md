#### Mechanism

`__extract_lane_i32x4__` in the then arm is a call, and a call that may write a narrowed place
retires its narrowing ([D2390](D2390.md)), here the outer `m["k"] != null`. The then arm's
retirement still stood when the else arm's complement (`setNarrowFromCondElse`) was pushed, so
the complement started from `v128 | string | null` and reached `print` as the box. The checker
takes the complement from the narrowing at the condition. Retirements are now journaled
(`narrowRetireSlot`), the then arm records where it opened (`narrowThenOpen`), and the else
arm lifts the then arm's retirements below that depth only while its complement is pushed
(`narrowElseLift` / `narrowElseRelift`).
