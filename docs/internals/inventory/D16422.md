### D16422 — a hole parameter whose argument holds a record with an empty list field, written another such record in the body, builds the body's field at its own element

**closed 2026-11-15 — the witness runs and prints `0.5` · was: check-clean invalid wasm · clause 1 · found grading D16365's close (`hole-param-record-empty-list-field.matrix.vl`, cell `index_writes`) · filed 2026-11-15 against master `a058e28f`**

Repro:

    function t(a) {
      a.push({ ys: [] })
      a[0].ys.push(0.5)
      print(a[0].ys[0])
    }
    t([{ ys: [] }])
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: invalid module inside `t`

Want:

    0.5

#### Mechanism

The argument `[{ ys: [] }]` holds the caller's own open list, and the body pushes a record whose
`ys: []` is the body's. At the call only a bare `[]` was a list written at the call, so neither the
argument's list nor the inner one took a pin, and the body's record kept an element of its own.
Now a list literal written at the call, and the open lists inside it, are pin rows. A record the
body's deferred push delivers into such a list's record element shares its open list fields
(`tyLinkRecLists`) or takes the element's concrete one. A binding's list is [D16425](D16425.md).
Fixture: `tests/cases/closures/hole-param-record-pushed-beside-the-call-record.vl`.
