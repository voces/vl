### D1837 — a nullable element's list rep is decided by its BACKING FAMILY, not by the element being a reference: `(string | null)[]` rides the string list and `(boolean | null)[]` the i32 list

**runs today and must keep running · a REFUTATION PIN for the rep-descriptor campaign's phase-3 nullable landing, refused 2026-09-07 against master `6f76e471a` · the over-broad candidate moved 35 modules `runs → not-runs`, six of them compiler traps; the narrow nullable-STRUCT arm is byte-identical and ships · `docs/internals/rep-descriptor-campaign.md` §9.6**

Repro:

    type S = { a: i32 }

    function main() {
      const xs: (S | null)[] = [{ a: 1 }, null]
      const ys: (i32[] | null)[] = [[1], null]
      print(xs.length + ys.length)
    }

    main()

#### Mechanism

`repOfArray` declines a nullable element, so `vtKindOfType`'s ladder owns the answer. The
tempting phase-3 move is to cover every nullable element on the reasoning that a nullable cell
is a reference — a `ref null` niche or a box — for every inner but the two i32 sentinels. The
oracle endorses it: `reflist` LEFT-ONLY falls by exactly its census in each population
(4,088 and 185) with CONTRADICT 0. **Byte identity refuses it: 35 modules `runs → not-runs`,
six of them compiler traps.**

**The rows were never missing, and the first write-up of this row said they were.**
`refArrShapeKindGo` already interns nullable-element rows across the board — `(P | null)[]` and
`(Circle | null)[]` are kind 1, `({[string]: V} | null)[]` is kind 3, `(i32[] | null)[]` is
kinds 4/6/7/8/9/10/11 — and all five shapes run on master today.

**What decides the list is the element's BACKING FAMILY**, which is the contract this row
states: `(string | null)[]` rides the **string list** (the nullable lives inside the element and
the wrapper is `$mkListIdx` either way), `(boolean | null)[]` and `(K | null)[]` ride the **i32
list** at sentinels 2 and -1, and only a nullable **struct**, **map** or **ref-list** element
makes the list a ref list. A one-level variant switch cannot express that, so the broad
candidate could not have been rescued by lengthening its exclusion list. The narrow
nullable-STRUCT arm — flat and tree together, the two-producer audit between them — is
byte-identical on both populations and is what ships.

**And the oracle's CONTRADICT 0 was partly vacuous.** A module that fails to emit never runs its
shadow sweep, so the broken modules produced no rows at all: the programs that would have
contradicted took their counter-evidence with them. That is also the whole of the −627 in AGREE
and the ~5,400 fewer `vtKindOfType` invocations, first attributed to `vtKindOfParam` by
per-site counters. Campaign §5.1 now requires every oracle reading to be reported over the
population that emitted under BOTH seeds, with that count printed.
