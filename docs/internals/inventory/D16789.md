#### Mechanism

`elabCbAtBuild` instances the callback at the receiver's build (`#r.0`) and re-checks its body.
That re-check reports the captured parameter undeclared, while the same program without the build
is accepted. Not yet traced. The suspect is the nested lambda's free-name snapshot (`elabSnapIx`),
which may not declare a parameter its instance binds by a row entry rather than a hole.
