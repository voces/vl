#### Mechanism

Not traced past the ablation; the likely shape is D3399's. The inner `??` is a deferred hole
(`HD_COAL`) that no reader resolves at the pin. #3318 made its operands' reads resolve, so the
fused inner read now yields the `K | null` niche, and the chain's re-association
(`a ?? (b ?? d)`) builds the outer `??` over it with the literal default lowered as an atom.
The consumer of the whole reads the result at the template, as a plain `i32`, and `print`
formats the atom's id. The direct spelling types every node, so it prints `a`.

**A triple chain over a non-nullable map, recorded here:** `print(p.m[9] ?? p.m[8] ?? p.m[7])`
over `{[i32]: AB}`, all three keys missing, prints `-1` (the sentinel) under #3318 for the hole
and `<T>` spellings. On master `8df60e472` the hole printed `0` and `<T>` printed `-1`. The
direct spelling is a check reject (`tErrUnsupported`), so the owed value is `null` or that
refusal.
