#### Mechanism

Localised at the close. The pin's re-ask (`asPinHolds`) refused where no member may fit, though a
type parameter pinned at no number answers `null` there (D2791), and the instance's re-check
refused again at the cast. Skipping both built an invalid module, because the niche cast over
`string | null` was lowered as the identity. Now:
- The pin skips the refusal for a cast whose operand holds no member that is a number, a
  `boolean` or the target. A numeric member that may fail to convert keeps D2906's refusal.
- The instance's re-check banks the miss per cast and instance (`asInstMiss`, keyed by the
  elaboration key), and a bare `as` must be able to propagate its operand there.
- The emitter reads that bank for the instance it emits (`elabKeyAt`): `as?` is the operand,
  dropped, then the cast's `null`; `as!` traps after it; a bare `as` returns the operand.

The direct spelling keeps its refusal, as the spec rules (E8.6).
