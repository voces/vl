### D3345 — `is i32` and `is f64` over a join pinned at `i32` and `f64` both answered true

**closed 2026-09-30 · was check-clean silently wrong, clause 1, on master `78abe1318` (`true true … true true`), then answered by the `f64` widening · re-closed under the owner's numeric-join ruling of 2026-09-30: the pinned join is `i32 | f64` · found by the #3310 review's generator (`i32_f64_is{i32,f64}`) · fixture `tests/cases/generics/is-over-a-widened-numeric-join.vl`**

#### Mechanism

Master collapsed the join to `T`, so the instance tested the `f64` value against the `i32` it was
typed as and answered both questions true. With the two parameters kept apart (D3268) the pin
re-joins them as the direct join does. Under the numeric-join ruling two runtime numbers of
different types join as their union, so a join the checker marked as of runtime arms
(`markTpJoinApart`) re-joins apart at the pin (`rejoinMembers`), the instance boxes `i32 | f64`,
and `is` answers for the arm taken, as the direct spelling now does.
