Was: `cannot compare _ and S`, with `<` saying `operator '<' mixes _ and S` and `+` saying
`argument 1: operator '+' is not defined` — three operators, one shape, all three now running.

#### Mechanism

Probed, where the row filed it as predicted, and the prediction was right about the channel.
`nomBinMixes` compares the two operands' brands and has no deferral for a hole: `nomNameOfTy` of
an unbound `TyVar` is `""`, the other side is `"S"`, they differ, and a hole parameter reference
is not a brand-adopting literal — so it reports. Every other operand question in the checker
defers on that precondition; `binOpDefinedFor` opens with the very line (`tyHasHole` → true).

**It is [D1732](D1732.md)'s shape, and the fix is the same two-part one.** The body defers on a
hole, and the PIN — where both operands are concrete — gains the brand question as a mirror. The
deferral ALONE is unsound and was measured to be: two DIFFERENT brands through a hole then RUN
where they were correctly refused, at `==`, `<` and `+`. The mirror judges only a branded PAIR,
since a pin has no expression node for `nomBinMixes`'s literal-adoption legs.

The `+` cell needed a third piece: `binOpDefinedFor`'s `+` arm copied `checkBinary`'s
string-concat gate at [D1732](D1732.md) and deliberately not its NEWTYPE branch, which this adds
— a brand concatenates with a same-brand value or a literal and keeps the brand.
