#### Mechanism

D1341's close parks the cast on the hole and re-records its result type as `T | null` from
`castPinAdjudicate`, at the call that PINS the hole. That is the first moment the operand's
type exists — and it is strictly after `take`'s own body was checked, so every consumer INSIDE
the body adjudicated the cast at the pre-pin type `i32`. In argument position that consumer is
`assignableExpr(args[i], at, expected)` against `id`'s `i32` parameter, which passed against
`i32` and was never re-asked. `print` is not affected because its own classifier reads the node
type at EMIT time, after the re-record.

**THE CLOSE HAS TWO HALVES, and the second is a node the re-record never reaches.**

* **The destination is re-asked at the pin.** The argument loop records the parked cast's
  destination (`noteCastArgDest` — the parameter position and its concrete type), and
  `castPinNullLand` re-asks it through `argMismatchErr`, the one home of that wording AND of
  the anchor policy. So the hole reports what the annotated twin reports, at the same place.
  NOT into a HOLE parameter: `id(a as? i32)` over `function id(x)` pins `x` from this very
  argument, so `expected` is the pre-pin answer rather than a declared demand — holding the
  cast to it refused a program whose annotated `take` twin runs.
* **A `Paren` BANKS A COPY OF ITS INNER'S CHECKED TYPE, and the re-record lands on the inner
  node only.** `checkNode` writes `nodeTyIx[ix]` for every node, so `(a as? i32)` carries the
  stale `i32` after the pin rewrote the cast. `emit_rewrite`'s dead-`??` fold reads the
  operand's node type, saw a non-nullable operand, folded the coalesce away, and handed the
  box straight to `id` — while the byte-identical un-parenthesised `a as? i32 ?? 0` ran.
  `emitCoalesce`'s own first line already states the rule this violated ("parens must not
  change `??` semantics"); the fold and the D962 desugar now peel before they ask.

**THE REFUSAL IS THE ONE THE DESIGN OWES, AND BOTH HALVES OF THE TEST ARE ON RECORD.**
`docs/error-handling-design.md` §Trio: "`x as? T` … `as?` yields `T | null` as an expression",
so `id(a as? i32)` into `function id(x: i32)` is ill-typed at every spelling. The annotated
`function take(a: i32 | null)` twin has always said so, in those words. This is D1342's close
one delivery over, and the same discriminator applies: the refusal names a CONTRACT stated at
another spelling, so the program was ill-typed all along and had merely lost the diagnosis at a
monomorphization pin.
