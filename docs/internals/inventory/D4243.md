#### Mechanism

`monoPinGenericList` asks `monoListOnlyCalled`, `monoListElemCalls` and `monoListElemWrites`,
and `monoPinGenericRecord` asks `monoRecordFieldUses`; each walks every node of the frame the
literal is declared in, looking for reads of its one name. One walk per literal makes the pass
quadratic in the frame's size. Guest fuel for `vl build` of N copies of the two lines above:

| N | master `f6ee50929` (refuses) | lane SC (runs) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | 42 M | 211 M |
| 400 | 227 M | 2,105 M |

Master pays the same for `[gA, gA]` lists, which it already runs (485 M at N = 200, 6.29 G at
N = 800 in the review's reading), and for records. A per-frame census of reads keyed by name
fixes it only with a generation key: the pass renames identifiers in place.
