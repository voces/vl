### D15597 — [CLOSED 2026-11-09] a `const` lambda capturing in a generic encloser, handed to a parameter that calls it at two types, refuses as a "copy of a reassigned binding"

**closed 2026-11-09, runs and prints `7`, `x`, `7`, `2`, `true`, `x`, `true`, `2` · was loud emit reject, clause 2, `vl check` rc 0, then `emitProgram: the function binding `same`, a copy of a reassigned binding, is called at more than one argument type` · filed 2026-11-09 against master `82d99608` · two faults. #### Mechanism

The nested-function spelling (`function same(k)`) is bundled per encloser instance since D15596.
Bound with `const`, the lambda takes another path that refuses, and the refusal is wrong twice:
nothing reassigns `same`, and one value called at two signatures is a bundle, not a refusal.
Not yet traced past the message.
