#### Mechanism

Not localised past the ablation. The join of two or three declared parameters with a literal is
kept apart (`T | U | string`), and at a pin whose union mixes a literal union, a string and a
null the instance's re-typed join reads a scratch local no function declares. The instance
refuses that shape loudly (`monoMultiParamLitNullJoin`) until the box is built.
