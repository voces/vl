#### Mechanism

The tag decision declines `{inc0, half}`: probe traps pin it at the holder-context check
(`ftHoldCtxOk`, check_fn_tag.vl, its `LetDecl` arm) for the bound list. The set stays closures,
and [D15926](D15926.md)'s floor refuses the split call where it is lowered. The run-fix is the
decision accepting that holder.
