### D17835 — a declared union is minted empty and filled in place through its member array

**runs today and must keep running · refactor target: S2a keeps an empty union out of the intern table because this writer fills it afterwards · filed 2026-11-28 against master `9ee417b8d` · owner COV-IS**

Repro:

    type U = "a" | "b"
    const u: U = "b"
    const v: "a" | "b" = "a"
    print(u)
    print(v)
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: b, a

Want:

    b
    a

#### Mechanism

The top-level pass mints a declared union's placeholder with `mkUnionTy([])` (`check_top_level.vl`)
and `unionDeclFillMembers` (`check_relate_core.vl`) later pushes its members into the same array, the
row's `uMembers`. A row whose content is written after its mint cannot be keyed by that content at
mint, so the intern table refuses an empty union. The close mints the declared union once its members
are known, or keys it on its declared name.
