#### Mechanism

`half` is needed at `(f64) => f64` through `ws` and at `(i32) => f64` through `m4`, which pins at
the second. The binding `v` holds `m4`, but nothing gave it `m4`'s pin: D15757's walk followed only a
`const` bound to a held binding. So `v.get("a")` stayed open, and the call through it found no
signature.
