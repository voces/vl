#### Mechanism

The backtrace is a repeated pair of compiler frames, so this is a RECURSION rather than a table
read of a sentinel — D1513's class, not the `sentinel-index-unguarded` family, and asking which
of the two a new trap is before auditing readers is the rule. It is not a stack overflow
either: the trap is `allocation size too large`, and the pair is named once the seed is built
with `--names`, which is the step that turned a wasm function index into a diagnosis:

    0: vl!internInlineShapeTy   1: vl!internInlineShapeTy   2: vl!internShapeDeepTy
    3: vl!internInlineShapeTy   4: vl!internShapeDeepTy     5: vl!internInlineShapeTy   …

`internInlineShapeTy` walks a shape's fields, and for each one it interns the field's nested
shape TWICE — once through `internShapeDeepTy`, and again through `internInlineShapeTy` itself
to recover the row when `nameFieldCodeTy` declines. Two walks of the inner spine per level is
2^depth walks of the whole one, and the timing says so exactly: 48, 82, 154, 288, 571, 1106,
2157, 5304 ms at depths 12 through 19, doubling each step, and at 20 the work reaches an
allocation the GC heap cannot hold.

The second visit is a no-op by construction — `annShapeIndexOf` answers it with the row the
first visit pushed — so a memo keyed on the (name, type-hint) pair and live only while an outer
intern is on the stack collapses the doubling without changing an answer. Depths 12 to 24 then
cost 15 to 27 ms, and 0 of 3,057 `tests/cases` modules move.

**The bound the capture comparator carries had to move with it, and that is measured rather
than assumed.** With the intern linear, the ladder reaches the comparator's own wall: at a
bound of 24 the capture returns at depths 24 and 25 as a check-clean WRONG VALUE and traps
again from 26 through a SECOND exponential on the member-access side. Raising the bound to 64
refutes there instead — which mints the literal its own row and never reaches that second
recursion — and the ladder is then clean from 1 to 63. The constant is free: 0 of 3,057 modules,
one byte of seed. What stands at 64 and at 70 is filed as D1705 and D1706.

The ladder is sharp and the ablation is short. Nesting depths 10 through **19 run and print
`7`**; depth **20 and every depth above traps**. Deleting the declared `type H` makes depth 20
run, so the ingredient is a declared row of the same deeply-nested shape beside the literal, not
the literal alone. No union is involved: an `i32` leaf traps identically.

**And this is what makes the capture comparator's depth bound unreachable.**
`elemRowsCaptureCompatAt` walks one nesting level or one container per step and stops at 24,
returning its lenient default — so the wall stands at depth 25, four levels ABOVE the deepest
program the compiler can compile at all. Measured, not argued: an A/B of the bound at 8 against
24, one seed each, over container depths 2–10 and nesting depths 2–10, moves exactly the cells
past 8 —

| axis | bound 8 | bound 24 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| container depth 2, 4, 6, 7, 8 | runs `7` | runs `7` |
| container depth **9, 10** | check-clean invalid wasm | runs `7` |
| nesting depth 2, 4, 6, 7 | runs `7` | runs `7` |
| nesting depth **8, 9** | prints `true` (wrong value) | runs `7` |
| nesting depth **10** | loud `object literal is missing a struct field` | runs `7` |

— and the seed is byte-identical at both bounds (2,227,315 bytes) with a 0-of-3,053 module diff
at both, so the constant is free and only moves where the wall stands. At 8 the wall is reached
by a probe; at the shipped 24 it is not, because this row's trap fires first. The counter is
still a wall and not a proof; what removes it is a visited-pair guard, which terminates on
exactly the cyclic case and lets an acyclic walk go as deep as it likes.
