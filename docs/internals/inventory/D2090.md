#### Mechanism

`declaredSlotOf` and `declaredSlotLive` (compiler/emit_classify.vl) answer a name's local slot
from `scopeSlotOf` when the scope index has it, and otherwise by a linear scan of the whole
function's `localNames` — every local ever allocated, one per `const t` here — comparing
strings; with the name declared more than once the scan also counts the duplicates
(`dupScanActive`). Each read of `t` pays O(locals), so n arms reading their own `t` cost O(n²):
the build's profile at 200 statements is 77% inclusive in `declaredSlotOf`, 54% self in
`__str_eq__`. It was unreachable at this size before D1990, whose chain trapped first.
