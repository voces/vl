#### Mechanism

The literal is delivered to the destination `(… | {w: i32})[] | null`, but the checker records
it at its own inferred type, with the literal union softened to `string`
(`(string[] | {w: i32})[]`). It does not record it at the destination's non-null arm. No union
row is keyed for that softened element union: the row is keyed under the declared
`K[] | {w: i32}`. So `repOfArray`'s union-element arm finds no `unRowOfCanon`/`unRowOfKey` and
declines, and under `VL_REP_SHADOW=1` the column reads `listRep reflist -> unc` (4 notes on this
witness). `exprListRepOf` then falls back to the ArrayLit shape rung (`arrLitIsRef`), which
answers `reflist`, the right rep. This blocks deleting the ArrayLit shape rung, not the field-read
rung. The likely place to record the destination's type is the delivery seam in
check_assignable.vl (`recordRepTyAdopt`, `recordElemRepArrayLit`).
