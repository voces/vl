#### Mechanism

The delivery walk's three recursive steps stop once a query's `seen` list holds more than 32
entries: `elabSlotReadDeliveries`, `elabRetSlotOn`, and the `elabCallsReading` / `elabSlotReadKeys`
step. They return without adding a delivery. A tripped branch therefore answers "delivers
nothing", the unsafe answer for an uncalled or pin reader. A slot whose calls lie past the 32nd
binding looks uncalled there.

The witness hands a generic function down a chain of 40 `const` aliases and calls it only at the
end. It trips the cap 11 times (counted with a scratch build) and runs on master, because other
mechanisms deliver `id` its instance. The depth cap beside it (paths of 8 steps) became a cycle
rule in the climb-depth PR. This breadth cap is unchanged.
