#### Mechanism

A prediction from the ablation: `globalArmScopeOk` (`emit_sections.vl`) withholds the start
function's arm frame from an un-annotated global whose initializer yields an arm tail, and
it counts a `??` over the join as yielding one. D5430's close classifies the join binding by
its type, but here the join is the `??` operand, not the binding, so the operand is still
read with `t` unbound and gets no nullable kind.
