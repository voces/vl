#### Mechanism

The map keeps its bundle record whole only when each read of its slot is held as the record: a
`const` holding `m["a"]` is held when every use of it is a test, a call at one of the record's
signatures, a copy or a hand (`elabSlotNulHolderOk`). That walk declines on any use of the holder in
another frame (`cwUseInOtherFrame`), so a module `const x` read from `get` or `pick` declines the
map's note. The map is then declared at the single closure and legacy refuses the store. It is
D17094's cross-frame decline one level down, at the holder, which D17094's module-map rung does not
reach.
