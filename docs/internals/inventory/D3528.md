#### Mechanism

Not localised past the ablation. The annotation is a union of the parameters that the checker
did not build as a kept-apart join, so the `??` result is a deferred join `A | B`. At `(i32,
i32)` the binding is `i32 | null` and the `??` unboxes to `i32`, but the `is` reads the result
as the box. The un-annotated spelling, whose join is kept apart, runs.

Closed by reading the join at the pin where the emitter classified the template's: the `??`
lowers to an `if` typed at the annotation's `A | B`, and `exprUnion` (whether that `if` is a
box), `exprString` and the `if`'s union name now substitute the instance's binding first
(`pinSubOperandTyOf`), as the `if`'s own blocktype already did. The if-chain's pin rebuild also
takes the recorded union where its arms have no pinned join of their own.
