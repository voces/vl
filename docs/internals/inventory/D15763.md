### D15763 — `is K[][]` over a `V[][] | string` box receiver in a generic instance answers false for an empty list

**closed 2026-11-12, prints `1` · was check-clean silently wrong value, clause 1 · the nested-list face of [D4912](D4912.md) · reachable where [D4937](D4937.md)'s spelling is refused · filed 2026-11-12 against master `c7474c370` · closed by `emitListEmptyFitArms` (wasmEmit.vl) admitting an arm whose elements part below its first level: it walks that arm's ref-list rows to the parting depth and tests their emptiness under the arm's tag, as `emitListEmptyFitIs` does for a bare receiver; the nullable face is [D15811](D15811.md) · fixture `tests/cases/generics/is-nested-list-over-union-arm-tests-emptiness-at-depth.vl`**

#### Mechanism

An empty list fits any list type, as D4912 settled at depth 1 (`emitListEmptyFitIs` guards the
emptiness test under the list arm's tag). At depth 2 the box receiver's `is` keeps the
instance's static fold from the `i32[][]` arm, so the empty list answers false. The same
receiver spelled `V[][] | null`, or bare `V[][]`, prints `1`.
