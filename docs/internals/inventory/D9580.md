#### Mechanism

D9513's close answers the parameter `f: (x: A) => A[]` without its render, and the signature
no longer doubles. What still doubles is the argument: the lambda's body `[x]` is a list
literal of the record, and `collectA` → `collectAArrayLit` spells its element with
`tyToStrObj`, `7·2^depth` characters, as [D9510](D9510.md)'s nested literal does; the
monomorphizer and `criClassify` read the lambda's result spelled the same way. The
parameter alone, with `g` never called, is polynomial.

#### Grading list

The witness prints `1` at every depth, and the row closes when the depth-16 to depth-20 fuel
ratio stops growing exponentially. Re-grade [D9510](D9510.md), whose nested list literal is
spelled by the same walk.

**After a record's boxed-field answer is decided once per arena node, 2026-10-22.** The filed
mechanism was a guess, and a depth-20 `--names` profile refuted it: 47% of samples sat in
`structFieldBoxRetName` (check_ret_names.vl), reached from `inferRetElemListRung` and
`inferRetAdoptUnionElemList` in `finishInferredReturn` through
`structFieldBoxElemListRetName`. For a nested record field it called itself, and each call
ended by rendering its record with `tyToEmitName`, so the diamond was walked and rendered once
per path. `sfbHasBox` now answers whether a record carries a boxed field through a per-call
memo keyed by arena id, a nested field asks `tyEmitNameNonEmpty` for the render's "" check, and
only the outermost record is rendered. The compiler builds itself byte-identically, and every
file under `distilled/` and `tests/cases/` builds byte-identically to master's (0 of 16,260
differ). [D9510](D9510.md)'s witness is not on this walk: 1.520e9 at depth 12 before and after.
