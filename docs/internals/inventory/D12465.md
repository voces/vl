### D12465 — `==` between a union-arm record and a same-shape record outside the union is refused

**closed 2026-11-10 — the witness prints `true` · `binEqStructRow` reads an arm operand at the struct row its values are stored at (`variantRowOf`), and the existing heap-type agreement test (D959) admits the pair, so `==` is the field-wise struct compare · fixture `tests/cases/unions/error-arm-struct-equality-mixed.vl` (both orders, `!=`, a list element, a nested field, a record holding each) · was loud emit reject · clause 2 · filed 2026-10-24 against master `b861c1d9f`**

#### Mechanism

`Circle` is a union arm stored at its variant row and `Dot2` a standalone record row, two heap types, so the `==` dispatch declines. Under the structural-records ruling (D3870, 2026-10-08) same-shape records are one type, so the comparison is type-valid.
