#### Mechanism

[D14505](D14505.md)'s chain past the budget: each literal round adopts one more binding of the
chain, and the eight rounds end with `l1` and `l2` still pending. A pin left pending when the
budget runs out is dropped without a diagnostic. The fix is for adopting a `Map()` binding to
adopt, in the same round, the `Map()` bindings stored into it, so the chain settles in one round
at any depth.
