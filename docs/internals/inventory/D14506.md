#### Mechanism

Two bounds, neither a diagnostic. The return pins `l10`, and only the re-check judges
`l10["k"] = l9` against that and queues `l9`: one binding a literal round, dropped silently when
the budget runs out. And the record-delivery queue (`recPend`, `recOwnPend`, `recAdoptNote`,
`recAdForce`, `recJoinCheck`) asked `tyHoldsObj(t, 8)`, so a map eight levels above its record
was never queued at all: the chain failed at depth 8 at any budget.
