#### Mechanism

The prediction below was half right. D2383's close keys the box on the `??` node's own
recorded type (`coalesceResultUnionName`). In an arm, that type is the arm's share of the
`if`'s join, so the `??` is not recognised as joining to the box, and the arm lowers through the
boolean niche's `i32` while the `if` block is the box. What the close found is that the arm's
own join, `boolean | A`, is named by no declaration, so no row existed for the node's type to
resolve to; registering every inline join's struct-bearing union from the checker's recorded
type ([D2373](D2373.md)'s mechanism) gives the arm its box, and the filed repro prints `7`.
Pinned by `tests/cases/unions/coalesce-nulbool-join-in-if-arm.vl`.
