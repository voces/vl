#### Mechanism

A depth-14 `--names` profile puts most of the samples in `collectA`, under
`mvShapeOfMapNodeTy` and `mvShapeOfValNameTys`, which shape the map-value tables from the
value's render, and 17% under `sigKeyOfTy` → `annParamKindTy`, the parameter's token read off
the same render. Both halves need the map-value table keyed by type, which is the re-keying of
[D9511](D9511.md).

Fuel is `VL_FUEL=1` guest fuel of one `vl build`, which is deterministic.

#### Grading list

The witness prints `1` at every depth, and the row closes when the depth-12 to depth-16 fuel
ratio stops growing exponentially.

**Left open by the lane that closed D9510 and D9770, 2026-10-22.** That close answers the
ref-list tables, not the map-value ones, and the fuel here is unchanged by it (56.6M, 317.0M
and 1.15e9 at depths 8, 12 and 14; without the `| null`, 31.4M, 160.6M and 550.0M). The cost
sites are the map-value tables' own: `mvShapeOfMapNodeTy`, `rlInternNameTy` reached through
them, `sigKeyOfTy` → `annParamKindTy` → `mvSlotOfMapNameTys`, and `mAssignMapValueStructs` →
`mvCanonRepOf`, each reading the value's render. `tests/vl_compile_gc_test.ts` takes this
row's witness at depth 23, which traps under the null collector; when this row closes, the
test needs a witness that still traps.

**Closed 2026-10-22, on master `23ac8244a`.** Two finds by type remove the per-site renders:
the map node's shape and the parameter's token both ask `mvDeepFuncSlotOfTy`, so only the
first visit renders the value, to mint its slot. Most of the remaining cost was
`tyDeeperThan` walking the record as a tree, which [D9930](D9930.md) answers. The depth-23
witness now compiles under the null collector in 252.3M fuel, so
