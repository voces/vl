### D3479 — a `const` holding radix arithmetic is computed once at `i32` and then widened, so an `i64` read gets the `i32` wrap

**closed 2026-11-02 · runs, prints `2147483648` · `+`, `-` and `*` over a radix pattern fold to a pattern when every value operand is in `i32` and the exact result fits 32 bits (`cexPatRingOk`, `cexPatResult` in `compiler/const_exact.vl`), as `<<` inside 32 bits already did: a pattern is its value at `i64` and that value's residue at `i32`, so the folded pattern is the computation at either width, and `MI` becomes a literal `const` whose reads are rewritten · was check-clean silently wrong · clause 1 · grading list: a 6,303-cell constant grid (pattern × operator × value, read directly, through a `const` and through a function at `i32`, `i64`, `f64` and with no context, graded against the direct spelling) changes 210 cells, every one to that spelling's answer, and none against it, with no outcome moving; the 118 `i64` cells still off are trees the fold leaves to the use's width (a result past 32 bits, `/`, `%`, an ambiguous `>>`, `<<` past 32 bits), filed as [D14990](D14990.md); 1 emitted-byte mover over `tests/**/*.vl`, `numerics/const-pattern-width-readings.vl`, whose `0x7FFFFFFF + 1` now folds and prints the same · pin: `tests/cases/numerics/const-pattern-ring-arithmetic-read-at-each-width.vl` · found in the #3331 review (exact-constant hover) · filed 2026-10-02 against master `a8c4dce6d`**

Repro:

    const MI = 0x7FFFFFFF + 1
    const mi64: i64 = MI
    print(mi64)
    // PRINTED -2147483648 before the close

Want:

    2147483648

#### Mechanism

A prediction until a close confirms it. Under the owner's ruling a radix literal is a bit
pattern of its USE's width, and arithmetic over one is computed at that width. So the
constant fold leaves `0x7FFFFFFF + 1` unfolded (`cexKeepWhole`), and `MI` is not a literal
`const` whose reads are rewritten. The declaration is checked and emitted once, at `i32`
(`i32.add` of `2147483647` and `1`, which wraps), and the `i64` read widens that wrapped
value. Read at `i64`, the pattern arithmetic should have been computed at `i64`.
