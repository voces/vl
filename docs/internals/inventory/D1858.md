#### Mechanism

ROADMAP row 25's oracle counts `rlElemStructRow`'s `structIndexByName` as a site a structural
name reaches, and converting it makes this program **check-clean invalid wasm**, along with
five more `structs/*twin*` modules. The conversion is wrong, and **not for D1857's reason**:
the nominal floor is untouched here — of the six modules, the shadow-gated note reports
`would-flip` for every one and `would-flip-AND-IS-A-VARIANT` for **none**, so the variant
floor never fires.

The function is a FOUR-RUNG ladder and its rung 4 is *already* `structIndexOfTypeName`: arena
identity, nominal identity, the canon-key row off the recorded type, then the fieldset scan.
Converting rung 2 does not add the structural bridge — it **duplicates it at rung 2**, ahead
of `repRowOfTyStruct`, the arena rung that disambiguates.

**The order test is the proof, and it needs no conversion at all.** Leaving the nominal call
and the floor exactly as they are and merely asking the function's own existing last rung ONE
PLACE EARLIER loses the identical six modules. The mechanism is rung ORDER, not nominal keying.

What goes wrong concretely: for an INLINE structural annotation whose inner and outer rows
share the one-field fieldset `{f}`, the fieldset scan's first match falls to the INNER row and
the element builds one level short. The arena rung is what tells them apart, and it no longer
gets asked.

#### Ablation

Three spellings of the same destination, on three seeds:

| face | base | rung reordered | rung 2 converted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| annotated, INLINE shape (`{f: {f: i64 \| null}}[]`) | runs `NULL` | **INVALID WASM** | **INVALID WASM** |
| annotated by the ALIAS (`T0[]`) | runs `NULL` | runs `NULL` | runs `NULL` |
| un-annotated (inference pins it) | runs `NULL` | runs `NULL` | runs `NULL` |

The load-bearing ingredient is the **inline structural spelling of the annotation** — the one
face that hands the ladder a name only the fieldset scan can match. Note the direction: here
the ANNOTATED face is the fragile one and the inferred face is safe, the opposite of the usual
missing-annotation shape.
