### D16440 — a fresh list `const` spread into a list literal beside a part at a wider record is not built at that record

**closed 2026-11-15 — the witness runs and prints `1` · was: check-clean invalid wasm · clause 1 · found probing `check_bind_adopt.vl:829`, an unprobed `--sites` row, for lane J · filed 2026-11-15 against master `6adb40099` · closed in the list stamp: with no part built elsewhere, a spread of a fresh list `const` is built at the list's element, as a spread into a push builds it (D15287), its other deliveries at its own storage refused, and any other spread storing a field apart is refused (`recListSpreadsApartRefuse`, check_bind_adopt.vl; `recHeldListSpreadStamp`, check_fit_rules.vl) · fixture `tests/cases/soundness/record-list-const-spread-beside-wider-part-runs.vl`**

#### Mechanism

`recListLitBuild` (check_bind_adopt.vl) builds a list literal's fresh parts, and a spread of a
list literal, at the record a part built elsewhere gives the list. Here no part is built
elsewhere: `a` and `...s` are both held at their own storage, so the stamp stops with nothing
built. The join types the list `{x: f64}[]`, `s` is built at `{x: i32}`, and nothing compared the
spread's records with the element. The fit check that refuses a held record part (`[a, m]`) does
not see a spread's elements.
