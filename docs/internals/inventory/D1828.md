#### Mechanism

A `(i32 | null)[]` element is a `{tag, anyref}` value-union box: `wasm-dis` of the guarded
spelling reads `array.new_fixed` over `struct.new $box (tag, payload)`, with tag 0 for the i32
and 6 for the null. `emitCoalesceGo`'s box arm (D960) claims an operand whose RESULT is
another box and hands the box on; here the `??` yields the unboxed scalar, so that arm
declines and the ladder falls through to its refusal.

The gate was the second half. The arm asked `tyIsNulValueUnionBox(nodeTyIxOf(e.binLeft))` —
a question about the element's TYPE, which reads `i32 | null` — and a probe printing both
answers reads `box=0` beside `repKind=union repNul=4`. What the read leaves is the box; the
type does not say so. The new arm asks `repOfNode(e.binLeft)`, stashes the box once, tests its
tag against `nullBoxTag`, and unboxes the payload at the result's own atom kind on the present
arm, with the default re-encoded at that kind so both arms of the `if` agree.

Four numeric widths are the family — `i32`, `i64`, `f64`, `f32`. A `boolean` element rides the
i32 sentinel niche and a `string` element the nullable array-ref niche, so neither reaches the
box arm; both ran before and are the controls.

#### Ablation

| --- | --- | --- |

| the annotation on the destination | refused either way | no |
