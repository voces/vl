#### Mechanism

The same disagreement D1632 named, at the initialiser shape D1476 named, in the scope D1633
named — and the three fixes together still do not reach it. A code-15 nested-struct field
read is stored `(ref null $S)`, so the local is the nullable niche; the annotation says the
bare struct; `captureValKind`'s annotated arm defers to `letInitCellKind` and takes the
annotation when that ladder does not answer the niche. Inside a frame it does not answer
here, and the env field is declared non-null over a nullable value.

D1633's fix gives the classifiers a frame-local `let` resolver, and it is not enough for
this shape: the receiver `src` resolves through it while the field read's own kind rung
still declines. Which rung is not yet ablated.

**The union declaration is load-bearing** — the template this cell comes from records it as
its own ingredient, and deleting `type U` from the witness makes it RUN. That is why the two
unused-looking declarations stay in the repro.
