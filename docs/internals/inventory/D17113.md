#### Mechanism

The left operand is the narrowed read of `{ f: null, g: i32 } | null`, the right a `J`. The record
compare walks the fields; its `f` field compare has a `null`-typed side (`f: null` on the left) and an
`i32 | null` side, and was planned as a union-box compare that serves neither. The D17126 route
(`nulName`) makes that field compare the declared side's null test.
