#### Mechanism

D9460's rung answers a list whose element is the declared record, not a list of such lists. A
depth-16 `--names` profile puts the cost under `collectA` → `collectAArrayLit` on the nested
literal, in `arrLitIsRef`, `arrLitNestedElemName` and `rlElemStoredName`, each spelling the
element with `tyToStrObj`, `7·2^depth` characters. Neither the record in the literal nor the
call is needed: `g([])` still doubles.

#### Grading list

The witness prints `1` at every depth, and the row closes when the depth-12 to depth-16 fuel
ratio stops being a power of two per level. Re-grade `g([])` and the `const` face.

**Left open by the lane that closed D9512-D9515, 2026-10-22.** The cost is not a signature
token a rung can answer from the type. `collectAArrayLit` on the nested literal spells the
element for the ref-list tables, `rlInternNameTy`, `rlElemStoredName` and
`rlCanonLitUnionAtoms`, and for `arrLitIsRef`, `arrLitNestedElemName`, `ensureRefElemTy` and
`refArrElemName`: those tables are keyed on the element's render, so a close needs them keyed
on the arena type. The fuel is unchanged by that lane (137.8M, 1.52e9 and 22.8e9 at depths 8,
12 and 16; `g([])` 82.3M, 727M and 11.0e9). [D9580](D9580.md), a lambda whose body lists the
record, is spelled by the same literal walk.

**Left open by the lane that closed D9511, 2026-10-22.** The map-value tables now find a deep
declared record's slot from its arena type, but the ref-list table cannot follow without a
wider change: it stores the element's render as `rlElemName`, read by about seventy sites
including the relocation keys and the section writers. The cost sites are unchanged:
`collectAArrayLit` → `arrLitIsRef` → `arrLitNestedElemName` → `nameIsRefArray`,
`refArrShapeKind` and `refArrElemName`; `ensureRefElemTy` → `rlInternNameTy` →
`rlElemStoredName` → `rlCanonLitUnionAtoms`; `mAssignRefListTwins` → `rlSlotOfArrName`; and
`sigKeyOfTy`, whose `sigDeclRecListTokOfTy` answers a declared-record element but not a list
of them. The fuel is unchanged (137.8M at depth 8, 1.52e9 at 12). With D9511 closed,
`tests/vl_compile_gc_test.ts` takes this row's witness at depth 23, which traps under the null
collector; when this row closes, the test needs a witness that still traps.
