#### Mechanism

`__f64_rem__` (`compiler/emit_sections.vl`) doubles the divisor up to the dividend and halves back
down, which is O(exponent gap), up to about 2 x 2098 iterations. The v1 contract admits `%`
because it is not on the deny list, and the #3039 amendment (I15) counts helpers like it as 0
because they are "bounded by the float format". Bounded is true; "never data-dependent", the
amendment's own invariant, is not. Re-measured for the synthesis: 200,000 reads of
`get m(self: R): f64 { (self as! f64) % 1.5 }` took 0.36 s at receiver `1.7e308` and a few
milliseconds at receiver `3.0`.

#### Grading list

The `% 1.5` pair above at both receivers; the same getter with an `f32` receiver; any constant
`%` in a constant-bounded loop once the amendment is built.

#### Close

The owner ruled (2026-09-23) to refuse float `%` in a getter body. `gwBinExpr` refuses a `%` whose
result erases to `f64` or `f32` (an `f32` remainder is promoted into the same `__f64_rem__`),
anchored at the operator, in #3046's one-error style, and offers the method. A constant-time
lowering (`x - y * trunc(x / y)` is not exact for large quotients) was not attempted. Integer
`%` — `i32`, `i64` — is one `rem_s` and stays admitted. `tests/cases/getters/getter-contract-allows.vl`
used `self.f % 1.5` as an allowed case and now uses float subtraction instead.

Fixtures: `tests/cases/getters/getter-step-budget-rejects.vl` (`f64` and `f32` `%` refused, `i32`
`%` beside them clean) and `getter-step-budget-allows.vl` (`i32` `%` runs, printing `2`).
