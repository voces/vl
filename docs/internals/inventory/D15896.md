#### Mechanism

`fnSlotRetAssignable` (check_relate_core.vl), the result rule of a function slot, admits a result
widened to its nullable or to a union and leaves the conversion to `fvaAdapt`'s adapter, but an
exact numeric widening fell to `fnSlotAssignable`, which refuses it.
