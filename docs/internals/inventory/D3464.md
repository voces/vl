#### Mechanism

The literal's cost is linear, but every element pays for many whole-literal questions. A guest
profile of a 1,000,000-element `i32` literal after D3417's close (`VL_PROFILE_GUEST`, a `--names`
seed) puts 58% of the compile in the emitter: `arrLitNumKind` 16% (asked once by `collectA` and
once by `emitArr`, each a pass over every element through `exprIsI64` and `exprIsF64`),
`arrLitDataBuild` 12% (each element lowered through the ordinary element emitter into a scratch
buffer and its constant decoded), `hasSpreadItem` 8% (a pass over every element, asked by six
classifiers of the same literal), and `numLexIsFloat` 9% (the lexeme re-scanned by each width
question). Lexing and parsing are 21%, the checker 18% (`checkArrayLitNode`, `joinTys`). The
answers are per-literal facts: one element-kind summary computed once per literal and
emission would remove most of the emitter's share, and that needs a memo registered with the
memo-generation census, which is why it is a row and not part of D3417's close.
