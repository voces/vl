### D5289 — `as` to a single literal or an inline sub-union of the operand's union is refused as not a member

**loud check reject · clause 2 · OPEN · found closing D5286 · filed 2026-10-17 against master `3f6a785db`**

Repro:

    type W = "a" | "b" | "c"
    function f(k: W): "a" { k as! "a" }
    print(f("a"))
    // vl check: `as "a"` names a type that is not a member of W

Want:

    a

#### Mechanism

The cast's target check asks whether the target is one of the operand union's MEMBERS by
identity, and an inline target (`"a"`, `("a" | "b")`) is a fresh type that matches no member
even though every literal it holds is one. The sentence is false of the program: `"a"` is a
member of `W`. A registered alias target goes another way and runs (D5286).

#### Ablation

| target | outcome | needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `"a"` | loud check reject | — |
| `("a" \| "b")` | loud check reject | no |
| a registered alias of `"a" \| "b"` | runs | **yes** (inline) |
