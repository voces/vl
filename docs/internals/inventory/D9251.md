### D9251 — a record holding a map of un-annotated functions, returned through a `const`, reaches a call with no signature

**loud emit reject · `vl check` rc 0 · `a function-value call has no interned signature` · clause 2 · found closing [D9181](D9181.md) · present on master `7ad01b8b3` · filed 2026-10-21 against master `7ad01b8b3`**

#### Mechanism

Not traced. D9181 types the caller's binding only when every `return` of the callee is a
record literal (`monoPinCallRecord`, emit_mono.vl). Here the `return` reads a `const`, so the
caller's `v` keeps the checker's open field type and the call through `x` has no signature.
