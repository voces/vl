#### Mechanism

Not yet located past the list stamp. D16444 copies a spread's call into the list's element; a
spread of a binding of the call has no copy recorded, as `recHeldCallPartCopy` records one for a
binding of a record call part (D16442). The record face's analogue would copy the call as it is
bound and refuse the binding's other deliveries at its own storage.
