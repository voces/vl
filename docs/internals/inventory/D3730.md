**runs today and must keep running · open · a cost row, no wrong value: the witness prints its Want, and the cell is the cost · residue of [D3664](D3664.md): `cboxScalarLets` admits `i32`, `i64`, `f64`, `f32` and `boolean` only · filed by lane L2 against master `687b42dbe`**

#### Mechanism

The write-back convention carries any valtype, but a reference binding's assignment lowering
reads per-slot seeds (`localStructIdx`, the nullable and union arms of `emitAssign`) that a
capture parameter does not have, so only scalars were admitted. Strings, records, lists, unions
and nullable bindings each need their seed answered for a capture parameter first.
