### D17422 — `o?.f ?? d` over a field typed `null` has no `if` blocktype for the field's rep

**closed 2026-11-24 by lane IR-P1 · runs, prints `x` · was a loud emit reject (``emitProgram: `?.` over a nullable struct has no `if` blocktype for this field's rep``) at check rc 0, clause 2 · fixed in emit_classify.vl: `exprIsNulNone` takes a `?.` whose leaf field holds the `null` unit as the `null` unit whatever its receiver, so the read takes the null-unit path (the receiver for its effect, then `null`) · filed 2026-11-24 by lane IR-S1 against master `f37a98e23`, found writing D17421's position matrix**

#### Mechanism

The `?.`…`??` site spells its `if` result from the field's rep, and a field typed `null` (the
`null` unit) has none to spell, so the site refuses. A standalone `u?.tags` refuses too, with
`a standalone \`?.\` has no rep for this leaf field`.

Both sentences are one family. `exprIsNulNone` (emit_classify.vl) took a `?.` as the `null` unit
only when its receiver was one (D16338). A `?.` whose leaf field holds the `null` unit is `null`
whatever the receiver, but the read missed the null-unit path in `emitOptMemberValueGo`. It fell
to `optLeafArmOf`, whose reps have no `null` field, and the `??` site did not know its left was
the unit. A `??` over a known null-unit left already ran (`z ?? "d"`, `u.f ?? g()`).
