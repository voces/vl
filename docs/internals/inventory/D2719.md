**closed · runs, prints `1` · was a loud check reject, then (since #8072's predicate) a loud emit reject (`as Set<i32>` needs a BOXED union operand or a nullable-REF one), clause 2 · found by D2684's close · filed 2026-09-26 against master `74bd044d7` · re-graded 2026-11-14: the witness read `w.size`, which a `Set` does not have; it reads `w.length` · fixed 2026-11-14: the niche cast reads the operand's type at the instance (`emitAsNulNiche`, wasmEmit.vl), where `T` pinned to a reference makes `| null` a nullable reference. #### Mechanism

The checker admits `u is Set<i32>` over `T | null`, since `T` may be that type, but its `as`
membership test compares the target against the declared members and finds neither `T` nor
`null` equal to `Set<i32>`. The same check refuses `match` patterns naming a concrete type over
`T | string` (the review's p3 to p6). An exact instance now binds `T` per pin (D2684), so the
emitter has what the test needs; the gate is the checker's membership rule.
