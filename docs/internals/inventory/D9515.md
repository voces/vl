#### Mechanism

A depth-16 `--names` profile puts the cost under `collectAArrayLit` on the field's list literal,
in `arrLitUnionElemName` and `arrLitElemKind`, spelling the element with `tyToStrObj`,
`7·2^depth` characters. The parameter is not needed: the binding `const b: B = {xs: [a]}`
alone costs the same. An empty list in the field (`g({xs: []})`) costs less but still
grows.

| face, master `0be192a3f` | depth 8 | depth 12 | depth 16 | depth 20 |

| `g(b: B)` with `g({xs: [a]})` (this witness) | 27.5M | 163.9M | 342.9M | 1.96e9 |

same with D9460's close.

#### Grading list

The witness prints `1` at every depth, and the row closes when the depth-16 to depth-20 fuel
ratio stops growing exponentially. Re-grade the binding alone and `g({xs: []})`.

**After a list-of-records field reads its row from the arena, 2026-10-22.**
`anonUnionFieldName` asks `anonRecListRenders` before it renders the field: a list whose element is a record, with a non-empty render, is never a union box by its spelling, so only the arena rung `unRowOfCanon` is asked, and the field gets that row's name or "". Two walks that visited the record once per path now visit each arena node once: `collectTyReachCloSigs` remembers the budget and registry generation each node was walked with, and `objNumWidens` the destination each source record was shown not to widen into. Before landing, a validating seed computed both answers wherever a rung fired and trapped on any disagreement, and one control seed per rung trapped whenever it fired: the `closure` parameter rung fired on 343 of 16,247 files under `distilled/` and `tests/cases/`, the `union` rung on 882, the returned-function rung on 133 and the record-list rung of `anonUnionFieldName` on 64. Every file builds byte-identically to master's under both the validating and the final seed, `scripts/rep-fuzz-check.sh` is exact, and the compiler builds itself byte-identically.
