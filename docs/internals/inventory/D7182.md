**closed 2026-10-19 by lane L1 · runs, prints `3 4 3 4` · was loud emit reject · `vl check` rc 0 · clause 2 · CAPABILITY: the same call at the literal index `fs[0](add)` runs `3 4` · the value-flow question answers a computed index as reading every element, and mono points a call statement's computed-index callee at the list's one generic, running a copy of the read first so its index is evaluated and bounds-checked · pins: `tests/cases/functions/hole-fn-list-computed-index-called-with-generic.vl`, `tests/cases/functions/hole-fn-list-computed-index-out-of-bounds-traps.vl` · the call used as a value is [D7385](D7385.md) · found closing [D7140](D7140.md) · the same on master `6a89bc66d` · filed 2026-10-19 against master `6a89bc66d`**

#### Mechanism

Probed 2026-10-19 against master `966471462`. Answering a computed index in the value-flow
question alone moves nothing (tried in #4179 and again here); the close needs it and the mono
rewrite together, and each alone still refuses. The refusal is mono's. The slot reads
that point a call at its one generic (`monoFlowElemAt`, `monoFlowReadSet`) work only at a
literal or folded `const` index. Rewriting the read into the generic's name at a computed index
would also drop the index's evaluation and its bounds check. A close has to keep the `Index`
and give the call the generic's instance as its callee's type.
