#### Mechanism

Every float literal and every folded `/` or `%` goes through the exact-rational folder, which
reduces fractions with `cxmGcd` and renders a float with `cexFloatSci` (an 800-bit significand).
Both call `cxmDivmod`. Before this change it shifted one bit per step, calling `cxmShl`,
`bigCmp` and `cxmSub` on the whole remainder each time, so a division cost
O(bits × limbs). On diderven's 9,820 float literals, multiplied by the per-root re-check
(D7321), `cxmDivmod` was on the stack of 5.90% of the guest's samples (`cxmGcd` 2.81%,
`cexFloatSci` 2.74%), and `cxmShr`, `cxmSub` and the other bignum helpers held 6.6% of its
self time. The limb-at-a-time division matched the old one on 300,000 random operand pairs,
including 5,168 that took the add-back step.
