#### Mechanism

Not traced. The list's element slot holds the generic `id`, read through two aliases (`c` in the
frame, `v` in the lambda), and the fixed-type `inc` written at index 1. A call of `id` at
`string` in the same lambda cannot reach the list. The trap is inside the lambda, so one of the
two calls through `ys` goes through a closure built at another signature than the call's.
