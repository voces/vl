#### Mechanism

A negation is a checker-only refinement with no rep of its own, so a binding annotated with one
should take the rep its INITIALIZER gives it — which is what the module-global face does since
D1775. `checkLocalI32` instead asks `tyScalarBasePrim` of the annotation, gets neither `i32`
nor `boolean` for a `TyNeg`, and refuses.

**Both spellings refuse here, before and after D1775.** That is what separates this from its
parent: D1775's ingredient was the ALIAS, and this one has no alias in it. D1775's close
reconciled the two faces at this position — the alias stopped being invalid wasm and joined
the inline spelling's loud refusal — and left this gap standing at both.

#### What the refusal forecloses

The measurements above are kept as the record of what a written negation would have cost had
it stayed legal — they are what the ruling decided against, not a gap still owed. `A & !B`
remains legal and unchanged: there the negation is an intersection OPERAND, subtracted from
the operand beside it, and the annotation's own type is the positive remainder.
