### D1773 — a negation-annotated LOCAL takes no rep from its initializer, at BOTH the alias and the inline spelling

**loud emit reject · `emitProgram: only i32 locals are supported` · clause 2 · filed
2026-09-06 against master `be5a79fd2` · residue of [D1775](D1775.md), named by its matrix**

Repro:

    function go() {
      const x: !string = 5
      print(x)
    }
    go()
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: emit error,
    // emitProgram: only i32 locals are supported

#### Mechanism

A negation is a checker-only refinement with no rep of its own, so a binding annotated with one
should take the rep its INITIALIZER gives it — which is what the module-global face does since
D1775. `checkLocalI32` instead asks `tyScalarBasePrim` of the annotation, gets neither `i32`
nor `boolean` for a `TyNeg`, and refuses.

**Both spellings refuse here, before and after D1775.** That is what separates this from its
parent: D1775's ingredient was the ALIAS, and this one has no alias in it. D1775's close
reconciled the two faces at this position — the alias stopped being invalid wasm and joined
the inline spelling's loud refusal — and left this gap standing at both.

#### Grading list

The seventeen positions of `negation-alias-binding.matrix.vl` that refuse after D1775 — every
one is a local, a block-scoped binding, or a container element whose rep the annotation does
not name. A close must not move the module-global positions, which run.
