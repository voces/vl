#### Mechanism

Under B′, `k` is renamed to `inner` in `twoTypes`' template, before cloning. `inner` captures `x: T`. With `twoTypes` at two types and `k` called at two types, the clone at `string` reads `x` at `i32`: invalid wasm in `twoTypes$1`. The elaboration's answer is the template's `inner` at every key. A rewrite in a shared template has to name the same instance under each enclosing key.
