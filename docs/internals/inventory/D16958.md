### D16958 — a reassigned `let` of a `const` record pushed onto a list adopted at a wider record does not settle: one adoption link per re-check

**loud check reject · clause 2 (the design permits the program; the refusal names the checker's re-check budget, a mechanism) · open · the same sentence as [D16566](D16566.md) on another shape · found driving `recHeldLetWritesAt` with fn-coverage · filed 2026-11-18 against master `665aeae36` by lane F2**

Repro:

    type J = { f: i32 | null }
    const xs = [{ f: 4 }]
    const wx: J[] = xs
    const o = { f: 9 }
    let r = { f: 6 }
    r = o
    xs.push(r)
    print(xs[1].f ?? 0)
    // vl check: rc 1, "the type of `o` is settled by what it is delivered to, through more re-checks than the checker runs; annotate `o`"

Want:

    9

#### Mechanism

The literal pin phase (`pinRun("literal")`, budget 2) pins only the adoption demands the previous
re-check produced, and a binding's demand appears only once something is delivered into an already
adopted binding. Round 1 adopts `xs` at `J[]`; its re-check makes `xs.push(r)` a delivery of `r`
into `J[]`, so round 2 adopts `r` at `J`; its re-check makes `r = o` a delivery of `o` into `J`,
which needs a third round. A chain of k links needs k rounds: with the budget raised to 6 on a probe
seed, this witness and a four-link chain both print `9`.
