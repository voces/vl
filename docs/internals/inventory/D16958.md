Repro:

Want:

    9

#### Mechanism

The literal pin phase (`pinRun("literal")`, budget 2) pins only the adoption demands the previous
re-check produced, and a binding's demand appears only once something is delivered into an already
adopted binding. Round 1 adopts `xs` at `J[]`; its re-check makes `xs.push(r)` a delivery of `r`
into `J[]`, so round 2 adopts `r` at `J`; its re-check makes `r = o` a delivery of `o` into `J`,
which needs a third round. A chain of k links needs k rounds: with the budget raised to 6 on a probe
seed, this witness and a four-link chain both print `9`.
