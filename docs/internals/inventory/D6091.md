#### Mechanism

From the ablation only. D5611's close gives a hole parameter one parameter per argument-type
key in the split plan (`compiler/fn_split.vl`), and declines on a cycle, since the recursive
call would hand itself the slots, and on an exported function, whose signature is what an
importer sees. Either decline leaves D5611's refusal in place.
