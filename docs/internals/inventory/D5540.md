#### Mechanism

The spelling `Pa | Pb | null` had two reps by position. The checker types it `Pa | null`
(D4989), so a binding, parameter or return is the nullable record. A list element or map
value was the union box, because the ref-list and map-value classifiers decide by the
spelling's member count, and each delivery between the two converted. Since D5461's close the
emit canon spells it `Pa|null` (`canonSameShapeNullable`, `check_program.vl`), so every
position is the nullable record and no delivery converts.

The candidate tried was a container arm in `registerCollapsedUnionName` (`emit_collect.vl`)
that minted the row through `registerInlineUnionAt` for a list or map annotation whose cell
is a record. It made both D5461 reads hit the pool, and broke five of eight delivery
positions, each from runs to check-clean invalid wasm. Once the row exists:

* a store of the record binding into the cell drops the box;
* `w != null` on the record global compiles to a box tag compare (`struct.get $box 0`,
  `i32.const 10`), and the row's null tag moves from 8 to 10.

A parameter and return of that spelling, a nested `(Pa | Pb | null)[][]` cell and a map of
such lists kept running.

#### Grading list

The fixture's eight positions, D5461's witness, and the `same-shape-union-into-nullable`
matrix. A compiler that mints a row for the spelling has to keep the cell's rep decided by
type, or give the binding the box as well.
