#### Mechanism

`nulFoldTiedTyAt` (`check_relate_core.vl`), under `nulFoldTieRecordGo`, writes `ft.nInner` and
`ft.aElem` of rows reached from a recursive record's fields so a null-fold wrapper is replaced by its
nullable. A row shared by structure would carry the rewrite to holders outside the record. The close
returns a rebuilt row and stores it in the field, leaving the reached rows unchanged.
