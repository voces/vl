#### Mechanism

`same`'s call `g(s, s)` goes through `{add}` in the instance `inner`'s list call makes and through
`{mul}` in `swap`'s. `same` is a value of a list, so the elaboration's rule for one call through
two sets in two instances (`elabMintFactsIn`, `elabMintValueFns`) marks both sets partial: a
closure of `same` names one instance, whose `g` cannot hold two sets. `{add}` therefore stays a
closure, and `twoCalls`, calling it at two types, has no instance to call. The rule could relax
only where every holder of `same` dispatches, which needs the holders' sets and the arm sets
decided together.
