#### Mechanism

A fresh list binding delivered whole to a list of a wider record adopts
that list (`recAdPushDecl` with `elem`), one level deep. A list of record LISTS bound and delivered
to `J[][]` is not adopted at the inner level, so its inner lists stay `{f: i32}[]` and are handed to
a list of `J[]`. The spread witness above is that whole delivery through a push.
