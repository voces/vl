### D4860 — a union list concatenated with, or spread beside, a list of a wider union has no copy between the two box rows

**loud emit reject · clause 2 · the residue of D4858, the same in the spread spelling · filed 2026-10-15 against master `0e1ea34b1`**

Repro:

    const a: (string | i32 | f64)[] = ["p"]
    const b: (string | i32)[] = [1]
    const xs = a + b
    print(xs.length)
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: emitProgram: the two operands of this list `+` do not resolve to one list rep

Want:

    2

#### Mechanism

Both operands are union-box lists at different ref-list rows. D4858's widening copies a
scalar or record list into a box row, but `spreadSrcBoxes` declines a source that is itself a
union-box list, and no row-to-row copy (or proof that the two backings are one heap type)
exists. `[...a, ...b]` takes the spread's boxing refusal for the same pair.
