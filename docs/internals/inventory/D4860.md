### D4860 — a union list concatenated with, or spread beside, a list of a wider union has no copy between the two box rows

**closed 2026-11-16 · runs, prints `2` · was loud emit reject, `the two operands of this list `+` do not resolve to one list rep` · clause 2 · fixed in emit_classify.vl: every union-box row shares one box heap and boxes a member under its program-global tag, so a union-box list whose members (and `null`) are each a member of the other row's union is already a list of that row's boxes (`rlUnionRowCovers`); `spreadSrcBoxes` takes it as a same-row source, the concat widens into the wider row with no copy, and the spread `[...a, ...b]` builds the same way · `list-concat-union-into-wider-union.matrix.vl` runs 48 of 48 · a pair whose members differ only by a numeric widening (`(string | f64)[]` + `(string | i32)[]`) still refuses, its elements need a conversion · the residue of D4858 · filed 2026-10-15 against master `0e1ea34b1`**

#### Mechanism

Both operands are union-box lists at different ref-list rows. D4858's widening copies a scalar or
record list into a box row, and `spreadSrcBoxes` declined a source that is itself a union-box list.
All union-box rows twin onto one (backing, wrapper) pair over the shared box (`rlTwinSigOf`), and a
member's box carries its program-global tag, so a covered row needs no copy: the fix admits it.
