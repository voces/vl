#### Mechanism

The list stamp (`recListLitBuild`) builds a part reading a fresh record `const` where the other
parts are (D15372), and a spread of a fresh list `const` there too (D16440). An `Index` part,
`s[0]` inline, was neither: the check passed it, and the emitter met the `{x: i32}` record at the
`{x: f64}` join. A bound read, `const a = s[0]`, already ran, since the binding reads the list's
build.

The close reads an element part as the spread is read: `recHeldListSpreadStamp` takes an
`Index` part's list as it takes a `Spread`'s, in both the no-build arm and the arm beside a held
part. So `s` is adopted at the wider list, and its other deliveries at its own type are refused
at check with the shared-record contract, as D16440's handed-on spelling is.
