#### Mechanism

The direct spelling `{[string]: boolean} | {[string]: i32}` is refused at check where an `is`
has to tell the two apart (D2493): both values are one i32 cell, so the two maps share one map
struct and one box tag. Inside the generic the checker sees `V | {[string]: i32}` and has
nothing to refuse; at the pin `V := {[string]: boolean}` the instance's union collapses the
two members onto the one mono map, and the `is` and the box disagree about the member set.
