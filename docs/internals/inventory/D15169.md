#### Mechanism

`holeSlotJudge` reads a binding initialised by a call through the call's returned values (`holeSlotRetJudge`), and that path does not take the lift D15138 gives a record whose field `elabFnFieldListTy` widened. [D15162](D15162.md) is the map spelling.
