#### Mechanism

Not localised. D2868 builds an un-annotated union-element spread at the literal's own union-box
row when every source boxes into it (`spreadOwnBoxRow`); the `i32[]` twin `[...ints, "s"]` runs
that way. For `(f64 | string)` no row answers, so `rlSlotOfTy` of the literal's element, or the
row's `RLK_UNION` kind, is the first thing to print.
