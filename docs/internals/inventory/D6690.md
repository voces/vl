#### Mechanism

From the ablation only. The call retirement of [D2390](D2390.md) is recorded when the checker
walks the then arm and still stands when it walks the else arm, though no path reaches the
else arm through the call. The else arm's own `is` complement is taken from the state at the
condition and is not affected ([D6650](D6650.md) mirrors that in the emitter).
