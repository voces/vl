#### Mechanism

Both walks group a function's arms by `(subject, kind)` and report the group at its FIRST arm.
Where one subject carries TWO chains, the two implementations pick a different member of that
group as "first": on master `eef2e200e`, `python3 scripts/ladder-budget.py --grade
compiler/wasmEmit.vl` names line 13289 and `vl check compiler/wasmEmit.vl --severity info
--json` names 13303, for the one ladder in `emitNumExactTest`.

The COUNT agrees — 19 either way — and so do the tree-wide totals, 398 + 8 on both sides. That
is why neither the ratchet nor `lint-self.sh` can see it: both read totals. Only a per-file
LINE comparison can, and that is the comparison to run whenever either walk changes.

**The agreement suite is a ten-fixture instrument, and the tree has a shape none of them has.**
`tests/vl_kind_ladder_test.ts` compares hit lines over its own fixtures, which is the right
design and is why its header says so; what it cannot do is find a disagreement in a function
no fixture resembles.

Not measured: which of the two positions the rule intends — the census's is the earlier arm —
and whether any other multi-chain subject in the tree has the same shape. The totals agreeing
bounds the damage: at most a re-ordering, never a missing hit.
