### D16880 — a union list concatenated with, or spread beside, a union list whose members differ by a numeric widening is refused at emit

**closed 2026-11-16 · runs, prints `2` · was loud emit reject, `emitProgram: the two operands of this list + do not resolve to one list rep` · clause 2 · fixed in emit_classify.vl and wasmEmit.vl: a union-box row whose members are each a member of the other row's union or widen to one (`rlUnionRowRetags`, `boxWidenAtomFor`, never a narrowing) is copied with each box rebuilt under the wider member (`emitUnionListRetagTop` over `emitBoxAtomRetagMembers`), in `+` either way round and in a spread · `list-concat-union-numeric-member-widened.matrix.vl` runs 48 of 48 · pin: `tests/cases/unions/union-list-concat-numeric-member-widened.vl` · the covered spelling is [D4860](D4860.md) · found closing D4860 · filed 2026-11-16 against master `2a0b71c1a`**

Repro:

    const a: (string | f64)[] = ["p"]
    const b: (string | i32)[] = [1]
    const xs = a + b
    print(xs.length)
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: emitProgram: the two operands of this list `+` do not resolve to one list rep

Want:

    2

#### Mechanism

Both operands are union-box lists, which share one box heap. D4860's fix joins a union list into
another row without a copy when each of its members is a member of that row's union. Here the
`i32` member is not: an element boxed under the `i32` tag is converted to the `f64` arm, so the
copy rebuilds each such box (unbox, widen, rebox under the destination's tag), the per-box retag
a field read already uses (`emitBoxAtomRetagMembers`), now over a list.
