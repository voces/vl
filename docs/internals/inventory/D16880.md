### D16880 — a union list concatenated with, or spread beside, a union list whose members differ by a numeric widening is refused at emit

**open · loud emit reject · `emitProgram: the two operands of this list + do not resolve to one list rep` · clause 2 · the covered spelling (each member of one union a member of the other) is [D4860](D4860.md) · found closing D4860 · filed 2026-11-16 against master `2a0b71c1a`**

Repro:

    const a: (string | f64)[] = ["p"]
    const b: (string | i32)[] = [1]
    const xs = a + b
    print(xs.length)
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: emitProgram: the two operands of this list `+` do not resolve to one list rep

Want:

    2

#### Mechanism

Both operands are union-box lists, which share one box heap. D4860's fix joins a union list into
another row without a copy when each of its members is a member of that row's union. Here the
`i32` member is not: an element boxed under the `i32` tag must be converted to the `f64` arm, so
the copy needs a per-member conversion (unbox the member, widen the scalar, rebox under the
destination's tag), which no element-converting copy builds.

#### Ablation

| ingredient changed | outcome | needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `b` a `string[]` (a scalar list, not a union list) | runs, prints `2` | **yes** |
| `b` a `(string \| f64)[]` (no numeric difference) | runs | **yes** |
| `[...a, ...b]` in place of `a + b` | still refused, the spread's boxing message | no |
