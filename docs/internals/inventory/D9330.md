polynomial since the arm-shape rows and the instance key stopped spelling the record. Pinned at

Fuel is `VL_FUEL=1` guest fuel of one `vl build`, which is deterministic. The ablation that
removes the cost is removing the list or the `null`. The generic's spelling (explicit or
inferred) does not matter.

Under `VL_COMPILE_GC=null`, this witness at depth 20 exits 70 with `filled its 4 GiB heap`, and
depth 19 builds. `tests/vl_compile_gc_test.ts` uses that as its null-collector trap witness;
when this row closes, the test moves to the next open trap.

Grading: the witness prints `false` at every depth, and the row closes when the depth-12 to
depth-16 fuel ratio stops being about 2^4.

**After the wrappers stop spelling the record, 2026-10-21.** Four sites changed.
`tyToEmitNameAt` memoises each record's render within one outermost render once it has visited
32 records, for a record whose walk met no cycle, so a record two fields share is walked once.
`tyEmitNameKey` keys a list or nullable of a deep record as the wrapper around the record's
interned key. `monoNarrowedPinName`, `monoNicheWidenedPinName` and `monoDeclRecListPinName`
decline a list of records, or a nullable record, before rendering it when no arm of the
ladder's pin holds a brace, and a nullable record only renders once its names have matched.
`unionArmSeedSafe` reads a record that two fields share once. Every corpus cell (10,389 under
`distilled/`) and every witness face at depths 6, 8 and 12 builds byte-identically to master's,
and the compiler builds itself byte-identically. Fuel, `VL_FUEL=1`, one build each:
