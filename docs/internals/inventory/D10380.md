#### Mechanism

Not traced. `monoFlowConstCallArgs` follows only a chain of unwritten bindings to the call,
and `monoFlowLetCalls` rewrites only reads of the written `let` itself, so the `const` copy of
a written `let` whose sources are calls and another `let` is rewritten by neither.
