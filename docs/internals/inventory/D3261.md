**closed — the witness prints `28319200`, and its `-O`/`-O3` modules carry no `struct.new` · was a per-call GC allocation (plumb PL-057/058, owner ruling (B) 2026-09-29) · no wrong value, a cost row · filed 2026-09-29 against master `73bbd20bb`**

#### Mechanism

binaryen's `--heap2local` scalarises only an allocation that never leaves its function, and a
`return` is a way out. `slerp` has three callers and is binaryen size ~20, over the `-O` rung's
`--always-inline-max-function-size 16`, so it stayed a call and its `Quat` went on the GC heap
100M times in plumb's squad (2.2 s, 270 MB against 0.6 s, 8 MB for the globals workaround). The
host's escape step (`escape_inline_choice`, lane L4) chose only callees that TAKE a per-call
struct. It now also chooses a small callee that RETURNS one it allocates: a struct no heap
location can hold, of at most 8 number or record fields, called from somewhere it stays; a
tiny loop-free producer may go into run-once code, as `run_once_hot_callees` already allows.

#### Ablation

| ingredient removed | `-O` on master | needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| two of `slerp`'s three call sites (one caller) | melts: binaryen inlines a single caller | **yes** |
| `slerp`'s body down to binaryen size 16 | melts: the rung inlines it | **yes** |
| a `Quat[]` anywhere in the program | still allocates (and does after the fix: D3263) | no |
| the record result (globals, plumb's workaround) | no allocation | **yes** |
