**closed 2026-11-26 by #10441 (lane COV-CLOS), runs and prints `true` · was check-clean invalid wasm, clause 1 · a closure returning a captured hole parameter returns it at the capture slot's row: `fRetStructIdx` takes `fnRetCaptureRow` (emit_collect.vl), the `$fnsig` key's result (`cloRetKeySuffix`) and a binding's read key (`readCloSigKey`) follow it · fixture `tests/cases/closures/hole-param-returned-from-its-capturing-closure-positions.vl` (lambda, named closure, `if` join, block body) · the binding, field and field-closure positions, and a function returning its own parameter, are D17682; an annotated parameter reached through the closure is D17681 · filed 2026-11-26 against master `dfb9a8026` by COV-CALL, found writing D17578's positions**

#### Mechanism

`viaReturn` is keyed at its hole binding `{ f: null }` with `#r.0` = `J` (D15173), so the capture of
`y` is stored at `J`'s row. The closure returns that capture at its own result type, `{f:null}`,
which is another struct. Neither legacy nor the IR converts the stored `J` back to the closure's
result row. D17578's close moves the IR's read of the capture to `J`'s row, which is the start of
the fix; the closure's result is the remaining part. This is the records part of the "place reads
at storage" series.
