#### Mechanism

`canonUnionKeyOfName` and `canonUnionKeyOfAnnName` (check_nj_join.vl) memoise the key by its
TEXT (`cukIds`), and build that text first, every call: `canonUnionKeyText` splits the
spelling into atoms, asks `aliasDeclRowSet` and `unionAliasMembers` of each, splits each
expansion again and concatenates. `canonUnionKeyOfAnnName` renders it twice, once to count the
atoms and once inside `canonUnionKeyOfName`. The L6 steps that moved binding annotations and
`monoAnnPinName` onto the key put this on the per-node classifier paths (`letAnnIsUnion`,
`globalStructIndexSid`, `nameKeyRow`). #3885 (`33e7e5b45`) cost +1.72% for 28 source lines
and #3904 (`26b6fad0c`) +1.62% for 57; `canonUnionKeyText` went from 0.08% to 1.22% inclusive
across #3885. On `7ce930c32`, `canonUnionKeyOfAnnName` is 2.68% inclusive.
