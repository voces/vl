#### Mechanism

`nssTry` (`compiler/emit_rewrite.vl`) splits a `const` read from a map or a scalar list into a
flag and a scalar when every use qualifies. It sorts each `is` over the binding by the pattern's
text: `isVariant == "null"` is the absent test, `isVariant == tyToStr(s)` the present one. The
checker's tested type at the node (`isVarTyIx`) answers both.

#### Close

`nssTry`'s absent arm reads `isTestsNull` (D15256), and its present arm whether the pattern's own
type (`isPatTyIx`) is the split scalar. The witness first filed here never reached `nssTry`: its
`print(v + 1)` disqualifies the split, so it ran either way. The witness above does, at a map
read and at a list `pop`; it is `tests/cases/maps/scalar-read-split-is-present-arm.vl`.
