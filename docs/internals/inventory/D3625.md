#### Mechanism

The escape step (D3261) can only inline a producer, and inlining stops short in three ways:
a producer over its size bound (D3262), a record type some other site stores (D3263, the
test is per wasm type and `--no-inline` per callee), and a producer whose results merge, as
`unit`'s two arms do once inlined (this witness; D3598's loop-carried variable is the same
merge). The fix is the call-boundary ABI ruling (B) names, written as a host step before
the escape step (`scripts/vl-host/src/multivalue.rs`): a producer of a record of at most
eight scalar fields that no write can reach gets a twin per call-site shape, returning the
fields as multi-value results and taking field-only record parameters as fields. A call
whose result only reaches `struct.get`s, a field-only local or parameter, or another twin's
argument calls the twin; every other use keeps the struct. A returned `struct.new` is
deleted, since its fields are already on the stack in order.
