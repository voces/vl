#### Mechanism

D14383 through a literal binding: `recFnBuiltAtDst` reaches `r`'s literal, but `recSitesLandAt`
refused to serve `const a = rec()` from the build at `{x: i64}`, so `r` stayed at `{x: i32}`. The
unused `take2` does not change the outcome. The D14383 change to `recNullWidened` closes both.
