#### Mechanism

`modBankUfcsScope` asks `modSelfFnDeclared` of every row of a module's rename map, and the registry
holds every top-level `self`-function across all modules. A linear scan made each module's UFCS scope
cost its rename rows times the registry's size. The arity-checking reader `modSelfFnTarget` still
walks the registry, since it needs every row with a matching name.
