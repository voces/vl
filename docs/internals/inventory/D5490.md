#### Mechanism

`jwSecondPass` (`compiler/driver.vl`) splices the generated walkers and calls a fresh
`checkProgram`. Its `expInfReset` drops the D3817 hint row pass 1 queued, and `x` now
carries the `i32` annotation pass 1 wrote, so D3817 has nothing to infer and raises no
hint. The walk then sees `const y: i32 = x` against an annotated `x` and reports the author's
annotation as redundant. Removing it leaves `x` with no pin and the program is refused. The
witness program itself runs; the filed defect is the hint stream, which the witness grader
cannot see.
