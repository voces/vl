#### Mechanism

D2201's shape with a string element: the union box classifies the empty literal `i32[]` and its
re-mapping ladder has no `string[]` rung. Traps at all five delivery positions (argument,
annotated binding, return, assignment, struct field).

The close builds an empty `[]` into a union with no `i32[]` arm as the list arm the checker
adopted it as. `recordRepTyAdopt` records the union's first list member on the literal, and
`emitEmptyArrUnionArm` tags and builds that arm (`string[]` and `(string | null)[]` share
tag 9). The checker pins an open list delivered to a union to the same member, so an
un-annotated function returning `[]` into `P[] | B` builds that arm too.

The grid's `union_dest` form, graded before and after at seven element types. `string[]`,
`(string | null)[]`, `P[]` and `string[][]` each went 1 → 27 of 44 runs. The other 17 are the
un-annotated face's `const v = []`, which refuses by design.
