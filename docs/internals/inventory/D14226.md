#### Mechanism

The checker pins the map BINDING, through its annotation or `recAdPushMapDecl`, but the `Map()`
call node keeps its pre-adoption type, and `emitMapNew` builds at the `mapSlot` its destination
threads. An emitter probe compared that seed with the call's own type and found them different
in 39 files: 22 where the seed is slotted and the type is mono, 10 with two different slots, and
7 with different sentinels. By directory: maps 20, unions 10, soundness 3, structs 2,
generics 2, sets 1, closures 1.
