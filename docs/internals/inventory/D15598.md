#### Mechanism

Both arguments are the one singleton `Fn#same`, so `F` binds once. A hole handed `Fn#d` is
bound per call as `Fn#d` (orchestrator ruling), and the call through `a` and the call through
`b` each pick their own instance, as the one-parameter spelling does with a bundle (D15596).
Mono refuses instead. Not yet traced past the message.
