#### Mechanism

literal sits in `deliver`'s return, where nothing delivers that signature to it: no call reaches
`inc0`, so mono emits the template's closure. Neighbours that run: the same literal bound at the
annotation directly, the call bound un-annotated, and a call `v.f(4)` through the binding.
