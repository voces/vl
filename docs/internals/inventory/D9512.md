#### Mechanism

D9460's rung answers a list of the declared record, not a union with the list as a member. A
depth-16 `--names` profile puts the cost under `collectAArrayLit` → `arrLitUnionElemName` and
`arrLitElemName`, spelling the element with `tyToStrObj`, `7·2^depth` characters, and under
`emitDirectCall` → `emitUnionCoerceRefArray` at the call. Without the list literal
(`g("s")`) the cost still grows about sixfold from depth 12 to 16. Under the null collector
the spelling outgrows the 64 MiB object cap at depth 22, which `tests/vl_compile_gc_test.ts`
uses as its trap witness.

Fuel is `VL_FUEL=1` guest fuel of one `vl build`, which is deterministic; the fuel is the
same with D9460's close.
