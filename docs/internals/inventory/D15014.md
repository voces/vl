### D15014 — a hole lambda calling a nested generic at two types, in a generic instanced at two types, is refused at emit

**loud emit reject · `vl check` rc 0 · `a call passes more arguments than the function it calls takes` · clause 2 · one instance of `outer` runs · the call-site twin of [D12213](D12213.md), found auditing mono's in-place callee renames · filed 2026-11-02 against master `c8c9c979` · bucket (d), L1 typed IR, secondary (c) (the 2026-11-14 clause-2 map)**

#### Mechanism

Traced 2026-11-25 by SOUND, not fixed. At emit, the lambda `g`'s call `inner(v)` is a direct
call whose target is `inner$1`, a slot whose `FuncDecl` has no parameters, while `inner<U>(u: U)`
takes one. That fits a pruned template stub (a template no instance replaced). So one of mono's
in-place callee renames of the lambda body that both instances of `outer` share points the call
at the wrong slot. A close gives each instance its own call target or its own copy of the body:
the D12213 class, not a local fix. The shape is D12213's: a call inside a lambda whose body every instance of `outer`
shares, renamed per instance by one of mono's in-place callee renames (`monoMakeInstanceHit`,
`monoMakeInstanceLater`, `monoSpecializeConcrete`, `monoLamCallByName`, `monoRespellLamFe`). The
direct call, the lambda calling `inner` at one type, and a `const` alias of `inner` in the lambda
all run at two instances of `outer`.
