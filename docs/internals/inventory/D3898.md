#### Mechanism

The callback's own pin was never the problem. The render `{v:{x:i32}}` reached
`structIndexOfTypeName`, which matches a shape spelling to a row by its field-name set, tightened
per field by `shapeFieldTypeCompatK`. A field type that is itself an inline record (`{x:i32}`)
classifies to no field code, and that arm was lenient against every row field except a struct.
So `{ v: A[] }` (here, `Box<A[]>`'s row), or any row with a field `v` that is not a struct,
claimed the spelling first. That one gap gives a loud refusal at a field read, an unresolvable
signature for std's `sort`, and check-clean invalid wasm where nothing reads through the
mis-resolved row (D3915).

Closed by refuting the match: an inline record is a struct field or a union box's arm, so a whole
shape is compatible only with those two codes. The canonical-key check still chooses among
struct rows.
