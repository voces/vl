#### Mechanism

The closure sibling of [D17086](D17086.md). A read narrowed out of a union box to `F | null`, a
nullable closure, is delivered as the box itself: no arm converts the box's null tag to the
closure's `ref.null` and any other tag to its payload cast to the closure struct. A null test and a
call through the read in place ran; every copy (a binding, an argument, a return) did not.
