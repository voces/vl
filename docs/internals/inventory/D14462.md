#### Mechanism

`fnWriteShapes` and `fnWriteEffects` (`compiler/check_write_effects.vl`) find the call graph's
strongly connected components on demand. A frame that reached a shallower open frame stayed
provisional, but the members recorded under it kept the depth of the frame they first reached.
Once that frame closed, a later ask from a sibling read such a member's low as below nothing
open, settled as a root, and memoised the member's partial sum. A second hazard sat in the
rounds: a member's re-walk that reached a frame below the root was discarded when the rounds
restored `weLow`. Either way the settled summary depended on ask order and could miss a write.
