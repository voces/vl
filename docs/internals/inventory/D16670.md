### D16670 — an `as!` cast of a float constant is folded as an exact constant and takes an `i64` destination's width

**check-clean silently wrong · clause 1 · open · `vl check` rc 0 · E9.1 lists no cast among a constant's operators, so `(2.0 as! i32)` is a typed `i32`, and D3043 keeps a fitting constant beside it at `i32` · the decimal spelling `(2 as i32)` and a typed value both wrap at `i32` · found by the independent oracle's arithmetic generator (fuzz cell `ar_s91_0048`, `gen.ts --seed 91`) · filed 2026-11-15 against master `93ad5a25c`**

Repro:

    const x: i64 = (2.0 as! i32) * 2000000000
    print(x)
    // master: vl check rc 0, prints 4000000000

Want:

    -294967296

#### Mechanism

Not ablated to a compiler function. The `i32` product wraps (`2 * 2000000000` is past
`i32`'s range) and is then delivered to `i64`. Master folds `2.0 as! i32` to the constant `2`,
and the tree `2 * 2000000000` then computes exactly at the `i64` destination.
