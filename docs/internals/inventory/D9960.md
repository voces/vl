#### Mechanism

`type L = X` with a non-record body parses as a one-member union, and `singleAliasMemberTyIx`
decides which such aliases denote their member. Its list arm admitted a list by its leaf:
scalar, declared struct, map, inline record, declared alias. A function leaf was in none of
them, so `L` kept its own one-member union row, and the member check asked for `length` on a
union whose one member is the list. D3298 is the same arm missing a union or nullable leaf.
