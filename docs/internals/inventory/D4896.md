**closed 2026-10-15 — now prints `true`, `1` · was check-clean silently wrong, clause 1 (the second union's `is` answered false, its `match` took the wrong arm) · the ref-list slot key folded a `boolean[]` union member onto `i32[]`, so the two unions shared one slot and its element name; a boolean-leaf list member now keys unfolded · pre-existing on master; both spellings annotated · found closing [D4894](D4894.md) · filed 2026-10-15 against master `8b65df0a7` · pin: `tests/cases/unions/bool-list-arm-union-elements-key-apart.vl`**

#### Mechanism

`boolean[]` and `i32[]` share one list storage but are distinct union arms with their own
box tags ([D2769](D2769.md)). The outer list's ref-list slot is interned on the `elem`-mode
rep key (`rlInternNameTy`), and that mode folds every `i32`-leaf list to `$rlI32L`, inside
union members too (`repKeyUnion`, `hcUnionId`). So `(string[] | boolean[])` and
`(string[] | i32[])` got one slot, and `unionNameOfExpr` read the first-registered union's
name for the second: its `is i32[]` found no such arm and folded to false, and with `f64[]`
arms its literal was even built under the wrong union. A boolean-leaf list member now keys in
the unfolded render (`repUnionMemberMode`, `compiler/emit_rep.vl`). The same fix covers a
parameter, a push, a record field, `for`, `match`, `boolean[][]` against `i32[][]` and
`(boolean | null)[]`. Found while closing: [D4902](D4902.md).
