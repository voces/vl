#### Mechanism

D9513's close answers a function-typed parameter's `closure` token without its render only
when the function's result is a scalar, a literal, a record, a function or a list. A result
spelled `string | null` or `i32 | null` renders as a union, and the render of a function
with a union result is the render of a union whose member is the function, which keys a
union box and not a closure, so the rung declines it. `sigKeyOfTy` → `sigParamTokOfTy` then
renders the parameter with `tyToEmitName`, `7·2^depth` characters, and `annParamKindTy`
classifies it: a depth-17 `--names` profile puts 84% of samples under `annParamKindTy`, in
`tyTopIndexOf`, `nullablePartOf` → `isUName` → `canonUnionKeyOfName`, `nameIsRefArray` and
`nameIsNulI32List`, reached from `collectCloSigs` and from the start function's
`calleeRetKindAt`. With `g` never called the build is polynomial.
