Repro:

#### Mechanism

A record shared between an own-type binding and a destination that stores a number wider has no
conversion, and the checker refuses it: `{j: {f: i32}} is not K: they store field `` differently,
and a record is shared, not copied` for `J = { f: f64 }`. The refusal does not look through
`| null`, at a top-level field, an inner one or an outer one, so the delivery passes check and the emitter
hands `K` a record of `mk`'s own row.
