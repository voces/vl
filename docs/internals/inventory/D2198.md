#### Mechanism

`const MODE = "debug"` has the widening literal type `"debug"` (D2157). A compare now asks, before
the operands widen, whether one side is such a literal (or a join of them) and the other is a
string literal, a declared string-literal type, or another such `const`, with no member in common
(`constLitNeverMeets` in `typecheck.vl`); then it refuses, naming the declaration that would make
the test mean something. An `is "lit"` test over the `const` is held to the same rule, and
`is string` over it reads it as the `string` it is. The rule does not reach a side typed `string`,
a `let`, a nullable literal type (which the compare already refuses on its own), or a literal
spelled with an escape, whose lexeme is not its value. `match` cannot take a string scrutinee, so
it has no arm to refuse. A `string` annotation on such a `const` is the escape, so it is no longer
reported as a redundant annotation.

#### Grading list

Both operand orders, `!=`, `const` against `const`, `const` against a declared literal parameter, a
join of `const`s, and `is "lit"` in the refused fixture; the suggested declarations, a `string`
annotation, a runtime string, a `let`, a member of the other side, an escape spelling and
`is string` in the allowed one.
