**closed · runs, prints `1` · was loud emit reject, clause 2 · graded CAPABILITY: `k?.has("a")` over the same receiver already ran, and the checker types an optional-chained method call generally, so the refusal at `?.length` is about a field read, not a call · closed in emit_rewrite.vl `drwWalkMemberCall`: under `?.` the map `get` arm takes a fresh receiver at the map's non-null type and hands the index read `k[key]` to the `?.` guard · fixture `maps/optional-chain-get-nullable-map.vl` (scalar, string, nullable, record and `f64` values, a field receiver, a list loop, a generic `look<V>` at two types), matrices `optchain-map-get-nullable-map` (0 → 66 of 66) and `optchain-map-get-nullable-map-generic` (0 → 66 of 66) · the generic `k?.get("a") ?? d` at a scalar pin still refuses, a different mechanism, [D10130](D10130.md) · found closing [D9990](D9990.md), whose grading list held it · filed 2026-10-22 against master `2c4263380`**

#### Mechanism

`drwWalkMemberCall` (emit_rewrite.vl) rewrites a map's `m.get(k)` to the index read `m[k]`,
gated on the receiver node being typed as a map. Under `?.` that node is the nullable map, so
the arm declined and the call reached the method-call emitter with a member callee it cannot
call: as an operand of `??` the guard's present arm was a void call, bound first or at a
`string` value the callee was not a function name. The arm now takes, under `?.`, a clone of
the receiver typed at the map's non-null type, which is what the guard's present arm reads it
as, and returns the index through `drwOptGuard` as before.
