#### Mechanism

The trap was the null collector's whole heap, not one object. wasmtime reports a heap that
cannot grow past its 4 GiB index space and an object past 64 MiB as the same
`AllocationTooLarge`. The host's note named only the 64 MiB cap, so the oversized object was
looked for and not found. At `eac4a9fe5` the trap's heap capacity is exactly 4 GiB. The failing
call (`bigShl1` under `ieeeBytes`) just happened to be the allocation that crossed the line.
Three tables were re-allocated per entity and regrown to the size of the whole program's
symbol-id space, which made the allocation quadratic. `plBuildLetMap` (emit_base.vl) replaced
`plLxHead` and `plMoreHead` with `[]` on every per-function plan build, and `sidArrPut` refilled
them with -1 up to the sid being written. `internInlineShapeTy` (emit_classify.vl) dropped its
re-entrancy memo after each outermost intern, and the next intern regrew it to the shape
spelling's sid. On sunpa that came to 85M of 88M `sidArrPut` slot pushes over 51,622 plan
builds against 11,413 sids. Each table is now cleared at the slots its last build wrote. Float
literals were the other large linear cost: `ieeeBytes` rebuilt bignums for each of sunpa's
99,025 literal emits. It is memoised by lexeme and width now (`ieeeBytesMemo`, registered in
the memo census).

Measured with a guest profile whose samples carry resident-set growth instead of time
(`VL_PROFILE_GUEST_ALLOC=1`). Under the null collector that growth is the allocation. sunpa's
`game.vl` allocation went from 3.50 GB to 2.31 GB. Its peak RSS with `-O3` went from 3.46 GB to
2.31 GB, against a 4.29 GB cap. The compiler's self-compile, which `refresh-compiler.sh` forces
onto the null collector, went from 4.10 GB to 3.40 GB peak RSS: master stood within 5% of the
trap on its own source. Output is byte-identical. The fixpoint holds from master's seed in two
compiles. sunpa's `game.wasm` and its source map, built with `-O3` and with no `-O`, are
identical to master's.

Still standing after this close: [D3739](D3739.md) at 850 MB of the self-compile, and
`rsIxReady` (emit_classify.vl). `rsIxReady` rebuilt its ten whole-arena columns and two
string-keyed maps 8 times on sunpa, about 490 MB, each time `P.nodes` grew between two
`objLitBindDestRow` questions. It is linear per rebuild, and its witness is unknown.
