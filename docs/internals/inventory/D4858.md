### D4858 — a list `+` whose operands are of two reps, one assignable to the other, had no converting copy

**closed 2026-10-15 · runs · was loud emit reject, clause 2 · found doing L2 §13.29, every cell of three 48-cell position matrices (`list-concat-box-string-right`, `list-concat-box-widen-record`, `list-concat-scalar-widen`) refused before · filed 2026-10-15 against master `0e1ea34b1` · pins: `tests/cases/lists/list-concat-widen-operand.vl`**

Repro:

    const xs = ["p", 1] + ["q"]
    print(xs.length)
    print(xs[2])
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: emitProgram: the two operands of this list `+` do not resolve to one list rep

Want:

    3
    q

#### Mechanism

The checker types `a + b` at the operand the other is assignable to, but the emitter's concat
(`catListKindOfBin`) admitted only two operands of one rep and one ref-list row, so a
`string[]` beside a `(string | i32)[]`, an `i32[]` beside an `f64[]`, or a `Cx[]` beside a
`(Cx | Dx)[]` fell to the refusal. `catWidenSideOfBin` now names the narrower operand, and
`emitListConcatG` copies it into the wider rep first: boxed into a union-box row by the spread's
`emitSpreadGroupBoxed`, or converted by `emitScalarListWidenTop`. The copy needs no write licence,
since a concat is a snapshot of both operands anyway; `refListElemNameOfExpr` gives the
concat the wider operand's row, which is what an un-annotated binding of it reads.

#### Ablation

| ingredient removed | outcome | needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the operands' rep difference (`["p", 1] + ["q", 2]`) | runs | **yes** |

| the literal operands (named bindings) | still refused | no |
| the union (`[1.5] + [2]`, two scalar reps) | still refused | no |

#### Grading list

The three `list-concat-*` templates in `scripts/capability-probes/matrix/` at every position,
both faces. Pairs that stay refused have rows of their own: a union list into a wider union
(D4860) and nested-list widening (D4861). `string[] + i32[]` is a design refusal (`cannot
concat`), since neither operand is assignable to the other.
