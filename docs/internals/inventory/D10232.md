#### Mechanism

The binder pairs a union pattern's members with the argument's. `v` is narrowed non-null, but
its spelling still carries the `| null` the map read gave it, so the pattern's open map atom was
handed a union of a map and `null` and bound nothing. That `null` is now left out of what an
open map atom binds.

#### Ablation

On master every spelling refuses through [D10171](D10171.md), the direct `h(mk(), "q")` included,
so this row is the delivery position a D10171 fix still refuses unless it strips the `null`:
binding the map member against the argument as spelled ran `h(mk(), "q")` and refused here.
