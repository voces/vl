### D6544 — a hole call `a(2.5, 1)` of a declared generic `same<T>(x: T, y: T)` is refused at emit as two instances

**closed 2026-10-18 by lane L1 · runs, prints `1` · was a loud emit reject · clause 2 · the checker instanced a several-parameter generic once per argument, so `a(2.5, 1)` bound `f64` and `i32` as two instances; it now takes the whole call's instance in argument order, and `1` adopts `f64` as at the direct call · pin: `tests/cases/generics/hole-call-of-generic-at-its-own-instance.vl` · found closing [D6540](D6540.md) · filed 2026-10-18 against master `fc3856155`**

Repro:

    function same<T>(x: T, y: T): T { y }
    function via(a) {
      print(a(2.5, 1))
    }
    via(same)
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: emitProgram: monomorphize: `a` is called at two instances of the function handed to it in a call to `via`

#### Mechanism

From the ablation only. There is one call. The literal `1` adapts to `f64` in the direct call
and at the hole's check, but the emitter keys the call by `i32` and `f64` for the two
arguments and reads them as two instances of `same`.
