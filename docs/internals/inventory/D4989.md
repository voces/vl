#### Mechanism

The reverse of how the row was first worded. `A` and `B` have one shape, so the arena collapses
`A | B` to that one record, and `A | B | null` to `A | null`. The emitter still registers the
spelling `A | B` (`registerCollapsedUnionName`), so an `A | B`-typed value is a union BOX, while
the `A | B | null` slot is the nullable record. The delivery into a bare `A` already unboxed;
the delivery into the nullable record did not, because `narrowedArmNeedsUnbox` declined every
expression that was not a narrowed struct arm. It now also asks when the checker typed the
expression as one record or as `record | null`, and the cast is nullable for the latter. The
module-global initializer gained the same unbox the global assignment arm had.

A second defect sat under the direct spelling (`u = b` with `const b: B`): a global annotated
`A | B | null` whose init is `null` had no struct row for its cell (`ref valtype with no
interned shape`) unless some other annotation registered `A | B`. `structIndexOfLet` now falls
back on the annotation's non-null arena type.
