### D15056 — `==` over a list of `f32 | null` is refused as having no lowering

**closed · runs, prints `true` · was a loud check reject, clause 2 · `nulBoxedNumTy` admits `f32`; a float-literal operand (`a == [1.5, null]`) builds an `(f64 | null)[]` and the leaf-key test refuses the pair loudly, before the emitter (D15055's mixed-rep pair) · matrix `nullable-f32-element-list-eq` · the `f32` member the nullable-number list compare (#5936) leaves out · filed 2026-11-02 against master `92b47e1f1`, closed 2026-11-03**

Repro:

    const a: (f32 | null)[] = [1.5 as f32, null]
    const b: (f32 | null)[] = [1.5 as f32, null]
    print(a == b)
    // was: vl check, "`==` over (f32 | null)[] has no lowering"

#### Mechanism

`(i32 | null)[]`, `(f64 | null)[]` and `(i64 | null)[]` compare through the union-box element
class. `f32` is left out of the checker's admission (`nulBoxedNumTy`) because a float-literal
operand (`a == [1.5, null]`) builds an `(f64 | null)[]`, a second rep, which would then reach the
emitter as `the two operands of this list == do not resolve to one comparable list rep`. Admitting
`f32` needs the literal operand built at the other side's element type first.
