#### Mechanism

`mfrChainDescend` (`mfr_shadow.vl`) follows the chain of bindings and returns from a read back to
the function it holds, and answers "a link is unknown" once `hops > 24`. A chain of 25 module-level
aliases, or a 128-deep chain of functions each returning its argument, crosses it, the read is not
re-pointed at the function's instance, and emit refuses the generic function as a value even though
its call sites pin it. Raising that one limit (a scratch build) makes the 25-alias, 128-alias and
128-return chains print `3`; raising `check_scopes.vl`'s `hops < 128` does not.
