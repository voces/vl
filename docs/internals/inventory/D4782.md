#### Mechanism

#3482 (`0b5ec267c`, sunpa SP-056) changed `emitListIdxGuard`, `emitListIdxGuardHoisted` and
`emitListIdxTrap` in `compiler/wasmEmit.vl` from `array.get backing (select i -1 (i <u len))`
to `if (i >=u len) { array.get backing -1 }` followed by `array.get backing i`. Its win was
measured on the sunpa track under V8. The bench harness runs `vl run`, which is wasmtime.

#### Fix

The trap branch keeps its bounds trap (the read at -1, or a record list's copy to `len`) and
then ends in `unreachable`. A branch that fell through was a path on which the index stayed
unchecked, so `-O` had to keep every later check of it. Now binaryen drops those and moves the
one trap block out of the loop. The message is still the engine's `out of bounds array access`.
