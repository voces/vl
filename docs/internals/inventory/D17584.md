### D17584 — a missed read of an un-annotated map of two-signature bundles is held at the non-null bundle record, so its `null` check traps

**trap after load · clause 1 · open · `vl check` rc 0 · found by COV-CLOS checking whether the bundle holder's dropped `| null` is reachable (coordinator's check (a) for the nullable-holder step) · filed 2026-11-25 against master `9ab0eb7bd`**

Repro:

Want:

#### Mechanism

`m`'s mv slot stores `lap`'s two-signature bundle record (D17094). A binding of a read of it is held at that record whole (the bundle holder, `letCellKind` and `holderRecordTy`), which drops the read's `| null`. A missing key therefore reaches a non-null cell, and the `null` test or the store traps instead of taking the `else`. `m.get("z")` traps the same way. The IR does not claim the reading code on master, so these are legacy's bytes. The IR's own holder (`holderRecordTy`) drops the `| null` the same way, which is the nullable-holder step COV-CLOS has open. Not ablated into the emitter further.
