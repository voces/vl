#### Mechanism

Both maker bindings hold the one lambda node the maker returns, so they share one function-value
class, which closes at one signature drawn from both calls (`eplHolderTy`, reached through
`elabSlotTyAt`). Where the maker returns its lambda literal directly, each instance's lambda is
lifted at its own pins and the call is keyed at its checked types (`cloMakerBindingSigKey`).
Through a local `const`, both instances' lambdas are lifted at the class's one signature, and the
`i32` call finds no closure built for it. Each maker call builds a fresh closure at its own pins,
so each call's binding wants a class of its own.
