#### Mechanism

An un-annotated parameter's member call grows the hole's demanded shape (`{loadI32: _}`), and
`holeDemandOk` judged the argument against it, where a field is required. The shape now drops the
members the argument's type answers by a type-bound `self`-function (`shapeLessTypeBound`,
D4033's `typeBoundSelfFnOf`) before that judgement. The call itself was already resolved per
instance by D4033's rung, in the checker and in mono's pin.
