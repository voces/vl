### D16134 — a `filter` callback over a list of a kept maker's build is instanced at the element's own type, so the read is refused

**closed 2026-11-14 · runs, prints `null` · was a loud check reject, clause 2, `this container's {f: null} elements are stored at J, the row its stores build; handing it on as {f: null}[] is type-valid but not supported yet` · closed by keying the callback's instance by the list's build in the elaboration (D16133's ruling, option (ii), stage 1) · in D16133's class, a decision made after its consumer · filed 2026-11-14 against master `b8b695bf`**

#### Mechanism

`xs` holds `mk2()`'s build, `J`, which `recStoresAgree` decides in check_program's post-pass,
after the callback is typed at the element `{f: null}`. The close keys the late fact in the
elaboration. `elabCbAtBuild` (`emit_elab.vl`) enqueues the callback's instance with a `#r.0`
entry at the receiver's build: a lambda's own key, or a named hole function's beside its hole
binding. A lambda inside an open declaration is keyed under each of its instances. Mono reads
the row (`elabCbParamRowTy`) and decides nothing. The `filter` or `slice` result holds the same
elements, so the elaboration stamps the call at the build (`elabSameSlotsAtBuild`), which
`mfResultSlotOf` reads. The check's floor (`recBuiltCallbackRefuse`) keeps only what the
elaboration does not key: a parameter annotated at another type, and a `map` whose result holds
the record ([D16211](D16211.md)).
