**runs today and must keep running · the IR claims it since COV-CALL's capture-row PR (a hole parameter's capture is read at the row its slot stores), so `VL_IR_REQUIRE_CLAIM=1` builds it · was: the IR declined the closure's call (`convert record -> record`) · check-clean invalid wasm inside `g` before D17660's close · filed 2026-11-26 against master `fa5d6e86e` by SOUND**

#### Mechanism

`g` is keyed at its hole binding `{ f: null }` with `#r.0` = `J` (D15173), and D17660's close stamps
the read of `y` inside `b` at `J`, so `takeAny` is keyed with `#r.0` = `J` and legacy passes the `J`
it holds. The IR's delivery of that read into `takeAny`'s parameter converts from the read's rep to
another record rep and finds no retag; the same call from a list callback's closure (D17660) is
claimed. Not traced further.
