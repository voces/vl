#### Fix

The anchor is resolved from the node, not chased to its mint: `emitErrTokIx`
(`compiler/driver.vl`) takes a node still holding the parser's final cursor to its recorded source
(`nodeSrc`), else to its first child in source order that has an anchor of its own (a member
read's object, a call's callee), and only then to the cursor at the failure. The `.length`
Member's object is the call `xs[0]("abc")` as parsed, on line 6.

#### Mechanism

The refusal ([D15010](D15010.md)'s) is anchored at a `.length` Member the instance's body was
rebuilt into after parsing. That node holds the parser's final cursor rather than its source
expression's token, so [D15155](D15155.md)'s fallback names the instance's template instead.
The pass that mints it was not found: wrapping `monoCloneBody`, `monoCloneGenericCalls`,
`monoPinBinOps` and `monoFoldLayoutAt` to adopt their rebuilt nodes' anchors moved nothing.
The fix is the mint adopting its source node's anchor (`adoptNodeTok`), wherever it is.
