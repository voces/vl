#### Mechanism

Not traced. `id` is handed two records chosen at run time, one holding functions of `i32`, one of
`f64`; the elaboration keys one of the two instances, and mono demands the other when it pins
the argument at the type the record's place class closes (D16842's `eplValueSig`).
