Want:

#### Mechanism

`c` is built at `Circle` (`mkcall`'s literal is delivered into a `Circle` sink) and `k` at `K`.
`rel(c, { f: k })` binds `T` at two records, so the checker marks the call's rep row mixed
(`callBindRepMixedAt`): the instance keeps its own types while each argument is pinned at its
rep, which is `a: Circle` and `b: { f: K }`. A body that never relates `a` and `b` runs that way;
this one joins `b.f` and `a` into one `T`, and the two records meet in the `if`.
