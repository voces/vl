#### Mechanism

From the ablation and the driver. A monomorphizer refusal returns at once, so a compile
records one split ask, and each round of `compileSrc`'s loop (`compiler/driver.vl`) plans the
asks seen so far. The loop stops after `FS_ROUNDS` = 4 rounds. Each binding here is asked in a
round of its own, so the fifth is never planned. The generic-through-hole spelling is not
affected.
