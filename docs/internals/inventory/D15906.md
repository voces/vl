### D15906 — a hole lambda's bundle dropped at the top level, or as a `void` function's last statement, is refused at emit

**closed 2026-11-12, runs and prints `1` · was loud emit reject, clause 2, `emitProgram: a function returning a parameter called at two signatures is called where its result is not used at one of them` · found working [D15904](D15904.md) · filed 2026-11-12 against master `96590b46` · closed in the elaboration: every top-level call statement and the last statement of a `void` function's body drop their value (`elabResScanBlock`, emit_elab.vl), as a block's statement before its last already did · fixture `tests/cases/closures/hole-lambda-bundle-handed-on-copied-and-dropped.vl`**

Repro:

    function lap(f) {
      f(2.5)
      f(3)
      return f
    }
    lap((a) => a + 1)
    print(1)

Want:

    1

#### Mechanism

The scan marked a call dropped only as a `Block` statement before the last. Top-level
statements are the `Program`'s, and a `void` function's last statement is no value, so the call
had no member fact and the pick refused.
