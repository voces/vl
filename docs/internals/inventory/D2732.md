#### Mechanism

Closed by `annAdoptRegisteredName` ([D3099](D3099.md)): with that one call removed, the same seed refuses this row's binding cells again (ablated build, 2026-09-28). What follows is the mechanism as filed.

The same `Q | R` value runs at an argument, a `return`, a list element, a `??` and an `if`
join, and refuses at a cell: an annotated binding with `a map value's union arm cannot be told
from another map of its representation`, a function-local `R | Q | string` binding with `only
i32 locals are supported`, a module one with `union atom has no value box`, and a record field
with `has no struct-field rep`. The two maps share one map struct (D2690), so the cell ladders
that key a map-union arm by its struct find two arms at one key. The invalid-wasm spelling the
#3198 review reported for `R | Q | string` delivered from `Q | R` did not reproduce in 29 cells
across ten positions on `90b63b840`; every non-running cell is one of these four refusals.
