### D15104 — a written `let` read back into itself from a nested function, its value stored into another written `let` of a wider set, builds only through the split plan

**runs today and must keep running · split-plan dependent (W family): with the split plan off (`compileSrc` returning after its first compile) it is a loud emit reject, `the reassigned function binding `d1` holds functions called at more than one argument type` · the residue of `closures/cell-peer-called-at-two-types.vl` after W-lam alias slices 1 and 2 (#5993, #6000) · filed 2026-11-03 against master `0ab8a73b0`**

#### Mechanism

Not located past the decline. After the elaboration the decision plans `{g}` and refuses it at `d3 = d1` (`ftCtxOk`'s store arm): the value read holds `{g}` and the binding written holds a wider set, which `ftHeldWider` accepts only once that set is tagged or under decision. Not per-instance: `mk` is annotated and has one instance.
