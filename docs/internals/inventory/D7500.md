#### Mechanism

Whole-root serving (`wiBankWhole`, driver.vl) serves a root only from an importer's passing
check. On diderven, 110 of the 330 files in `src/` are imported by no other file (102 are
tests), so nothing banks them, and each one checks its whole import graph again. Under
`VL_FUEL=1 VL_CHECK_STATS=1` at two workers this is 114 to 119 roots and about 5,300 of
9,250 module checks, against 3,630 served.

A batch check was tried and dropped: one synthetic entry importing several unseen roots, its
banks used to serve each root afterwards. A batch banks nothing when any root in it has a
diagnostic or meets another's demands, so on diderven it banked 1 to 4 roots and the total went
9,446 → 9,597 to 9,631 module checks.

#### Grading list

The fix is to serve a root's imports one module at a time from a shared bank: a module checked
once under the same importer demands is not checked again. Grade it with `vl check src` over a
copy of diderven under `VL_CHECK_STATS=1`, counting `unseen` module checks, at byte-identical
output against master at 1, 2 and 6 jobs.
