#### Mechanism

`nulArmIfThenValueIx` (emit_if_arm_values.vl) returned the chain's first non-null arm as the
representative of every non-null arm, and `exprNulLitUnion` (emit_classify.vl) asked
`exprIsLitAtom` of that arm (and of a prelude join's first arm). So `if {K} else if {i32} else
{null}` was given the `K | null` niche, while its join is `K | i32 | null`, a box. The close
reads the chain's rep off its join at the instance: `nulArmIfThenValueIx` answers only when the
join is one rep made nullable, and `exprNulLitUnion` takes a value `if` from `nodeRepIsNulAtom`.
The first-arm fallback, both `exprNulLitUnion` rungs and `initIsPreludeJoin` are deleted.
