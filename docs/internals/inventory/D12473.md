**loud emit reject · clause 2 · CAPABILITY, the program is type-valid · open · site `emitProgram: field access receiver is not a struct` · found by lane L3 grading the D1433 rule · present at `4e8c0207c` · filed 2026-10-24 against master `38621e99f`**
