#### Mechanism

DECISIONS.md §"`void` is a real type in the lattice" (d) rules `void` non-storable, with no `void | i32`. D4052's close refused `void` inside a list or map; a union with `void` at the top of a parameter, binding, field or result annotation was the half it left. The owner ruled on 2026-10-24 that (d) covers every written `T | void`, a result annotation included.

#### Close

`voidStoreCheck` (`compiler/check_relate_core.vl`) asks `tyJoinsVoidAt` (`compiler/tyquery.vl`) for the first union or nullable with a `void` member that an annotation is or holds (through a function type's parameters and result, a list element, a map key or value) and refuses it at the annotation, naming `T | null` as the spelling to write. An un-annotated body mixing `return 1` with a bare `return` was already refused by the checker (D1600); its note now points to `T | null` too.
