### D15870 — a fresh call held in a record literal's union field, where the record stores the member's field more widely, builds the field raw

**closed 2026-11-12, runs and prints `1` · was check-clean invalid wasm, clause 1, master `544adb4cb` · the literal's kept-call rep (`litKeptObjRep`, emit_elab.vl) replaced the field's type with the call's built record, so a union field lost its box; a union field now keeps its storage and the call is boxed under its member · fixture `tests/cases/soundness/fresh-call-into-union-of-wider-record-runs.vl` · found closing D15828 · filed 2026-11-12 against master `544adb4cb`**

    type J = { r: i32 | null }

#### Mechanism

`mkU` is called once, so its literal is built at the member `J` the field's union lands it on.
The record literal around it is stamped `W`, but the elaboration re-derives a literal holding a
kept call's result as the literal's rep with each such field replaced by the call's record
(`litKeptObjRep`), which turned `f: J | string` into `f: J`: the literal was built at a row of
the raw record, and the binding's `W` slot did not take it.
