#### Mechanism

A binding's cell row comes from its rep slot, and for an arm that slot is
`repVariantSlotRep`, which folds a variant row onto the first earlier row that
`repVariantSlotsTwin` calls its twin. That pairwise rung folded two arms that share a
field-name signature and are told apart by a literal field, without D2155's requirement that
both arms belong to one union. `Tri` therefore took `Named`'s heap, while `TW2.g` and the
literal stayed at `Tri`'s own row, which `buildVariantTwins` had kept apart.

D2155's module-level witness, which runs on master through the global storage ladder and goes
to invalid wasm when global storage reads the rep slot unless this is fixed.
