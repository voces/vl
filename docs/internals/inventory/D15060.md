#### Mechanism

of the slot's set, so the two sets are decided together as D15036's literal elements are
(`ftSlotWriteSet`). The map spelling has its own fixture,
`function-list-element-at-two-types-beside-wider-map-set.vl`. Other ways of handing the member to
the wider holder (a record pushed onto a list of records, a hole helper's push, a spread) are
D15062's.

Was: as D15036, whose close decides `{inc0}` together with `{inc0, dbl0}`; here the wider set's plan
declines, inferred from the push into its holder, so `{inc0}` again stays a closure called at
`i32` and `f64` with no refusal. A set left a closure while its calls disagree on a signature
owes a loud refusal at least.
