#### Mechanism

`top.vl` (served) asks for the write shapes of `report` (in `lib.vl`, re-typed). The whole check
answers through `hook`'s targets, which include `w` because `install` stores it, so the banked
answer carries `w`'s element write. The partial check types only S (`lib.vl` and what it reaches
back to), its `cwFnValueTargets` sees no store into `hook` because `install` is in a served
module, and its answer is a strict subset. `skQsMoved` (`compiler/driver.vl`) compares the two
and declines. The compiler's own instance: `check_operators.vl` (served) asks for `tErr`'s
shapes (`check_relate_core.vl`), whose banked answer reaches `checkerForceFn`, stored into
`checkerForce` by served `check_program.vl` inside `checkProgramPass`; that is why
`check_relate_core.vl` and `check_fit_rules.vl` decline under a no-op edit.

#### Why it is sound

The banked answer is the complete one: it was computed by a whole check that saw every store.
A store into a binding of S made from S itself is in the partial check's own reach, a change to
S's writers is caught by `SK_WRITER` (13), and a change on the served side moves the banked
answer itself, which `skQsMoved` compares. Measured on the witness: skipping `skQsMoved` serves 2
modules and the partial check's diagnostics and exit code equal the whole check's, with the
narrowing used either side of the call (`use` in `top.vl`, or the same body in `lib.vl`), and with
an `i32` annotation that makes both checks refuse the stale `a[0]`.
