#### Mechanism

Not ablated. The join is the union `{j: {a: i32}} | {j: null}`, and its arms are built at `K`. The
push's argument is coerced into the union box (`emitUnionBox`'s `if` arm), which does not read the
join's stamp at `K`, so the box reaches a list of `K`.
