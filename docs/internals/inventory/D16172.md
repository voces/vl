### D16172 — `is Set<T>[]` over a union of a list of sets and a scalar has no interned arm

**loud emit reject · clause 2 · `` emitProgram: `is` names a declared union member with no interned arm representation (deferred value-union composition) `` · found closing D16047 (lane F) · filed 2026-11-14 against master `a1e5fe53d`**

Repro:

    const s: Set<i32> = Set()
    const u: Set<i32>[] | i32 = [s]
    print(u is Set<i32>[])

Want:

    true

#### Mechanism

Not traced. The union's list-of-sets arm is not interned as a member the `is` test can name, so the
test refuses where the list-of-maps twin (`{[i32]: i32}[] | i32`) runs.
