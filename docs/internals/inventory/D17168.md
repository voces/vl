**runs today and must keep running · an IR coverage defect · filed 2026-11-24 against master `aef0be317` by lane IR-N2 (census class `start: field read coercion`, family N, 1 cell)**

Repro:

#### Mechanism

`buildMember` (`ir_build_ref.vl`) declines a plain struct field read when the field's kid rep is
not the read's rep: here two different record reps (measured with a probe build naming both). Legacy
builds the read; which decision gives the read its rep is the fix's to establish.

| ingredient changed | outcome | needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `const _dA: J = mk2()` removed | covered | **yes**, the annotation |
| a plain binding (`const v = mk2()`) in place of the map read | covered | **yes**, the map read |
| `__mv.g` read directly, no outer record | covered | **yes**, the outer record |
| an extra field `b: 1` in the outer record | still `field read coercion` | no |
