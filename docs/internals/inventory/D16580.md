#### Mechanism

`elabLamDeliveredParams` called `elabSlotDeliveries` for the lambda, and the walk demands the
instances that the lambda's calls reach as it climbs. The classes answer the same parameter
list without demanding anything, so the instance of `pick` that `k` calls at `ys: i32[]` is never
minted during the run, and mono builds `pick` at the open parameter. The reader that replaces the
walk here must demand the lambda's body instance at the delivered parameters before it answers.
The witness is [D4661](D4661.md)'s, which the switch moved from runs to emit reject.
