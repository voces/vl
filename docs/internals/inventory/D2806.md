#### Mechanism

D2802's fold lives in the instance rewrite (`monoPinBinOps`), which knows the pin of a
parameter by name only (`monoPinnedOperandTy`). A local initialised from the parameter, and a
lambda body that captures it, are not parameters, so the compare keeps its `T`-typed operand and
reaches the niche dispatch as before. At a record pin the sentence is `struct equality is not
supported yet`. The close is the pin of the operand read, not of the parameter name.
