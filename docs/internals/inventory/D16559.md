#### Mechanism

Every `if`, `match` or block `checkNode` checks calls `joinLeavesAt` (typecheck.vl) at its own type,
and `recordLeafCtxTy` (check_fit_rules.vl) walks the whole tail spine under it (`valueTailSplit`),
stamping each spine node and leaf through `ctxTyStampOne` (`atomSrcStamp`, `numPairStamp`). A
nested chain's inner joins lie on the outer's spine, so level i restamps the D − i nodes under it:
D²/2 stamps, 79% of samples at D = 800.

Skipping a subtree already stamped at the same type is not exact: between the inner stamp and the
outer, `joinAdoptLits` < `numLitAdoptJoin` at the outer level can change the leaves' `nodeTyIxOf`,
which `atomSrcStamp` and `numPairStamp` read, and `boxExitsStamp` / `elabCapLive` differ by mode.

#### Options

(a) An inner join that is the tail of a join being checked defers its `recordLeafCtxTy` to the
outermost, which restamps the same nodes last; it needs a depth flag (new ambient check-phase
state, raising the phase-state census) and a proof that no `nodeCtxTyIx` / `elabCapCtx` reader
runs between. (b) Park, chosen: the cost is generated-depth only.
