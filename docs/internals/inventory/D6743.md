#### Mechanism

From the ablation only. `h` is called at `string` and is given `id` at its `i32` instance, the
one `app3`'s module-scope call takes; the call `app(id, id)` sits in `use`, whose hole parameter
makes its body generic, and without `app3` the by-name plan of [D6705](D6705.md) runs it.
