#### Mechanism

The narrowed argument's pin named one of two same-shaped records for both, so whichever
instance was minted first answered the other branch. The owner ruled (D3870) that records are
structural: `A` and `B` are one type, both `is` tests hold, and a `self`-function on each is a
duplicate binding at the merge (#3464). The witness is that refusal now. With records of two
shapes the narrowed pin is right without a change (`narrowed-record-pin`).
