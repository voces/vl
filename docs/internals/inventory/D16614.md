#### Mechanism

The write scan judged the spread argument `s` as a list delivered into `v`, so a widening write
through `v` was charged to `s`. A spread hands over the list's elements, not the list: `v` is a
new list, and only its records are shared. The records are built where `v`'s are (D16440), so no
write through `v` reaches a record of `s` at the wrong storage; the annotated spelling, where `v`'s
type is not inferred from the spread, already ran. D16449's build adopts `s` at `{x: f64}[]`
through the adoption pass, after which the write scan sees `s` at the pushed type.
