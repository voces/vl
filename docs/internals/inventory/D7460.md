#### Mechanism

`lexText` interns every lexeme of one to four bytes in an open-addressed table of 256, 1024 or
4096 slots, filled to at most half. The key is the lexeme's bytes packed into an `i32`, and the slot
was `(key ^ (key >>> 7) ^ (key >>> 17)) & mask`, probed linearly. Names that differ only in their
last byte, such as `a0`..`a9` or `ix`, `iy`, `iz`, land on nearby slots, so their occupied runs merge.
Per call, the probe visited 2.42 slots on diderven `src/game.vl` and 2.83 on `src/view.vl`, with a
longest chain of 131 on both. The murmur3 finaliser spreads every key bit over the slot bits. The
same counts now read 1.21 and 1.24 slots per call, with a longest chain of 26.

The finaliser costs more instructions per call than the probe steps it saves, so guest fuel does not
move beyond noise. The gain is the bound on the longest chain, for a source whose names cluster
harder than diderven's.

The same sweep read every other hand-rolled table in `compiler/*.vl` under an instrumented build:

- `pxSlotOf` (compiler/emit_atom_kinds.vl) now takes the same mix. It made 0 calls on diderven and on
  the self-compile.
- `hcIntern` (compiler/emit_rep.vl) chains its buckets, with a longest chain of 5–10. Mixing its hash
  lengthened the self-compile's chains, so it is unchanged.
- `rasSlotOf` (compiler/emit_shape_rows.vl) is a direct-mapped cache that evicts 7–10% of the time.
  Mixing moved that by under one point, so it is unchanged.
- The runtime `Map`'s probes do not cluster. String keys use FNV-1a masked to the index, and `i32`
  keys use Fibonacci hashing. A microbenchmark of 20,000 `i32` keys (sequential, strides 16, 4096 and
  65536, and pseudo-random; 250 rounds, each with one hit and one miss per key) runs in 53–59 ms for
  every pattern.
