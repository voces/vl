#### Mechanism

Two halves. Bound, `r` was classified at the `if`'s arms (an `i32` cell) rather than at the
pinned join `i32 | null`, and no row for that box existed in the instance, since only an
annotation or a non-generic inference registers one; the bare `null` arm then had no rep. At a
`boolean` pin the join is the sentinel niche, which `exprNulBool` did not read through the pin.
Unbound, the written `(if …) ?? d` is not lifted into a local, as this row first said: D10130's
`coalNullArmIf` rewrites it to `if c { x ?? d } else { d }`, and the inner `x ?? d` refused
because its default `d: V` was read at its generic type, not at the pin, and because a body
shared by two pins answered no pin through `coalesceOperandPinTy`.
