**loads then traps · `cast failure` · clause 1 · open · the same join over annotated `P | null` parameters types `P | i32 | null` and refuses `q + 1` · filed 2026-10-30 against master `ba2bf3d3`**

Repro:

#### Mechanism

In the template, `a ?? 5` types as an operator's pending answer (a derived hole, resolved at each pin), and `joinTys(hole, T)` returns the hole: `T` is assignable to it inside the body. At the pin `T = P | null` the hole resolves to `P | i32`, so the call is typed `P | i32` and `null` is gone from the type. The instance never re-joins the arms. Built, `q` holds `null` and the else arm adds to it. Measured 2026-10-30: a join rule taking a derived hole beside a declared parameter of the open body as their union, re-joined at each pin (`tyParamBesideDerived` in `joinTys`), types the call `P | i32 | null` and refuses `q + 1`. - 25 run as before;
