#### Mechanism

D4615's close folds a self-referential arm's unrolled spelling onto its rolled one when their
arena types agree once a lone literal compares as its base. Here the unrolled spelling softens
the tag `"a" | "b"` to `string` while the rolled one keeps the literal union, which the canon
key also keeps apart from `string`. The fold misses and the arm keeps two heap types. Master
refused the declaration itself, with no struct-field rep for `Node|null`.
