### D3516 — `??` over a nullable mixed-width literal union with a narrow default is refused at check as the join `9000000000 | 1 | i32`, which an `i64` destination rejects

**closed 2026-10-03 — the witness prints `5` · was a loud check reject, clause 2 · found by the #3340 review · filed 2026-10-03 against master `878f6ebe8`, closed by lane LC against master `f6e17953b` with D3523, the same mechanism · fixture `tests/cases/unions/litunion-mixed-width-into-wider-number.vl`**

Repro:

    type E = 9000000000 | 1 | null
    function g(x: E): i64 { x ?? 5 }
    print(g(null))
    // vl check: "expected i64, got 9000000000 | 1 | i32"

Want:

    5

#### Mechanism

Confirmed as D3523. The `??` result joins the non-null members of `E` with the default's type,
`i32`, and that join (two literals and a plain `i32`) is what `numUnionConverts` could not
read. `litNumUnionConverts` admits it into `i64`, `f64` and `f32` when every member converts
exactly, and the emitter converts the join's box at the member it holds. The literal default
is not retyped: `i32` widens into `i64` exactly, so the join needs no adoption to deliver.
