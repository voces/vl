#### Mechanism

`captureBoxRewrite` boxes a captured-written binding by rewriting its `let` into a length-1
list (D2285). A parameter has no `let` to rewrite, so `cboxScan` never lists it among the
maker's bindings, the parameter stays captured by value, and the lambda's write finds no
local. The likely lowering is a synthesized `let p = [p']` at the top of the body over a
renamed parameter; that is a prediction, not a finding.
