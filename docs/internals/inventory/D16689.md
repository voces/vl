#### Mechanism

A list test is settled from its pins (`settleListIsPins`, check_operators.vl) for an operand that is
a bare hole or type parameter, or a parameter, field or annotated `let` of a list of one. Every pin
fixed and disjoint makes it `never` with dead, unlifted arms; fixed pins that also meet it answer
per instance (`listIsFixedOnly`, read by the emitter through `elabIsTyIn`). Two spellings keep the
emptiness answer (`emitListEmptyFitIs`, D4910): a union operand (`V[] | null`, `V[] | string`),
whose pins are not bare lists, and a test whose function also receives an empty literal or a
defaulted list (`listTestOwnerHasOpenCall`), whose instances the checker cannot tell apart from a
fixed one's until inferred interfaces record each call's binding.
