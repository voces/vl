#### Mechanism

The typed `f64[]` is not a lost arm. It is the type the checker gives the same call made
directly: `const u: f64[] = w(2.5, false)` checks, and `w(2.5, false)[0] / 2` prints `0.5`,
because the `[1, 2, 3]` literal adapts to the joined element. The spelling `f64[]|i32[]` is the
instance's REP union, which is wider than its type. So `substTyDeep` is right about the type, and
C1 step 1 must not take the let's rep from it. The rep comes from the apart-join knowledge
(`tpIfJoinPinTy`, `monoApartJoinAnn`). `type-param-list-join-concrete-list-apart.vl` is the
same: the typed `(i32|null)[]` is the type, and the spelling is the rep.
