Repro:

    const outer: {[string]: {[string]: i32}} = Map()

#### Mechanism

The `.values()` face of [D2515](D2515.md): one ladder (`mapValElemKind`) serves both walks, and
its kind-6 arm had no map-element rung.

| the keys `string` replaced by `i64` (both levels) | still refuses | no |
