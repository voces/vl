#### Mechanism

Narrowing the box `K | null | i32` leaves `K | null` with `K` re-minted at a fresh arena index,
a copy of the alias rather than the alias. `anonFieldCode`'s niche rung
(`anonNulFieldCodeOfTy`) asks `tyIsLitUnionAlias` of the inner type, which compares by
index, so the field coded nothing, the literal minted no anonymous row, and the object literal
fell through to the union-variant boxer. `nulLitAliasNameOfTy` (`emit_classify.vl`) names the
alias by the member texts; `anonFieldCode` codes such a value `FC_NUL_LIT_UNION` and
`anonFieldElemName` records the alias as the companion. The field store then converts the
box through [D3118](D3118.md)'s niche read.
