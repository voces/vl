#### Mechanism

`captureValKind` (`emit_classify.vl`) types `__c`'s env field `i32`, which a probe on the
refusal read back, while the block's own cell is the nullable string. It classifies the
initializer `t?.tag` from the capturing frame, where the block-local receiver `t` has no
slot. So the record-union chain resolves no leaf field, and the ladder falls to its `i32`
default. D5332's declaration rung answers only the union question, not the chain's leaf.
