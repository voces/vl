#### Mechanism

A `??` whose operand cannot be re-read (a call) and is a nullable value-union box lowers to an
`if` whose result is the box (D960's arm in `emitCoalesceGo`, and `emitCoalesceBoxRuntime`
beside it). Its default arm went through `emitUnionCoerce`, which passes a record-typed
expression through raw when the union has no variant struct for it: the record rides the box's
payload under its arm's tag, so the raw `(ref $Rec)` landed where `(ref $box)` was owed. The
union `if` arm already delivers through `emitUnionBoxArg`, which boxes such a value and defers
every other shape to `emitUnionCoerce`; both `??` arms now do the same.
