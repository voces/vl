#### Mechanism

Not localised. `clrB(z)` runs before `z.f != null` is tested, so the narrowing the test makes
holds in the then-arm. The call is charged against it anyway: the condition's calls are
counted as though they followed the narrowing (D2390's rule applied out of order), and the
program, which prints `none`, is refused. Localised at the close: `collectThenNarrows` filtered
the whole `&&` against its first writing call, after each side had already been filtered against
its own calls, so the right side's fact was dropped for a call that ran before its test.
