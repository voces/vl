### D4194 — a type alias of a nullable-element list is refused `.length` and indexing

**loud check reject · clause 2 · `field 'length' is not on every member of L` · filed 2026-10-12 against master `040ff8a3d` · found by the review of [D1832](D1832.md)'s close**

Repro:

    type L = (i32 | null)[]
    const xs: L = [1, null]
    print(xs.length)
    // vl check: rc 1, "field 'length' is not on every member of L — narrow with `is` first".
    // Should print 2.

#### Mechanism

Not yet ablated to a rung. The alias reads as a union of its element's members rather than as a
list: `xs[0]` on an `L` is refused `cannot index non-array L` the same way. Aliasing the
element instead (`type E = i32 | null`, then `E[]`) runs.
