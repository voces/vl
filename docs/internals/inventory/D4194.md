#### Mechanism

Not yet ablated to a rung. The alias reads as a union of its element's members rather than as a
list: `xs[0]` on an `L` is refused `cannot index non-array L` the same way. Aliasing the
element instead (`type E = i32 | null`, then `E[]`) runs.
