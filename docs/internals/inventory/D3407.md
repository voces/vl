### D3407 — a `+` chain in a generic body costs the checker time exponential in its length

**closed · runs, and the chain's pin no longer doubles per term: the witness builds in 4.2 M fuel at 24 terms (was 9.4e10) · was a compile-time cost exponential in the length, a compiler trap (`allocation size too large`) at 24 terms before the copying collector · found by the #3316 review · filed 2026-09-30 against master `4ff12b994`**

Repro (runs, prints `200`):

    function g<T>(a: T): T {
      a@@repeat(199, " + a")@@
    }
    print(g(1))
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: prints 200 (24 terms took 9.4e10 fuel before the close)

#### Mechanism

Each `+` over a hole operand types as a deferred binary-op hole (`HD_BINOP`, `mkBinOpRetTy`)
whose BASE is the left operand's hole and whose INNER answer is that same left hole again. At a
pin, `substHoleTyReal` (`compiler/typecheck.vl`) resolved both — `substHoleTy(hdBase)` and then
`substTyDeep(hdInner)` — so one substitution of an n-term chain visited 2^n paths, and every
visit banked a pin through `notePinnedHole`. A `--names` guest profile at 16 terms put 95% of
the samples under `substTyDeep`/`substHoleTy`, reached from `validateBinCstrs` (one constraint
per link) and from the mono pin's `monoPinnedTyOf` (one per rebuilt node).

Closed in two parts. The `HD_BINOP` arm returns the base's answer when the inner answer is
the base, which made one substitution linear; the ladder was then cubic (1.3e8, 9.4e8, 7.2e9
fuel at 96, 192, 384 terms), because each of the n links re-substituted the whole chain below
it and banked each derived hole under a name as long as the chain. `substHoleTy` now keeps its
answers (`shMemo`) while the bindings, the arena's size and in-place epoch, the parked
argument demands, the substitution mode and `inferQuiet` stay the same, outside a composite's
cycle guard and while no join clash waits; a repeated answer would only re-bank a pin
`notePinnedHole` already holds.

The residue is about n^1.7: `noteBinCstr` scans the body's constraints per link, and a derived
hole's name grows with the chain. The non-generic chain (`a: i32`) reads 25 M fuel at 384 terms.
`tests/vl_scaling_shape_test.ts`'s `generic-body operator chain length` pair reads 0.71 after
and 1,018 on master `313eb4681`.

The lambda variant (`const h = (b: T) => b + … + b` in a generic body) reads 6.2 M and 8.4 M
fuel at 12 and 16 terms (was 31 M and 402 M). The un-annotated recursive variant, the review's
`recgrad` family, is no longer exponential (30 M and 33 M at 15 and 16 terms, was 1.1 G and
2.2 G) but is still cubic, 0.79 G and 5.5 G at 64 and 128 terms, from the emitter's
`binListRep` (compiler/emit_classify.vl) asking `pinnedHoleTyOf` per operand.
