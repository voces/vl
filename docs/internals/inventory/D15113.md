#### Mechanism

The elaboration held both signatures: one key of `inc0` at `i32` and one at `f64`, and
`elabStampAt` gave each literal's field value its own closed function type. The two literals were
adopted at two rows, not one. D15206's `monoPinLitFieldCall` ignored the stamp and derived the
signature from the row (`nodeRepTyIxOf`), then required `fnHasUnannotatedParam` of the template's
slot. The first literal's pin instanced `inc0` in that slot, so the second literal saw no hole and
was skipped, and both call sites keyed the first signature. The pin now hands the called field to
`monoCoerceLitPart`, the reader of the stamp the bound and literal paths already use, and the row
read and the slot gate are gone. The six same-row cells whose string result is printed directly
also need D15114's read of the call's closed type in `exprString`.
