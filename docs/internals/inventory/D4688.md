    print(w.f.length)

#### Mechanism

The field `f` is laid out as a list of the struct `H` (`array (mut (ref null $H))`) rather
than of the union `H | string`, and the second element is pushed into it unboxed. The
record element's structural match against the declared `H` is the ingredient: a type with
another field name leaves the list a union list and the program runs.
