### D6732 — a list whose element is a union of `i32[]` and a literal-union list is minted at two distinct `i32[][]` types

**open · check-clean invalid wasm · clause 1 · 4 cells of `union-of-one-list-members.matrix.vl` and `union-of-one-list-members-alias.matrix.vl` (`array_push`, both faces), red on master before and after the D6690 close · found grading D6690 · filed 2026-10-18 against master `89cdf67b1`**

Repro:

    type K = 0 | 1
    const xs: (i32[] | K[])[] = []
    print(xs.length)
    // vl check: rc 0.  vl run: invalid module, expected (ref null $7), found (ref $9); $7 is `i32[][]`

Want:

    0

#### Mechanism

`K[]` shares `i32[]`'s rep, so the union's two arms collapse to one list rep, but the
outer list is emitted under two distinct `i32[][]` struct types: the empty literal is built
at one and the binding is declared at the other. A union whose arms keep distinct reps does
not reach it.
