#### Mechanism

The multi-value step returns a record as fields only to a call site that reads it field by
field. A site that stores the result keeps the struct, and the producer's twin for that site
(`qnorm.mv` in sunpa: fields in, a struct out) allocates. The store could take the record
apart if the inline-record step held the field (`Joint.rot`) inline. That step refuses because
`last = j.rot` reads the field whole, and a whole read of an inline field re-boxes, where
today it shares the box. In sunpa the stores are `quatOf`'s five keeping call sites
(`twoBone`, `fabrik`, `aimChain`: type 32 field 4, "5 of its 7 read(s) take the whole
record (first in parentRotation)"). `unit`'s keeping sites hand its result to `cylAlong`, which
keeps it in a `Cyl` (type 67, "2 of its 4 read(s) take the whole record (first in holeOf)"),
and to locals that another use keeps (`legsLayer`, `stepSplit`).

sunpa `frame-alloc` under `feet-test` (10,938 frames): `qnorm.mv` 42,090 `struct.new` (3.8 a
frame), `unit.mv_2196` 119,344 (10.9 a frame), the same on master and this lane.
