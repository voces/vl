#### Mechanism

`letIsNulMap` asked the annotation for a nullable MAP and, finding none, asked the
initializer, and an `if` with a `null` arm over a map arm answers "nullable map"
(`nulArmIfThenValueIx`). So the binding took the nullable map's rep while `emitUnionCoerce`
built the union box into it. An annotation whose rep is a union now declines the initializer's
answer. Two maps of one representation (`IM | BM | null`, the review's z1/z4) reached the same
lowering once D2493 let them share a union.
