#### Mechanism

A function-typed destination other than a parameter (a binding, a return, a map value) pins the maker's returned hole lambda through `monoCloneLambdaSubst`,
which clones a capturing lambda as a template (D943) and leaves its environment to
`captureValKind`; that reads the captured `n` through a frame chain the clone does not reach and
answers the default `i32`, so `adder` builds an `f64` into an `i32` field. The fix builds the
lambda as an instance of the maker's twin at the binding's signature, whose frame is the twin.
