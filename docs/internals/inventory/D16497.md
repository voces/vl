#### Mechanism

The `f32` adoption predicates read a bare literal (`isF32AdoptingLitExpr`) or a join of bare
literals (`f32LitTailsAdopt`); a join-free tree is one constant the fold types. A tree over a
join, or a negated join, was neither, so it stayed `f64` beside an `f32`, and `min`/`max`'s
width (`argForcesF64`) read even a bare join as a genuine `f64`.
