#### Mechanism

#6094 made `variantStructHeapTwinAt` (emit_shape_rows.vl) ask `recordRowClassOf` for each union
arm's record type and each struct row's. The class is memoized per type index. Each miss runs
`rrArmKey`, which walked every union `rrUnionsOf` collects and asked `rrHas`, a structural `tySame`
against each member. The cost was O(record types asked × record unions × members).

A guest profile on a `--names` seed shows the jump. At #287 (`5c029b0b2`), `rrArmKey` is 8.27%
inclusive, `rrHas` 3.36%, `rrSameNames` 1.31% and `tySame`/`tySameAt` 4.3%. None of them is in the
top 40 at #285 (`1593aa195`). Samples rose 10%, as fuel did.

Most of that walk was over copies. The arena records a union type once per construction, so a large
union the compiler reaches many times appears in `rrUnionsOf` many times. Every copy holds a member
with the asked record's field names, so the walk visited each one.

The fix keeps the walk and its only producer, and starts it from fewer unions, in the same arena
order:

* `tySame` on two records needs the same field-name set, so each record union is filed under its
  members' sorted field names. A union with no member of the record's names was already skipped
  by `rrHas`.
* A union `tySame` to one filed earlier is not filed again. It would record the same field
  overrides as the earlier copy, and recording an override twice is recording it once.

Fixed-source fuel at master `17ccaa12e` was 78.89 G. With the field-name filter alone it was
71.72 G. With one copy per union as well it is 63.65 G, and `rrArmKey` leaves the profile.
