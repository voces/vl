#### Mechanism

Not ablated to a function. Each level is a distinct named record, so no part is shared; the
cost grows with the chain depth through the union's registration and its spelling at the pin.
The same chain through `id<T>(x: T): T` compiles in under a second (`lin240_pin`), so the
`T | string` union over the deep record is the ingredient.

#### Ablation

| ingredient removed | outcome | needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the union (`id<T>(x: T): T`) | fast | **yes** |
| depth (60 levels) | fast | **yes** |
