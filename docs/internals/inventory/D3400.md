#### Mechanism

The body is checked again at the call's argument types, and a member read on a nullable is
refused there, as a nullable record field is (`member access '.x' on non-object Rc | null (the
body of use at the call's argument types)`). But `r.m[9]` is an element derivation (`HD_ELEMV`)
and `substHoleTyReal` resolves one for a list or a string pin, never a map: at a map pin it
stays a hole, and the member demand on it passes. Lane MH resolved the derivation for the
EMITTER (`holePinReadOf`, `nodeTyPinIxOf`) and left the checker, since the #3282 candidate that
changed `substHoleTyReal` lost cells in four review rounds. The owed outcome is the direct
spelling's check reject.
