#### Mechanism

Whether `noop()` ends main's narrowing of `g` is a whole-program question (`crInfoOf`,
check_narrow_paths.vl): which functions write `g`, and whether any of them escapes. The skip
re-checked only lib, and nothing banked compared the answer main's banked check had read: lib's
interface did not change, since `zz` is unexported and its body is cut from the hash.
