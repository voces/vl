#### Mechanism

`xs` stores its members at `dbl`'s `(f64) => f64`; `one` stores `g` alone at another closure
signature. `walk` has one instance for both arguments, so its read of `ys[0]` and the call through
it take one storage's rep, and the other's closure is called at a type it was not built at. The
close keys the hole parameter's instance by its argument's storage rep, as the monomorphizer keys
an instance by type (coordinator ruling 2026-11-28): no converting copy at the call, which would
break aliasing, and no unifying of the two storages, each decided at its declaration.
