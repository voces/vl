#### Mechanism

`holeLetJudge` judges the binding's members at the call's types. Where their results differ
and every member is capture-free, it hands its refusal to the tag decision, which dispatches.
With a capturing member it refuses at the write instead; which of its deferral conditions fails
is not yet ablated.
