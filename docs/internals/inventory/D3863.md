Repro:

Want:

    vl check: Duplicate binding "d"

#### Mechanism

The wrong record was picked because `A` and `B` have one shape and the instance was keyed by
it, so whichever was minted first answered both. The owner ruled (D3870) that records are
structural: `A` and `B` are one type, and a `self`-function on each is a duplicate binding at
the merge (#3464, D3871). The witness is that refusal now, and with records of two shapes the
same reads pick their own `d` without any change here (`two-record-pin-through-lists`).
