#### Mechanism

D9420's rung answers a nullable record parameter, not a list of one, so `sigParamTokOfTy`
renders `(A | null)[]` with `tyToEmitName`, `7·2^depth` characters, and `annParamKindTy`
classifies that spelling. A depth-16 `--names` profile puts the cost under `annParamKindTy`,
in `scalarListRepOfName` → `arrElemIs` / `nameIsI32Array`, `refArrElemName` and
`nameIsRefArray` → `refArrShapeKind`, each scanning the render with `tyTopIndexOf`, and the
list literal's walk (`collectAArrayLit` → `arrLitElemKind`, `arrLitUnionElemName`,
`arrLitIsRef`) spells it again. It is the same walk as the list of the plain record
([D9460](D9460.md)), with a nullable element.

| face, master `83f96a8ba` | depth 8 | depth 12 | depth 16 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `g(u: (A \| null)[])` with `g([a])` (this witness) | 72.2M | 519.2M | 5.97e9 |
| the same, through `const xs: (A \| null)[] = [a]` | 73.4M | 525.7M | — |

Fuel is `VL_FUEL=1` guest fuel of one `vl build`, which is deterministic.

ratio stops being a power of two per level. Grade with [D9460](D9460.md), whose list of the
