#### Mechanism

The dropped-field plan (`recDropFits`, `check_rec_drop.vl`) asks `fieldStorageEq` whether each
field pair is stored alike. `fieldStorageEq` takes a pair that reaches an inference hole as stored
alike, asked as `tyReachesHole(a, 8)`, and that answers "reaches a hole" when its budget runs out.
A record eight levels deep spends the budget, so the differing field read as stored alike, the
plan found no drop to land, and the delivery was refused. At seven levels and fewer it runs.
Deepening `tyReachesHole` alone runs every depth from 8 to 12, and the call-site bisection names
the guard in `fieldStorageEq`. `fieldStorageEq`'s own budget and the `rec_drop` walks' budgets do
not move it.
