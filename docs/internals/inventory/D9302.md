#### Mechanism

`monoPinCallRecord` (emit_mono.vl) retypes a call to a function returning a closed record
only when a `const` or `let` binds the call, and then the reads through that binding. A call
read in place (`mk().m["k"]`) is matched by no pass, so the member and index reads on it keep
the checker's open types. The [D9252](D9252.md) walk (`monoRetypeRecMemberReads`) is the
natural owner once the call node itself takes the record.
