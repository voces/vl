**closed 2026-10-18 by lane L1 · runs, prints `x 3` · was loads then traps · clause 1 · found closing [D6470](D6470.md) · filed 2026-10-18 against master `d9c8431e5` · a hole parameter copied, through a write or a further copy, into a binding the body calls with a closed argument unrelated to the parameter's pin is split one parameter per call type, as D6470's copy at the declaration is; the same seeded binding over a hole LAMBDA (`const id = (u) => u`) was check-clean invalid wasm and is now refused loudly by the split's two-pin refusal · matrices unchanged · pins: `tests/cases/functions/generic-seeded-binding-written-with-param-called-at-another-type.vl`**

Repro:

    function same<T>(k: T): T { k }

Want:

#### Mechanism

From the ablation only. `b` is seeded with `same` and then holds `a`, which is called at
`string`; the call `b(3)` goes through a signature built for one of the two values, and the
other is what arrives. D6470's copy split does not reach it, because `b` is not a copy of `a`
at its declaration.
