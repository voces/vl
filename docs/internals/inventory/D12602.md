#### Mechanism

In the body `z.h` is a hole, so `pushDiscrimParentFact` had no member list to filter and pushed
no fact for `z.h.k == "a"`; the arm's read `z.h.v` was re-asked at the pin, where `z.h` is `A | B`
and nothing narrowed it. The test now pushes a derived hole on the parent, `HD_DISC` (the field, the
tested type and whether the arm is the one the test fails), and the pin answers it with the same
member filter the concrete test uses (`discrimKeepTy`), in the checker's `substHoleTyReal` and in
emit's `holePinReadAt`.
