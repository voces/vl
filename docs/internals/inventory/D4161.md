#### Mechanism

Not located. The literal's element context is the single-literal nullable `"a" | null`, whose
inner is a `TyLit` rather than a literal union, and the bare `null` element is lowered without
the niche the two-member union gets. A literal holding a non-null member beside the `null`
(`["a", null]`) and a `push(null)` both run.
