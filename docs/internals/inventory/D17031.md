**closed 2026-11-20 by lane IR6 · now runs, `false` then `1` · was check-clean silently wrong, clause 1 · the `nicheNever` arm evaluates its receiver for its effects and drops it unless it is a bare name, the one rule (`deadOperandEvaluated`) the dead null test (`emitDeadNullTest`) and the IR's `deadNulTest` and niche `is` also ask · pins: `tests/cases/narrowing/is-niche-never-evaluates-its-receiver.vl` (a value, an `if`, a `while` test, an `||`, a function, an assignment) · found writing IR-REV's effectful-receiver witness for the IR's `nicheNever` arm · present on master `a42095870` · filed 2026-11-20 against master `a42095870`**

#### Mechanism

`mk() is C` over the niche `A | null` takes `litIsArmOf`'s `nicheNever` arm, which
`emitIsTestLitArms` writes as `i32.const 0` alone: the test cannot hold, and the receiver is never
evaluated, so its call and the write it makes are gone. The null test's own dead form
(`emitDeadNullTest`) evaluates its operand for its effects and drops it.
