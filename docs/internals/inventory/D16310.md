### D16310 — an un-annotated lambda delivered to a closed function type builds its result at its body's own type, not the delivered result's rep

**closed 2026-11-14 — the witness runs and prints `1` · was: check-clean invalid wasm · clause 1 · bucket (f) · 9 of the 16 unsound fuzz findings of 2026-11-14 (fuzz-sweep, 40,000 programs over the four legs), three faces: invalid module, `indirect call type mismatch` trap, and a silently false `is` · filed 2026-11-14 against master `fe9585a72`**

Repro:

    const f: () => {z: f64[] | i32} = () => {z: [14.5]}
    print(1)

Want:

    1

#### Mechanism

The lambda carries no result annotation, so its tail is built at the type its body infers
(`{z: f64[]}`, a record whose field is a bare list), while the binding, the call sites and every
reader use the delivered function type's result (`{z: f64[] | i32}`, a union-box field). The two
reps meet in the lambda's own `return`, so the module fails to validate inside `f`. The same
seam is a trap when the reps are two scalar widths (`f: () => i64` given `() => 6`, called through
`$fnsig` `() => i64` while the closure was built at `() => i32`), and a silently false `is` when the
field is itself a union of function types (the closure is boxed under its own signature's tag).
Annotating the lambda's result with the delivered one makes every face run, so the fix is the
delivered result standing in for a missing annotation.

**Closed.** A lambda delivered to a closed function type, with no result annotation, takes the delivered result as that annotation when a value its body hands back is not of that type. The walk queues it (`lamRetQueue`, check_fn_decl.vl); the `lamRet` pin phase writes the result as its return-annotation node and re-checks, so the body is checked and built as its annotated twin (`lamRetApply`). A union arm guessed by arity, a generic parameter's type, and a literal result are not held to (`cbExpectedRetHeld`, `lamCtxRetClosed`). The record arm of a union is seeded whatever its fields' kinds once they are resolved (`unionArmSeedSafe`, tyquery.vl), so faces two and three run; a list of lists of closures is seeded level by level (`unionNestedArmSeeds`). The nine fuzz findings of family A pass, re-run at their seeds.
