#### Mechanism

`litTreeWideTy` keeps a negation chain (`k == 0`) at `i32` when its lexeme fits `i32`. A chain
over a join has no lexeme, so it took that exit and kept `i32`; the `f32` adoption predicates
read a bare literal or a bare join, never a negated one.
