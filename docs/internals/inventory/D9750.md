#### Mechanism

`v` is never narrowed, but its un-annotated type is read by chasing its initializer `w`, and
`unionNameOfExpr`'s Ident arm answers that chase from the name-keyed narrowing table
(`narrowedValueAtomOf(e.identName)`, emit_classify.vl) at the read of `v`, where `w` is
narrowed to `i32`. So `print(v)` lowers the i32 arm while `v` is still the union box. The
narrowing is a fact about the read of `w` at a position, not about every read that reaches
the name `w` through an alias.

Traced at the close: the alias's classifiers chase its initializer `w` and ask about that node
(`exprUnion` of the init in `letIsUnion`, `letInitIsBox` and `letInitCopiesBox`;
`unionNameOfExpr` of it in `letUnionNameOf`; and `unionIdentReadKind`'s one-level inheritance,
D1013, which found the `let` by a name scan). Each answered from the live table, so `w`'s
narrowing at the read of `v` made the init, and therefore `v`, read as `i32`. A chased
initializer is now judged at its declaration: it is narrowed only where the checker did not
type it as a value union (`narrowChasedInit`, `narrowedValueAtomAtDecl`, emit_classify.vl),
and the inheritance starts from `identDeclOf`'s binding when the resolver bound one. The gate
is confined to chased initializers on purpose: applied to every read it broke the emit-only
narrowing `??` lowering puts on a read the checker typed as the union
(`generics/coalesce-over-kept-apart-join.vl`, `literal-unions/coalesce-litunion-int-default-arith.vl`).
The function-local, early-return, local-shadow and nullable faces, which refused loud, shared
the mechanism and now run.
